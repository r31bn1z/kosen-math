### (1)
$$y^2 = 4ax$$

$F = 4ax - y^2$ とおくと

$$F_x = 4a \qquad F_y = -2y$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = -\frac{4a}{-2y} = \frac{2a}{y}$$

$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \left(\frac{2a}{y}\right)_x = \left(\frac{2a}{\sqrt{4ax}}\right)_x = \frac{-8a^2}{2\sqrt{(4ax)^3}} = \frac{-4a^2}{y^3}$$

### (2)
$$x^2 + y^2 - a^2 = 0$$

$F = x^2 + y^2 - a^2$ とおくと

$$F_x = 2x \qquad F_y = 2y$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = -\frac{2x}{2y} = -\frac{x}{y}$$

$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \left(-\frac{x}{y}\right)_x = \left(-\frac{x}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}\right)_x = -\frac{\sqrt{a^2-x^2} - x \cdot \dfrac{-x}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}}{(\sqrt{a^2-x^2})^2} = -\frac{a^2-x^2+x^2}{(\sqrt{a^2-x^2})^3} = -\frac{a^2}{y^3}$$

### (3)
$$x^2 - 4xy + 5y^2 = 2$$

$F = x^2 - 4xy + 5y^2 - 2$ とおくと

$$F_x = 2x-4y \qquad F_y = -4x+10y \qquad F_{xx} = 2 \qquad F_{xy} = -4 \qquad F_{yy} = 10$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{-(2x-4y)}{-4x+10y} = \frac{x-2y}{2x-5y}$$

$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = -\frac{F_{xx}F_y^{\,2} - 2F_{xy}F_xF_y + F_{yy}F_x^{\,2}}{F_y^{\,3}} = \frac{2(-4x+10y)^2 + 8(2x-4y)(-4x+10y) + 10(2x-4y)^2}{(4x-10y)^3}$$

$$= \frac{2}{(2x-5y)^3}$$

### (4)

$$\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}=\sqrt{a}$$

$$F=\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}-\sqrt{a}$$

$$F_x=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} \qquad F_y=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{y}} \qquad F_{xx}=-\frac{1}{4\sqrt{x^3}} \qquad F_{xy}=0 \qquad F_{yy}=-\frac{1}{4\sqrt{y^3}}$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{\sqrt{y}}{\sqrt{x}}$$

$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\left(-\frac{\sqrt{y}}{\sqrt{x}}\right)_x=\left(\frac{\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{x}}\right)_x=\frac{\sqrt{a}}{2\sqrt{x^3}}$$
