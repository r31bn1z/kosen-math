### (1)
$g = x^2 - xy + y^3 - 1$ とおくと

$$g_x = 2x - y \qquad g_y = -x + 3y^2$$

接線: $f_{x(a,b)}(x-a) + f_{y(a,b)}(y-b) = 0$

$P(0,1)$ から $\quad -x + 3(y-1) = 0 \qquad \therefore y = \dfrac{1}{3}x + 1$

### (2)
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{2x-y}{x-3y^2}$$

$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{2x-y}{x-3y^2}\right) = \frac{\left(2-\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)(x-3y^2) - \left(1-6y\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)(2x-y)}{(x-3y^2)^2}$$

$$= \frac{\left(2-\dfrac{2x-y}{x-3y^2}\right)(x-3y^2) - \left(1-6y\dfrac{2x-y}{x-3y^2}\right)(2x-y)}{(x-3y^2)^2}$$

$$= \frac{(2x-6y^2-2x+y)(x-3y^2) - (x-3y^2-12xy+6y^2)(2x-y)}{(x-3y^2)^3}$$

$$= \frac{(-6y^2+y)(x-3y^2) - (x-12xy+3y^2)(2x-y)}{(x-3y^2)^3}$$

$$= \frac{-2x^2+24x^2y-24xy^2+2xy+18y^4}{(x-3y^2)^3} = \frac{-2(x^2-xy)+24y(x^2-xy+y^3)-6y^4}{(x-3y^2)^3}$$

与式から

$$= \frac{-2(1-y^3)+24y-6y^4}{(x-3y^2)^3} = \frac{-6y^4+2y^3+24y-2}{(x-3y^2)^3}$$

$P(0,1)$ から

$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = -\frac{-2}{3} < 0 \quad \text{極大}$$

よって上に凸
