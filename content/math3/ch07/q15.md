### (1)
$$xy(y-x)=2$$

$F=xy^2-x^2y-2$ とおくと

$$F_x=y^2-2xy \qquad F_y=2xy-x^2$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{y^2-2xy}{2xy-x^2}$$

### (2)
$$e^y-e^x+xy=0$$

$F=e^y-e^x+xy$ とおくと

$$F_x=-e^x+y \qquad F_y=e^y+x$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{e^x-y}{e^y+x}$$

### (3)
$$x^y=y^x$$

両辺対数を取って

$$y\log x=x\log y$$

$F=x\log y-y\log x$ とおくと

$$F_x=\log y-\frac{y}{x} \qquad F_y=\frac{x}{y}-\log x$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=-\dfrac{\log y-\dfrac{y}{x}}{\dfrac{x}{y}-\log x}$$

### (4)

$$\log\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}} = \tan^{-1}\frac{y}{x}$$

$F = \log\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}} - \tan^{-1}\dfrac{y}{x}$ とおくと

$$F_{x} = \dfrac{\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}} - \dfrac{-y}{x^{2}+y^{2}} \qquad\qquad F_{y} = \dfrac{\dfrac{y}{\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}}}{\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}} - \dfrac{x}{x^{2}+y^{2}}$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = -\dfrac{\dfrac{x+y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}{\dfrac{y-x}{x^{2}+y^{2}}} = \frac{x+y}{x-y}$$
