### (1)

接線の方程式は $y - y_0 = y'(x - x_0)$

点Qのとき $-y_0 = y'(x - x_0)$

点Pは$QR$の中点であるので

$y_0 = \frac{y}{2}$ $x_0 = \frac{x}{2}$ として代入すると

$-\frac{y}{2} = y'(x - \frac{x}{2})$  $xy' + y = 0$

### (2)

$xy' + y = 0$    $y' + \frac{1}{x}y = 0$

$P = \frac{1}{x}$、$Q = 0$ とおくと $\int\frac{1}{x}dx = \log x$    これから

$y = \frac{1}{x}\{\int 0dx + C\} = \frac{1}{x}C$

$(\sqrt{3}, \sqrt{7})$ のとき $C = \sqrt{21}$    これから

$y = \frac{1}{x}\sqrt{21}$    $xy = \sqrt{21}$

### (3)

$y' + \frac{1}{x}y = 0$    $xy = \sqrt{21}$ から    $y' = -\frac{1}{x^2}\sqrt{21}$

$S = 2\pi\int_{\sqrt{2}}^{\sqrt{10}} \frac{1}{x}\sqrt{21}\sqrt{1 + \frac{21}{x^4}}dx = 2\pi\sqrt{21}\int_{\sqrt{2}}^{\sqrt{10}} \frac{1}{x}\sqrt{\frac{x^4 + 21}{x^4}}dx = 2\pi\sqrt{21}\int_{\sqrt{2}}^{\sqrt{10}} \frac{\sqrt{x^4 + 21}}{x^3}dx$

$x^2 = t$ とおくと   $2xdx = dt$    $dx = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{t}}dt$    $x: \sqrt{2} \to \sqrt{10}$    $t: 2 \to 10$    これから

$= 2\pi\sqrt{21}\int_2^{10} \sqrt{\frac{t^2 + 21}{t^3}} \cdot \frac{1}{2\sqrt{t}}dt = \pi\sqrt{21}\int_2^{10} \sqrt{\frac{t^2 + 21}{t^4}}dt = \pi\sqrt{21}\int_2^{10} \frac{\sqrt{t^2 + 21}}{t^2}dt$

$t = \sqrt{21}\tan\theta$ とおくと   $dt = \sqrt{21}\frac{1}{\cos^2\theta}d\theta$    $t: 2 \to 10$    $\theta: \tan^{-1}\frac{2}{\sqrt{21}} \to \tan^{-1}\frac{10}{\sqrt{21}}$    これから

$$= \pi\sqrt{21}\int_{\tan^{-1}\frac{10}{\sqrt{21}}}^{\sqrt{21}} \frac{\sqrt{21}\sec\theta \cdot \sqrt{21}}{2l \cdot \tan^2 \theta \cdot \cos^2 \theta} d\theta = \pi\sqrt{21}\int_{\tan^{-1}\frac{10}{\sqrt{21}}}^{\sqrt{21}} \frac{1}{\cos\theta \cdot \sin^2 \theta} d\theta$$

$$= \pi\sqrt{21}\int_{\tan^{-1}\frac{10}{\sqrt{21}}}^{\sqrt{21}} \left(\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin^2 \theta} + \frac{1}{\cos \theta}\right)d\theta$$

第１項の式を $\sin\theta = k$、第２項の式を $\tan\frac{\theta}{2} = u$とおくと

$$= \pi\sqrt{21}\left(\int \frac{1}{k^2} dk + \int \frac{2}{1-u^2} du\right) = \pi\sqrt{21}\left[\left[-\frac{1}{k}\right] + \left[\log\frac{1+u}{1-u}\right]\right]$$ $k,u$を元に戻して

$$= \pi\sqrt{21}\left[-\frac{1}{\sin \theta} + \log\left|\frac{1+\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}{1-\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}\right|\right]_{\tan^{-1}\frac{10}{\sqrt{21}}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}-\tan^{-1}\frac{2}{\sqrt{21}}} = \pi\sqrt{21}\left[-\frac{1}{\sin \theta} + \log\frac{1+\sin \theta}{\cos \theta}\right]_{\tan^{-1}\frac{10}{\sqrt{21}}}^{\tan^{-1}\frac{2}{\sqrt{21}}}$$

$$= \pi\sqrt{21}\left\{\left(-\frac{11}{10} + \log\frac{1+\frac{10}{11}}{\frac{\sqrt{21}}{11}}\right) - \left(-\frac{5}{2} + \log\frac{1+\frac{2}{5}}{\frac{\sqrt{21}}{5}}\right)\right\} = \sqrt{21}\pi\left(\frac{7}{5} + \log 3\right)$$

下から2行目の $\log\frac{1+\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}{1-\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}$ の変換

$$\frac{1+\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}{1-\tan\frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{1+\frac{\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}}}{1-\frac{\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}}} = \frac{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}} \cdot \frac{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}-\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}$$

$$= \frac{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}-\sin\frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{(\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\sin\frac{\theta}{2})(\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\sin\frac{\theta}{2})}{(\cos\frac{\theta}{2}-\sin\frac{\theta}{2})(\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\sin\frac{\theta}{2})}$$

$$= \frac{1+2\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2}-\sin^2\frac{\theta}{2}}$$

$$\cos(\frac{\theta}{2}+\frac{\theta}{2}) = \cos\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}-\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{\theta}{2} = \cos^2\frac{\theta}{2}-\sin^2\frac{\theta}{2}$$

$$\sin(\frac{\theta}{2}+\frac{\theta}{2}) = \sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{\theta}{2} = 2\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}$$

これから

$$\frac{1+2\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2}-\sin^2\frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{1+\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} \text{となる}$$
