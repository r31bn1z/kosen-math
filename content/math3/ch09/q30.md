$y' = y^2 - 1$ $\frac{dy}{dx} = y^2 - 1$

$$\int \frac{1}{y^2-1} dy = \int dx$$

$$\frac{1}{2}\log\left|\frac{y-1}{y+1}\right| = x + C_1$$

$e^{2C_1} = C$ とおくと

$\frac{y-1}{y+1} = Ce^{2x}$

$1 - \frac{2}{y+1} = Ce^{2x}$

$\frac{2}{y+1} = 1 - Ce^{2x}$

$y = \frac{2}{1-Ce^{2x}} - 1$

$y(0) = -1$ のとき $C = -\infty$ よって $y = -1$

$y(0) = 0$ のとき $C = -1$ よって $y = \frac{1-e^{2x}}{1+e^{2x}}$

$y(0) = 1$ のとき $C = 0$ よって $y = 1$

$y(0) = 2$ のとき $C = \frac{1}{3}$ よって $y = \frac{3+e^{2x}}{3-e^{2x}}$
