$$x^2+y^2=2az \qquad z=x$$

$x=r\sin\theta \quad y=r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0\leq r\leq 2a\cos\theta \quad -\dfrac{\pi}{2}\leq\theta\leq\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

$$\iint_D x-\frac{1}{2a}(x^2+y^2)\,dxdy=\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\int_0^{2a\cos\theta}\left(r\cos\theta-\frac{1}{2a}r^2\right)r\,dr\,d\theta=\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\left[\frac{1}{3}r^3\cos\theta-\frac{1}{8a}r^4\right]_0^{2a\cos\theta}d\theta$$

$$=\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{8}{3}a^3\cos^4\theta-2a^3\cos^4\theta\,d\theta=2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{8}{3}a^3\cos^4\theta-2a^3\cos^4\theta\,d\theta$$

$$=2\left(\frac{8}{3}a^3-2a^3\right)\left(\frac{3}{4}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\frac{\pi}{4}a^3$$
