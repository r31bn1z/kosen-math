$$\iint_D \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}}\,dxdy \qquad D: x^2+y^2 \leq 1$$

$x = r\cos\theta \quad y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \leq r \leq 1 \quad 0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$

$$\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^1 \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-r^2}}\,r\,dr\,d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi}\left[-\sqrt{1-r^2}\right]_0^1 d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta = 2\pi$$
