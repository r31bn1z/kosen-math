### (1)
$$\int_0^1\int_1^2 (x-y)dydx = \int_0^1\left[xy-\frac{1}{2}y^2\right]_1^2 dx = \int_0^1 2x-2-x+\frac{1}{2}dx = \int_0^1 x-\frac{3}{2}dx = \left[\frac{1}{2}x^2-\frac{3}{2}x\right]_0^1 = -1$$

### (2)
$$\int_{-1}^1\int_0^1 xy^2 dxdy = \int_{-1}^1\left[\frac{1}{2}x^2y^2\right]_0^1 dy = \int_{-1}^1 \frac{1}{2}y^2 dy = \left[\frac{1}{6}y^3\right]_{-1}^1 = \frac{1}{3}$$

### (3)
$$\int_0^a\int_0^b xy(x-y)dydx = \int_0^a\left[\frac{1}{2}x^2y^2-\frac{1}{3}xy^3\right]_0^b dx = \int_0^a \frac{1}{2}b^2x^2-\frac{1}{3}b^3xdx = \left[\frac{1}{6}b^2x^3-\frac{1}{6}b^3x^2\right]_0^a$$

$$= \frac{1}{6}(a^3b^2-a^2b^3)$$
