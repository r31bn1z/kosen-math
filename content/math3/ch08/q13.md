$$z = \sqrt{a^2 - x^2 - y^2}$$

$x = r\cos\theta$ $y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \le r \le b$ $0 \le \theta \le \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ として

$$8\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\int_0^b \sqrt{a^2 - r^2} \cdot r\,dr\,d\theta = 8\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left[-\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{(a^2-r^2)^3}\right]_0^b d\theta = 8\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} -\frac{1}{3}\left(\sqrt{(a^2-b^2)^3} - a^3\right) d\theta$$

$$= 8 \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\left(a^3 - \sqrt{(a^2-b^2)^3}\right) = \frac{4}{3}\pi\left\{a^3 - \sqrt{(a^2-b^2)^3}\right\}$$
