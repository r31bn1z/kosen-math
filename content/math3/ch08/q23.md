### (1)
$$x^2+y^2\le1 \qquad -y\le z\le2y$$

$x=r\cos\theta$ $y=r\sin\theta$ とおくと上関係式から $y\ge0$ となり $0\le r\le1$ $0\le\theta\le\pi$

$$\iint_D 3y\,dx\,dy=\int_0^\pi\int_0^1 3r\sin\theta\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta=\int_0^\pi\left[3\cdot\frac13r^3\sin\theta\right]_0^1d\theta=\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta=\left[-\cos\theta\right]_0^\pi=2$$

### (2)
$$x^2+y^2+z^2\le a^2 \qquad x^2+y^2\le ax$$

$x=r\cos\theta$ $y=r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0\le r\le a\cos\theta$ $0\le\theta\le\pi$

$z=\sqrt{a^2-x^2-y^2}$ から

$$2\int_0^\pi\int_0^{a\cos\theta}\sqrt{a^2-r^2}\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta=2\int_0^\pi\left[-\frac13\sqrt{(a^2-r^2)^3}\right]_0^{a\cos\theta}d\theta=2\int_0^\pi-\frac13a^3\sin^3\theta+\frac13a^3\,d\theta$$

$$=-\frac23a^3\int_0^\pi(\sin\theta-\sin\theta\cdot\cos^2\theta)-1\,d\theta$$

第２項において $\cos\theta=t$ とおくと

$$=-\frac23a^3\left\{\left[-\cos\theta\right]_0^\pi-\left[-\frac13t^3\right]_{-1}^1-\left[\theta\right]_0^\pi\right\}=-\frac23a^3\left(2-\frac23-\pi\right)=\frac43\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac23\right)a^3$$

### (3)
$$x^2+y^2\le a^2 \qquad 0\le z\le x+y$$

$x=r\cos\theta$ $y=r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0\le r\le a$ $0\le x+y$ から $0\le\cos\theta+\sin\theta$ $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\le\theta\le\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$

$$\int_{-\frac14\pi}^{\frac34\pi}\int_0^a r^2(\cos\theta+\sin\theta)\,dr\,d\theta=\int_{-\frac14\pi}^{\frac34\pi}\frac13a^3(\cos\theta+\sin\theta)\,d\theta=\left[\frac13a^3(\sin\theta-\cos\theta)\right]_{-\frac14\pi}^{\frac34\pi}$$

$$=\frac13a^3\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt2}+\frac{2}{\sqrt2}\right)=\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}a^3$$

### (4)

$$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \le 2 \qquad z \ge x^2 + y^2$$

$$z^2 = 2 - x^2 - y^2 = 2 - z \qquad z^2 + z - 2 = 0 \qquad (z+2)(z-1) = 0 \qquad z = 1, -2$$

$x^2 + y^2 \ge 0$ から $z = 1$として $x^2 + y^2 \le 1$

$x = r\cos\theta$ $y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \le r \le 1$ $0 \le \theta \le 2\pi$

$$\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^1 (\sqrt{2-r^2} - r^2) r \, dr\, d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi} \left[-\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{(2-r^2)^3} - \frac{1}{4}r^4\right]_0^1 d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi} \left(-\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4} + \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\right) d\theta$$

$$= \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{1}{12}(8\sqrt{2} - 7)\, d\theta = \frac{1}{6}(8\sqrt{2} - 7)\pi$$
