$x = r\cos\theta$ $y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \leq r \leq a$ $0 \leq \theta \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \int_0^a r \tan^{-1} \frac{r\sin\theta}{r\cos\theta} \, dr d\theta = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \int_0^a r \tan^{-1}(\tan\theta) \, dr d\theta = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \int_0^a r\theta \, dr d\theta$$

$$= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left[ \frac{1}{2} r^2 \theta \right]_0^a d\theta = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1}{2} a^2 \theta \, d\theta = \left[ \frac{1}{4} a^2 \theta^2 \right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = \frac{\pi^2}{16} a^2$$
