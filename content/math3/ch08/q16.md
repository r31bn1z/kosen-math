### (1)

$$\int_0^\infty x^2 e^{-x^2}\,dx = \int_0^\infty x \cdot (xe^{-x^2})\,dx = \left[-\frac{1}{2}xe^{-x^2}\right]_0^\infty + \frac{1}{2}\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{4}$$

### (2)

$$\int_0^\infty \frac{e^{-x}}{\sqrt{x}}\,dx$$

$\sqrt{x} = t$ とおくと $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}\,dx = dt$  $dx = 2t\,dt$  $x : 0 \to \infty$  $t : 0 \to \infty$ これから

$$= \int_0^\infty \frac{1}{t}e^{-t^2} \cdot 2t\,dt = 2\int_0^\infty e^{-t^2}\,dt = \sqrt{\pi}$$
