### (1)
$0 \le x \le 1 \quad 0 \le y \le \sqrt{1-x}$

$$\int_0^1\int_0^{1-y^2}(1-x^2)\,dx\,dy=\int_0^1\left[x-\frac{1}{3}x^3\right]_0^{1-y^2}dy=\int_0^1 1-y^2-\frac{1}{3}(1-y^2)^3\,dy$$

$$=\int_0^1 1-y^2-\frac{1}{3}+y^2-y^4+\frac{1}{3}y^6\,dy=\left[\frac{2}{3}y-\frac{1}{5}y^5+\frac{1}{21}y^7\right]_0^1=\frac{18}{35}$$

### (2)
$2y=x \quad x+y=2a \quad$ これから $\quad y=\frac{2}{3}a \quad 0\le y\le\frac{2}{3}a$

$$\int_0^{\frac{2}{3}a}\int_{2y}^{2a-y}\frac{1}{a}xy\,dx\,dy=\int_0^{\frac{2}{3}a}\left[\frac{1}{2a}x^2y\right]_{2y}^{2a-y}dy=\int_0^{\frac{2}{3}a}\frac{1}{2a}\{y(2a-y)^2-4y^3\}dy$$

$$=\int_0^{\frac{2}{3}a}\frac{1}{2a}(4a^2y-4ay^2+y^3-4y^3)dy=\frac{1}{2a}\left[2a^2y^2-\frac{4}{3}ay^3-\frac{3}{4}y^4\right]_0^{\frac{2}{3}a}=\frac{14}{81}a^3$$

### (3)
$1\le x\le3 \quad 1-\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}\le y\le1+\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2} \quad$ これから

$$\int_1^3\int_{1-\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}}^{1+\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}}xy\,dy\,dx=\int_1^3\left[\frac{1}{2}xy^2\right]_{1-\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}}^{1+\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}}dx$$

$$=\int_1^3 \frac{1}{2}x(1+2\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}+1-(x-2)^2)-\frac{1}{2}x(1-2\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}+1-(x-2)^2)\,dx$$

$$=\int_1^3 2x\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}\,dx$$

$$=\left[2x\cdot\frac{1}{2}\{(x-2)\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}+\sin^{-1}(x-2)\}\right]_1^3-\int_1^3(x-2)\sqrt{1-(x-2)^2}+\sin^{-1}(x-2)\,dx$$

$x-2=t$ とおくと $dx=dt \quad x:1\to3 \quad t:-1\to1 \quad$ これから

$$=2\left(3\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}\right)-\int_{-1}^1 t\sqrt{1-t^2}+\sin^{-1}t\,dt$$

$$=2\pi-\left[-\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{(1-t^2)^3}\right]_{-1}^1-[t\sin^{-1}t]_{-1}^1+\int_{-1}^1\frac{t}{\sqrt{1-t^2}}dt=2\pi-\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)-\left[\sqrt{1-t^2}\right]_{-1}^1=2\pi$$

### (4)

$$z = \sqrt{4ax} \qquad y^2 = ax - x^2 \qquad y = \sqrt{ax-x^2} \qquad 0 \le x \le a \qquad 0 \le y \le \sqrt{ax-x^2}$$

$$4\int_0^a \int_0^{\sqrt{ax-x^2}} 2\sqrt{ax}\, dy\, dx = 8\int_0^a \sqrt{ax(ax-x^2)}\, dx = 8\sqrt{a}\int_0^a x\sqrt{a-x}\, dx$$

$$= 8\sqrt{a}\left\{\left[-\frac{2}{3}x\sqrt{(a-x)^3}\right]_0^a + \frac{2}{3}\int_0^a \sqrt{(a-x)^3}\, dx\right\} = 8\sqrt{a}\cdot\frac{2}{3}\left[-\frac{2}{5}\sqrt{(a-x)^5}\right]_0^a = \frac{32}{15}a^3$$
