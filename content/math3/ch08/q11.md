### (1)

$$\iint_D xydxdy \qquad D: x^2+y^2 \leq a^2 \quad x \geq 0 \quad y \geq 0$$

$$\int_0^a \int_0^{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}} xy\,dydx = \int_0^a \left[\frac{1}{2}xy^2\right]_0^{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}} dx = \int_0^a \frac{1}{2}x(a^2-x^2)dx = \left[\frac{1}{4}a^2x^2-\frac{1}{8}x^4\right]_0^a = \frac{a^4}{8}$$

### (2)

$$\iint_D xydxdy \qquad D: x^2+y^2 \leq 2x \quad y \geq 0$$

$x = r\cos\theta$ $y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \leq r \leq 2\cos\theta$ $0 \leq \theta \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \int_0^{2\cos\theta} r^3\cos\theta \cdot \sin\theta \, drd\theta = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left[\frac{1}{4}r^4\cos\theta\cdot\sin\theta\right]_0^{2\cos\theta} d\theta = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 4\cos^5\theta\cdot\sin\theta \, d\theta$$

$\cos\theta = t$ とおくと $-\sin\theta d\theta = dt$ $\theta: 0 \to \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ $t: 1 \to 0$ これから

$$= \int_0^1 4t^5 dt = \left[\frac{2}{3}t^6\right]_0^1 = \frac{2}{3}$$

### (3)

$\iint_D e^{x^2+y^2} dxdy$ $D: x^2+y^2 \leq a^2$ $x = r\cos\theta$ $y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \leq r \leq a$ $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$

$$\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^a e^{r^2} r \, drd\theta = \int_0^{2\pi}\left[\frac{1}{2}e^{r^2}\right]_0^a d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi}\frac{1}{2}(e^{a^2}-1)d\theta = \pi(e^{a^2}-1)$$

### (4)

$$\iint_D xdxdy \qquad D: a^2 \leq x^2+y^2 \leq 2ay \quad x \geq 0 \quad y \geq 0$$

$x = r\cos\theta$ $y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $a \leq r \leq 2a\sin\theta$ $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \leq \theta \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

$$\int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \int_a^{2a\sin\theta} r^2\cos\theta \, drd\theta = \int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left[\frac{1}{3}r^3\cos\theta\right]_a^{2a\sin\theta} d\theta = \int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{8}{3}a^3\sin^3\theta\cdot\cos\theta - \frac{1}{3}a^3\cos\theta \, d\theta$$

$\sin\theta = t$ とおくと $\cos\theta d\theta = dt$ $\theta: \dfrac{\pi}{6}\to\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ $t: \dfrac{1}{2}\to 1$ これから

$$= \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^1 \frac{8}{3}a^3t^3 dt - \int_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1}{3}a^3\cos\theta \, d\theta = \frac{8}{3}a^3\left[\frac{1}{4}t^4\right]_{\frac{1}{2}}^1 - \frac{1}{3}a^3\left[\sin\theta\right]_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = \frac{11}{24}a^3$$
