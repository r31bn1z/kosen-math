### (2)

$$\iint_D (x^2+y^2)\,dydx = 2\int_{-1}^0\int_0^{1+x}(x^2+y^2)\,dydx + 2\int_0^1\int_0^{1-x}(x^2+y^2)\,dydx$$

$$= 2\int_{-1}^0\left(x^2+x^3+\frac{1}{3}(1+x)^3\right)dx + 2\int_0^1\left(x^2-x^3+\frac{1}{3}(1-x)^3\right)dx$$

$$= 2\left[\frac{1}{3}x^3+\frac{1}{4}x^4+\frac{1}{12}(1+x)^4\right]_{-1}^0 + 2\left[\frac{1}{3}x^3-\frac{1}{4}x^4-\frac{1}{12}(1-x)^4\right]_0^1$$

$$= 2\left(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{12}\right) + 2\left(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{12}\right) = \frac{2}{3}$$
