### (1)
$x^2+3y^2$  $D: x^2+y^2 \leq 1$

$x=r\cos\theta$ $y=r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \leq r \leq 1$ $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$

$$\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^1 r^3(\cos^2\theta+3\sin^2\theta)drd\theta=\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{1}{4}(\cos^2\theta+3\sin^2\theta)d\theta=\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{1}{4}(1+2\sin^2\theta)d\theta$$

$$=\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{1}{4}\left(1+\frac{2(1-\cos2\theta)}{2}\right)d\theta=\frac{1}{4}\left[\theta+\theta-\frac{1}{2}\sin2\theta\right]_0^{2\pi}=\pi$$

### (2)
$\dfrac{x^2y}{1+x^2+y^2}$  $D: x^2+y^2 \leq 1$ $x \geq 0$ $y \geq 0$

$x=r\cos\theta$ $y=r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \leq r \leq 1$ $0 \leq \theta \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

$$\int_0^1\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{r^4\cos^2\theta\cdot\sin\theta}{1+r^2}d\theta dr$$

$\cos\theta=t$とおくと $\sin\theta d\theta=dt$ $\theta:0 \to \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ $t:1 \to 0$ これから

$$=\int_0^1\int_0^1\frac{r^4}{1+r^2}t^2 dtdr=\int_0^1\frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{r^4}{1+r^2}dr=\int_0^1\frac{1}{3}\left(r^2-1+\frac{1}{1+r^2}\right)dr$$

$$=\left[\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{1}{3}r^3-r+\tan^{-1}r\right)\right]_0^1=\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\frac{2}{3}\right)$$

### (3)
$\cos(x^2+y^2)$  $D: x^2+y^2 \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

$x=r\cos\theta$ $y=r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \leq r \leq \sqrt{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}$ $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$

$$\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^{\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}} r\cdot\cos r^2\,drd\theta=\int_0^{2\pi}\left[\frac{1}{2}\sin r^2\right]_0^{\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}}d\theta=\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{1}{2}d\theta=\pi$$

### (4)

$$\sqrt{9-x^2-y^2} \qquad D : x^2+y^2 \le 3x$$

$x = r\cos\theta$ $y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \le r \le 3\cos\theta$ $0 \le \theta \le \pi$

$$\int_0^{\pi}\int_0^{3\cos\theta} r\sqrt{9-r^2}\,dr\,d\theta = \int_0^{\pi}\left[-\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{(9-r^2)^3}\right]_0^{3\cos\theta} d\theta = \int_0^{\pi} -9\sin^3\theta+9\,d\theta = 18\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 1-\sin^3\theta\,d\theta$$

$$= 18\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{2}{3}\right) = 9\pi - 12$$

### (5)

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2+y^2}} \qquad D : x^2+y^2 \le 1$$

$x = r\cos\theta$ $y = r\sin\theta$ とおくと $0 \le r \le 1$ $0 \le \theta \le 2\pi$

$$\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^{1} \frac{r}{\sqrt{1+r^2}}\,dr\,d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi}\left[\sqrt{1+r^2}\right]_0^{1} d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi} \sqrt{2} - 1\,d\theta = \left[(\sqrt{2}-1)\theta\right]_0^{2\pi} = 2\pi(\sqrt{2}-1)$$
