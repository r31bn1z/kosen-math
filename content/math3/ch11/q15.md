$$x^4 - 2x^3 + 5x^2 + 2x = x(x^3 - 2x^2 + 5x + 2)$$

$x = 1 + \sqrt{5}i$ を代入して

$$(1 + \sqrt{5}i)\{(1 + \sqrt{5}i)^3 - 2(1 + \sqrt{5}i)^2 + 5(1 + \sqrt{5}i) + 2\}$$

$$= (1 + \sqrt{5}i)\{1 + 3\sqrt{5}i - 15 - 5\sqrt{5}i - 2(1 + 2\sqrt{5}i - 5) + 5 + 5\sqrt{5}i + 2\}$$

$$= (1 + \sqrt{5}i)(1 - \sqrt{5}i)$$

$$1 + 5 = 6$$
