$|a| = 1$ から $a\bar{a} = 1$

$$\left|\frac{a-\beta}{1-a\bar{\beta}}\right| = \sqrt{\frac{(a-\beta)(\overline{a-\beta})}{(1-a\bar{\beta})(1-\overline{a\bar{\beta}})}} = \sqrt{\frac{a\bar{a} - a\bar{\beta} - \bar{a}\beta + \beta\bar{\beta}}{1 - a\bar{\beta} - \bar{a}\beta + a\bar{\beta}\bar{a}\beta}}$$

$a\bar{a} = 1$ を代入して

$$= \sqrt{\frac{1 - a\bar{\beta} - \bar{a}\beta + \beta\bar{\beta}}{1 - a\bar{\beta} - \bar{a}\beta + \beta\bar{\beta}}} = 1$$
