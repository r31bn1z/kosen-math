### (2)

$\sqrt{i} = z$ とおくと両辺を 2 乗して

$$z^2 = i$$

$z = r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)$ におくと

$$r^2(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^2 = i \quad r^2(\cos 2\theta + i\sin 2\theta) = i$$

これから

$$r = 1 \quad 2\theta = \frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi \quad \theta = \frac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$$

$k=0$ のとき $\theta = \frac{\pi}{4}$、$k=1$ のとき $\theta = \frac{5}{4}\pi$ これから

$$z_1 = \cos\frac{\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}i$$

$$z_2 = \cos\frac{5}{4}\pi + i\sin\frac{5}{4}\pi = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}i$$
