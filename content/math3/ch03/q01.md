### (1)
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n}$$

$$r = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{\dfrac{1}{n}}{\dfrac{1}{n+1}} \right| = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{n+1}{n} \right| = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| 1 + \frac{1}{n} \right| = 1$$

### (2)
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (-1)^n (n+1) x^n$$

$$r = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{(-1)^n (n+1)}{(-1)^n (n+2)} \right| = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{1 + \dfrac{1}{n}}{1 + \dfrac{2}{n}} \right| = 1$$

### (3)
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{\sqrt{n}}$$

$$r = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}}}{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}}} \right| = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{\sqrt{n+1}}{\sqrt{n}} \right| = 1$$

### (4)
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{2^n}$$

$$r = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{\dfrac{1}{2^n}}{\dfrac{1}{2^{n+1}}} \right| = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{2^{n+1}}{2^n} \right| = 2$$

### (5)

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{3^n n^2}$$

$$r = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3^n n^2}}{\dfrac{1}{3^{n+1}(n+1)^2}} \right| = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{3^{n+1}(n+1)^2}{3^n n^2} \right| = 3$$

### (6)

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n^n}$$

$$r = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{n^n}}{\dfrac{1}{(n+1)^{n+1}}} \right| = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{(n+1)^{n+1}}{n^n} \right| \qquad \text{分母分子に } \dfrac{1}{n^{n+1}} \text{ を掛けると}$$

$$= \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \dfrac{\left(1+\dfrac{1}{n}\right)^{n+1}}{\dfrac{1}{n}} \right| = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| n\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{n+1} \right| = \infty$$
