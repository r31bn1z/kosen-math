$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x - (a + bx + cx^2)}{x^3} = d \qquad (d \neq 0)$$

$$= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\left(x - \dfrac{x^3}{3!} + \dfrac{x^5}{5!} - \cdots\right) - (a + bx + cx^2)}{x^3}$$

これから

$$a = 0$$

$$x - bx = 0$$

$$cx^2 = 0$$

よって $a = 0$、$b = 1$、$c = 0$

このとき

$$d = -\frac{1}{3!} = -\frac{1}{6}$$
