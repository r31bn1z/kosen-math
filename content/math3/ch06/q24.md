$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial \varphi} = z_x \cos\alpha + z_y \sin\alpha$$

$$\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial \varphi^2} = z_{xx} \cos\alpha \cdot \cos\alpha + z_{yy} \sin\alpha \cdot \sin\alpha = z_{xx} \cos^2\alpha + z_{yy} \sin^2\alpha$$

$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial \rho} = -z_x \sin\alpha + z_y \cos\alpha$$

$$\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial \rho^2} = z_{xx} \sin^2\alpha + z_{yy} \cos^2\alpha$$

左辺 $= z_{\varphi\varphi} + z_{\rho\rho} = z_{xx} \cos^2\alpha + z_{yy} \sin^2\alpha + z_{xx} \sin^2\alpha + z_{yy} \cos^2\alpha$

$= z_{xx} + z_{yy} =$ 右辺
