### (1)

$$\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2=\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial r}\right)^2+\frac{1}{r^2}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial \theta}\right)^2$$

右辺$=\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\cdot\frac{\partial x}{\partial r}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\cdot\frac{\partial y}{\partial r}\right)^2+\frac{1}{r^2}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\cdot\frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\cdot\frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta}\right)^2$

$$=\cos^2\theta\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2+2\cos\theta\cdot\sin\theta\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\cdot\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}+\sin^2\theta\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2$$

$$+\left(\frac{1}{r}\right)^2\left\{r^2\sin^2\theta\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2-2r^2\cos\theta\cdot\sin\theta\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\cdot\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}+r^2\cos^2\theta\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2\right\}$$

$$=\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2=\text{左辺}$$

### (2)

$$\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}=\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial r^2}+\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial z}{\partial r}+\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial \theta^2}$$

$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial r}=\cos\theta\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+\sin\theta\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\qquad\qquad \frac{\partial z}{\partial \theta}=r\left(-\sin\theta\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+\cos\theta\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)$$

$$\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial r^2}=\cos^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}+2\sin\theta\cdot\cos\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y}+\sin^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}\qquad ①$$

$$\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial \theta^2}=r^2\left(\sin^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}-2\sin\theta\cdot\cos\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y}+\cos^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}\right)-r\frac{\partial z}{\partial r}$$

$$\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial \theta^2}+\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial z}{\partial r}=\sin^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}-2\sin\theta\cdot\cos\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y}+\cos^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}\qquad ②$$

右辺＝①+②

$$=\cos^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}+2\sin\theta\cdot\cos\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y}+\sin^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}+\sin^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}-2\sin\theta\cdot\cos\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y}+\cos^2\theta\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}$$

$$=\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}=\text{左辺}$$
