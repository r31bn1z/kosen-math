### (1)
$$u = \frac{x+y}{z}$$

$$u_x = \frac{1}{z} \qquad u_y = \frac{1}{z} \qquad u_z = \frac{-(x+y)}{z^2}$$

### (2)
$$u = \sin xyz$$

$$u_x = yz \cdot \cos xyz \qquad u_y = xz \cdot \cos xyz \qquad u_z = xy \cdot \cos xyz$$

### (3)
$$u = y\log(x^2+z^2)$$

$$u_x = \frac{2xy}{x^2+z^2} \qquad u_y = \log(x^2+z^2) \qquad u_z = \frac{2yz}{x^2+z^2}$$

### (4)
$$u = (ax+by+cz)^2$$

$$u_x = 2a(ax+by+cz) \qquad u_y = 2b(ax+by+cz) \qquad u_z = 2c(ax+by+cz)$$
