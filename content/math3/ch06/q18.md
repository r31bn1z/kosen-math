### (1)
$f_{(x,y)} = \log(x^2+y^2)$

$$f_x = \frac{2x}{x^2+y^2} \qquad f_y = \frac{2y}{x^2+y^2} \qquad f_{xx} = \frac{2(x^2+y^2)-4x^2}{(x^2+y^2)^2} \qquad f_{yy} = \frac{2(x^2+y^2)-4y^2}{(x^2+y^2)^2}$$

$$\Delta f = \frac{-2x^2+2y^2+2x^2-2y^2}{(x^2+y^2)^2} = 0$$

### (2)
$f_{(x,y)} = \tan^{-1}\dfrac{y}{x}$

$$f_x = \frac{-y}{x^2+y^2} \qquad f_y = \frac{x}{x^2+y^2} \qquad f_{xx} = \frac{2xy}{(x^2+y^2)^2} \qquad f_{yy} = \frac{-2xy}{(x^2+y^2)^2}$$

$$\Delta f = \frac{2xy-2xy}{(x^2+y^2)^2} = 0$$

### (3)
$f_{(x,y)} = e^{ax}(\cos by + \sin by)$

$$f_x = ae^{ax}(\cos by+\sin by) \qquad f_y = be^{ax}(-\sin by+\cos by)$$

$$f_{xx} = a^2 e^{ax}(\cos by+\sin by) \qquad f_{yy} = -b^2 e^{ax}(\cos by+\sin by)$$

$$\Delta f = (a^2-b^2)e^{ax}(\cos by+\sin by)$$

### (4)
$f_{(x,y,z)} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}}$

$$f_x = -\frac{x}{\sqrt{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3}} \qquad f_y = -\frac{y}{\sqrt{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3}} \qquad f_z = -\frac{z}{\sqrt{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3}}$$

$$f_{xx} = -\frac{\sqrt{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3} - x\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot 2x\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}}{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3} = -\frac{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}\,(-2x^2+y^2+z^2)}{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3}$$

同様に考えて

$$f_{yy} = -\frac{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}\,(x^2-2y^2+z^2)}{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3}$$

$$f_{zz} = -\frac{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}\,(x^2+y^2-2z^2)}{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3}$$

$$\Delta f = \frac{\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}\,\{(-2x^2+y^2+z^2)+(x^2-2y^2+z^2)+(x^2+y^2-2z^2)\}}{(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3} = 0$$
