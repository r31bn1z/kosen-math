### (1)
$$z = xy$$

$$dz = ydx + xdy$$

### (2)
$$z = \log(x^2+y^2)$$

$$dz = \frac{2x}{x^2+y^2}dx + \frac{2y}{x^2+y^2}dy = \frac{2(xdx+ydy)}{x^2+y^2}$$

### (3)
$$z = \frac{y}{x}$$

$$dz = -\frac{y}{x^2}dx + \frac{1}{x}dy$$

### (4)
$$z = \sin^{-1}\frac{y}{x} \qquad (x>0)$$

$$dz = \frac{-\frac{y}{x}}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}dx + \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}dy = \frac{xdy-ydx}{x\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}$$
