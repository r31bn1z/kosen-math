### (1)
$$z = xye^{xy}$$

$$z_x = ye^{xy} + xy^2 e^{xy} \qquad z_y = xe^{xy} + x^2 ye^{xy}$$

### (2)
$$z = \log_y x = \frac{\log x}{\log y}$$

$$z_x = \frac{1}{x\log y} \qquad z_y = \frac{-\log x}{y(\log y)^2}$$

### (3)
$$z = x^2\sqrt{1-y^2}$$

$$z_x = 2x\sqrt{1-y^2} \qquad z_y = \frac{-x^2 y}{\sqrt{1-y^2}}$$

### (4)
$$z = \log(\sin x + \cos y)$$

$$z_x = \frac{\cos x}{\sin x + \cos y} \qquad z_y = \frac{-\sin y}{\sin x + \cos y}$$
