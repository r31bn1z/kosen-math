$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = 2x + \frac{y}{2}$$

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = ax + y$$

$$\int f_{(x)}\,dx = x^2 + \frac{y}{2}x + C_1 \qquad \int f_{(y)}\,dy = axy + \frac{1}{2}y^2 + C_2$$

$$\frac{1}{2}xy = axy \qquad a = \frac{1}{2}$$

よって

$$f_{(x,y)} = x^2 + \frac{1}{2}xy + \frac{1}{2}y^2 + C \qquad (\text{C \text{は積分定数}})$$
