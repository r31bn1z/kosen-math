### (1)
$$u = \log(x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz)$$

$$u_x = \frac{3x^2 - 3yz}{x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz}$$

### (2)
$$u_y = \frac{3y^2 - 3xz}{x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz} \qquad u_z = \frac{3z^2 - 3xy}{x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz}$$

$$u_x + u_y + u_z = \frac{3(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - xy - yz - zx)}{x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz} = \frac{3(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - xy - yz - zx)}{(x + y + z)(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - xy - yz - zx)}$$

$$= \frac{3}{x + y + z}$$
