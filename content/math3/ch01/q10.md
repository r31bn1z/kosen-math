### (1)

$$x = t^3 - t$$

$$y = t^2 + t \qquad (t = 1)$$

$$x' = 3t^2 - 1 \qquad t = 1 \text{のとき} \qquad x' = 3 - 1 = 2$$

$$y' = 2t + 1 \qquad t = 1 \text{のとき} \qquad y' = 2 + 1 = 3$$

$$x'' = 6t \qquad t = 1 \text{のとき} \qquad x'' = 6$$

$$y'' = 2$$

これから

$$\vec{v} = (x', y') = (2, 3) \qquad \vec{a} = (x'', y'') = (6, 2) \qquad v = |\vec{v}| = \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{13}$$

### (2)

$$x = t - \sin t$$

$$y = 1 - \cos t \qquad \left(t = \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$$

$$x' = 1 - \cos t \qquad t = \frac{\pi}{3} \text{のとき} \qquad x' = 1 - \cos\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{1}{2}$$

$$y' = -(-\sin t) = \sin t \qquad t = \frac{\pi}{3} \text{のとき} \qquad y' = \sin\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$x'' = \sin t \qquad t = \frac{\pi}{3} \text{のとき} \qquad x'' = \sin\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$y'' = \cos t \qquad t = \frac{\pi}{3} \text{のとき} \qquad y'' = \cos\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{1}{2}$$

これから

$$\vec{v} = (x', y') = \left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) \qquad \vec{a} = (x'', y'') = \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right) \qquad v = |\vec{v}| = \sqrt{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2} = 1$$
