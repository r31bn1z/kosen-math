### (1)
$$y = x^3 + 2x^2 - 3x - 1$$

$$y' = 3x^2 + 4x - 3$$

$$y'' = 6x + 4$$

### (2)
$$y = \frac{1}{2x+1} = (2x+1)^{-1}$$

$$y' = -2(2x+1)^{-2}$$

$$y'' = -2 \cdot (-2) \cdot (2x+1)^{-3} \cdot 2 = 8(2x+1)^{-3} = \frac{8}{(2x+1)^3}$$

### (3)
$$y = \sin 3x$$

$$y' = 3\cos 3x$$

$$y'' = -9\sin 3x$$

### (4)
$$y = \cos^2 x$$

$$y' = 2\cos x \cdot (-\sin x) = -2\cos x \cdot \sin x$$

$$y'' = -2\{-\sin x \cdot \sin x + \cos x \cdot \cos x\} = 2(\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x)$$

### (5)
$$y = e^{-2x}$$

$$y' = -2e^{-2x}$$

$$y'' = -2(-2e^{-2x}) = 4e^{-2x}$$

### (6)

$$y = xe^x$$

$$y' = e^x + xe^x = (1+x)e^x$$

$$y'' = e^x + (1+x)e^x = (x+2)e^x$$

### (7)

$$y = \log(x+1)$$

$$y' = \frac{1}{x+1}$$

$$y'' = \frac{-1}{(x+1)^2}$$

### (8)

$$y = \log \sin x$$

$$y' = \frac{\cos x}{\sin x}$$

$$y'' = \frac{-\sin x \cdot \sin x - \cos x \cdot \cos x}{\sin^2 x} = \frac{-\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x}{\sin^2 x} = \frac{-1}{\sin^2 x}$$
