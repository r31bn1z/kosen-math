### (1)
$$\sin^{-1}0=0$$

### (2)
$$\cos^{-1}(-1)=\pi$$

### (3)
$$\tan^{-1}1=\frac{\pi}{4}$$

### (4)
$$\sin^{-1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}=\frac{\pi}{4}$$

### (5)
$$\cos^{-1}\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}=\frac{\pi}{6}$$

### (6)
$$\tan^{-1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}=\frac{\pi}{6}$$

### (7)
$$\sin^{-1}\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)=-\frac{\pi}{6}$$

### (8)
$$\cos^{-1}\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)=\frac{3}{4}\pi$$

### (9)
$$\tan^{-1}(-\sqrt{3})=-\frac{\pi}{3}$$
