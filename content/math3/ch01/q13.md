### (1)
$$r\sin\theta = 1$$

$y = r\sin\theta$ から $y = 1$

### (2)
$$r\cos\theta = -2$$

$x = r\cos\theta$ から $x = -2$

### (3)
$$\theta = \frac{\pi}{3}$$

$\theta = \tan^{-1}\dfrac{y}{x}$  $\tan\theta = \dfrac{y}{x}$ から  $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{y}{x}$  $\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{y}{x}$  $\therefore y = \sqrt{3}x$

### (4)
$$r = 3$$

$r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ から $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = 3$  $\therefore x^2 + y^2 = 9$

### (5)
$$r = 2\cos\theta$$

$r^2 = 2r\cos\theta$  $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$  $x = r\cos\theta$ から  $\therefore x^2 + y^2 = 2x$

### (6)
$$r = 4\sin\theta$$

$r^2 = 4r\sin\theta$  $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$  $y = r\sin\theta$ から  $\therefore x^2 + y^2 = 4y$
