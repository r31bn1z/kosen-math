### (1)

$$x = 2t$$

$$y = t^2 - 1$$

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = 2 \qquad \frac{dy}{dt} = 2t$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{\dfrac{dy}{dt}}{\dfrac{dx}{dt}} = \frac{2t}{2} = t$$

### (2)

$$x = pt^2$$

$$y = 2pt$$

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = 2pt \qquad \frac{dy}{dt} = 2p$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{2p}{2pt} = \frac{1}{t}$$

### (3)

$$x = \frac{1}{1+t^2}$$

$$y = \frac{t}{1+t^2}$$

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = \frac{-2t}{(1+t^2)^2} \qquad \frac{dy}{dt} = \frac{(1+t^2) - t \cdot 2t}{(1+t^2)^2} = \frac{1-t^2}{(1+t^2)^2}$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{\dfrac{1-t^2}{(1+t^2)^2}}{\dfrac{-2t}{(1+t^2)^2}} = \frac{1-t^2}{-2t} = \frac{t^2-1}{2t}$$

### (4)

$$x = a\dfrac{1}{\cos t}$$

$$y = b\tan t$$

$$\dfrac{dx}{dt} = a \cdot \dfrac{-(-\sin t)}{\cos^{2} t} = a \cdot \dfrac{\sin t}{\cos^{2} t} \qquad \dfrac{dy}{dt} = b \cdot \dfrac{1}{\cos^{2} t}$$

$$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{b \cdot \dfrac{1}{\cos^{2} t}}{a \cdot \dfrac{\sin t}{\cos^{2} t}} = \dfrac{b}{a\sin t}$$
