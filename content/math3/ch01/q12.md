### (1)
$(2,2)$

$$r=\sqrt{2^{2}+2^{2}}=\sqrt{8}=2\sqrt{2} \qquad \theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{2}{2}=\tan^{-1}1=\frac{\pi}{4} \qquad \therefore (2\sqrt{2},\frac{\pi}{4})$$

### (2)
$(-\sqrt{3},1)$

$$r=\sqrt{(-\sqrt{3})^{2}+1^{2}}=\sqrt{4}=2 \qquad \theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{1}{-\sqrt{3}}=\frac{5}{6}\pi \qquad \therefore (2,\frac{5}{6}\pi)$$

### (3)
$(2,-2\sqrt{3})$

$$r=\sqrt{2^{2}+(-2\sqrt{3})^{2}}=\sqrt{16}=4 \qquad \theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{-2\sqrt{3}}{2}=\tan^{-1}(-\sqrt{3})=\frac{5}{3}\pi \qquad \therefore (4,\frac{5}{3}\pi)$$

### (4)
$(\sqrt{3},1)$

$$r=\sqrt{(\sqrt{3})^{2}+1^{2}}=\sqrt{4}=2 \qquad \theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}=\frac{\pi}{6} \qquad \therefore (2,\frac{\pi}{6})$$

### (5)
$(0,-\sqrt{3})$

$$r=\sqrt{0+(-\sqrt{3})^{2}}=\sqrt{3} \qquad \theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{-\sqrt{3}}{0}=\tan^{-1}(-\infty)=-\frac{\pi}{2}\to\frac{3}{2}\pi \qquad \therefore (\sqrt{3},\frac{3}{2}\pi)$$

### (6)
$(-1,0)$

$$r=\sqrt{(-1)^{2}+0}=1 \qquad \theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{0}{-1}=\tan^{-1}0=\pi \qquad \therefore (1,\pi)$$
