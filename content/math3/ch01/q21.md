### (1)

$$y = \sin^{-1}\sqrt{1-x^2}$$

$$y' = \dfrac{\dfrac{-2x}{2\sqrt{1-x^2}}}{\sqrt{1-(1-x^2)}} = \dfrac{\dfrac{-x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}}{\sqrt{x^2}} = \dfrac{-x}{|x|\sqrt{1-x^2}} \qquad \dfrac{x}{|x|} = \pm 1 \text{ から}$$

$$= \pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$$

### (2)

$$y = \tan^{-1}\dfrac{x-1}{x+1}$$

$$y' = \dfrac{\dfrac{x+1-(x-1)}{(x+1)^2}}{\left(\dfrac{x-1}{x+1}\right)^2+1} = \dfrac{2}{(x-1)^2+(x+1)^2} = \dfrac{2}{2x^2+2} = \dfrac{1}{x^2+1}$$

### (3)

$$y = x \cdot \sin^{-1}x$$

$$y' = \sin^{-1}x + \dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$$

### (4)

$$y = (1+x^2)\tan^{-1}x$$

$$y' = 2x \cdot \tan^{-1}x + (1+x^2) \cdot \dfrac{1}{1+x^2} = 2x \cdot \tan^{-1}x + 1$$

### (5)

$$y = x \cdot \sin^{-1}x + \sqrt{1-x^2}$$

$$y' = \sin^{-1}x + \dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} + \dfrac{-2x}{2\sqrt{1-x^2}} = \sin^{-1}x$$

### (6)

$$y = \tan^{-1} x + \log(1+x^2)$$

$$y' = \frac{1}{x^2+1} + \frac{2x}{1+x^2} = \frac{2x+1}{x^2+1}$$
