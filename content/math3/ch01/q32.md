### (1)

$$\begin{cases} f_{(t)} = e^{-t}\cos t \\ g_{(t)} = e^{-t}\sin t \end{cases}$$

$$f' = -e^{-t}\cos t - e^{-t}\sin t \qquad g' = -e^{-t}\sin t + e^{-t}\cos t$$

$$\begin{pmatrix} f' \\ g' \end{pmatrix} = A\begin{pmatrix} f \\ g \end{pmatrix} \qquad A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \text{とすると}$$

$$\begin{pmatrix} -e^{-t}\cos t - e^{-t}\sin t \\ -e^{-t}\sin t + e^{-t}\cos t \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} e^{-t}\cos t \\ e^{-t}\sin t \end{pmatrix}$$

$$-e^{-t}\cos t - e^{-t}\sin t = ae^{-t}\cos t + be^{-t}\sin t$$
$$-e^{-t}\sin t + e^{-t}\cos t = ce^{-t}\cos t + de^{-t}\sin t$$

これから $a = -1$、$b = -1$、$c = 1$、$d = -1$

よって $A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

### (2)

$$\begin{pmatrix} f^{(4)} \\ g^{(4)} \end{pmatrix} = A^4 \begin{pmatrix} f \\ g \end{pmatrix}$$

$$A^4 = \begin{pmatrix} -4 & 0 \\ 0 & -4 \end{pmatrix} \quad \text{なので}$$

$$f^{(4)} = -4f$$
$$g^{(4)} = -4g$$
