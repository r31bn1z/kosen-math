### (1)
$y = \sin^{-1} 5x$

$$y' = \frac{(5x)'}{\sqrt{1-25x^2}} = \frac{5}{\sqrt{1-25x^2}}$$

### (2)
$y = \tan^{-1} 3x$

$$y' = \frac{(3x)'}{9x^2+1} = \frac{3}{9x^2+1}$$

### (3)
$y = \sin^{-1} \dfrac{x}{3}$

$$y' = \frac{\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)'}{\sqrt{1-\dfrac{x^2}{9}}} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{3}}{\sqrt{\dfrac{9-x^2}{9}}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{9-x^2}}$$

### (4)
$y = \tan^{-1} \sqrt{x}$

$$y' = \frac{(\sqrt{x})'}{1+x} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}}{1+x} = \frac{1}{2(1+x)\sqrt{x}}$$
