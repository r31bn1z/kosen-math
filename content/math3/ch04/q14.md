### (1)
$$\frac{1}{(x^2+4)^2}$$

$$\int \frac{1}{(x^2+4)^2}\,dx = \frac{1}{8}\left(\frac{x}{x^2+4} + \frac{1}{2}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{2}\right) + C$$ （C は積分定数）

### (2)
$$\frac{x^2}{(x^2+4)^2}$$

$$\int \frac{x^2}{(x^2+4)^2}\,dx = \frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{x}{x^2+4} + \frac{1}{2}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{2}\right) + C$$ （C は積分定数）

### (3)
$$\frac{1}{(x^2+4)^3}$$

$$\int \frac{1}{(x^2+4)^3}\,dx = \frac{1}{16}\left\{\frac{x}{(x^2+4)^2} + 3\cdot\frac{1}{8}\left(\frac{x}{x^2+4} + \frac{1}{2}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{2}\right)\right\} + C$$

$$= \frac{1}{16}\left\{\frac{x}{(x^2+4)^2} + \frac{3}{8}\left(\frac{x}{x^2+4} + \frac{1}{2}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{2}\right)\right\} + C$$ （C は積分定数）
