### (1)

$$\int \frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\,dx = -\frac{1}{2(x^2+a^2)}$$

$$\int \frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\,dx = \int \frac{2x}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\,dx = -\frac{1}{2(x^2+a^2)}+C \qquad \text{(C \text{は積分定数})}$$

### (2)

$$\int \frac{x^2}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\,dx = \frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}\right)$$

$$\int \frac{x^2}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\,dx = \int x\frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\,dx = x\left(-\frac{1}{2(x^2+a^2)}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\int \frac{1}{x^2+a^2}\,dx$$

$$= -\frac{x}{2(x^2+a^2)}+\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}+C = \frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}\right)+C \qquad \text{(C \text{は積分定数})}$$

### (3)

$$\int \frac{1}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\,dx = \frac{1}{2a^2}\left(\frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}\right)$$

$$\int \frac{1}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\,dx = \int \frac{1}{a^2}\left\{\frac{1}{x^2+a^2}-\frac{x^2}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\right\}dx$$

$$= \frac{1}{a^2}\left\{\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}-\frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}\right)\right\}+C = \frac{1}{2a^2}\left(\frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}\right)+C$$

（C は積分定数）

### (4)

$$I_n = \frac{1}{2(n-1)a^2}\left\{\frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}}+(2n-3)I_{n-1}\right\} \qquad (n\ge 2)$$

$$I_n = \int \frac{1}{(x^2+a^2)^n}\,dx = \int \left\{\frac{1}{a^2(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}}-\frac{x^2}{a^2(x^2+a^2)^n}\right\}dx = \int \frac{1}{a^2}\cdot\frac{1}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}}\,dx - \frac{1}{a^2}\int \frac{x^2}{(x^2+a^2)^n}\,dx$$

ここで $z = \int \frac{x^2}{(x^2+a^2)^n}\,dx$ とすると

$$z = \int \frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^n}\cdot x\,dx = -\frac{1}{2(n-1)}\cdot\frac{1}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}}\cdot x + \int \frac{1}{2(n-1)}\cdot\frac{1}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}}\,dx$$

$$= -\frac{1}{2(n-1)}\left\{\frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}} - \int \frac{1}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}}\,dx\right\} = -\frac{1}{2(n-1)}\left\{\frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}} - I_{n-1}\right\}$$

これから

$$I_n = \int \frac{1}{a^2}\cdot\frac{1}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}}\,dx - \frac{1}{a^2}\cdot\left\{-\frac{1}{2(n-1)}\left(\frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}} - I_{n-1}\right)\right\}$$

$$= \frac{1}{a^2}I_{n-1} + \frac{1}{2(n-1)a^2}\left\{\frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}} - I_{n-1}\right\} = \frac{1}{2(n-1)a^2}\left\{2(n-1)I_{n-1} + \frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}} - I_{n-1}\right\}$$

$$= \frac{1}{2(n-1)a^2}\left\{\frac{x}{(x^2+a^2)^{n-1}} + (2n-3)I_{n-1}\right\}$$
