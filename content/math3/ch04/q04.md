### (1)

$$\frac{x+1}{\sqrt{x^2+4}}$$

$$\int \frac{x+1}{\sqrt{x^2+4}}\,dx = \int \left\{ \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+4}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+4}} \right\} dx = \sqrt{x^2+4} + \log\left|x+\sqrt{x^2+4}\right| + C$$

(C は積分定数)

### (2)

$$\frac{1-x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$$

$$\int \frac{1-x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,dx = \int \left\{ \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} - \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \right\} dx = \sin^{-1}x + \sqrt{1-x^2} + C$$

(C は積分定数)

### (3)

$$\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^4-3}}$$

$$\int \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^4-3}}\,dx \qquad x^2=t \ \text{とおくと} \qquad 2x\,dx = dt \qquad x\,dx = \frac{1}{2}dt \qquad \text{これから}$$

$$= \int \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt{t^2-3}}\,dt = \frac{1}{2}\log\left|t+\sqrt{t^2-3}\right| + C \qquad \text{元に戻して}$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\log\left|x^2+\sqrt{x^4-3}\right| + C$$

(C は積分定数)

### (4)

$$\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^4}}$$

$$\int \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^4}}\,dx \qquad x^2=t \ \text{とおくと} \qquad 2x\,dx = dt \qquad x\,dx = \frac{1}{2}dt \qquad \text{これから}$$

$$= \int \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-t^2}}\,dt = \frac{1}{2}\sin^{-1}t + C \qquad \text{元に戻して}$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\sin^{-1}x^2 + C$$

(C は積分定数)
