$$x = pt^2 \qquad y = 2pt \qquad (p > 0)$$

$$s = \int_0^t \sqrt{(2pt)^2 + (2p)^2}\, dt = \int_0^t 2p\sqrt{t^2+1}\, dt = 2p\left[\frac{1}{2}t\sqrt{t^2+1} + \log\left|t+\sqrt{t^2+1}\right|\right]_0^t$$

$$= p\left\{t\sqrt{t^2+1} + \log\left|t+\sqrt{t^2+1}\right|\right\}$$
