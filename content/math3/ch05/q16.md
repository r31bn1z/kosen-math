### (1)

$$\int_0^a x\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\,dx=\left[-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(a^2-x^2)^3}\,\frac{2}{3}\right]_0^a=\frac{1}{3}a^3$$

### (2)

$$\int_0^{2a}\sqrt{2ax-x^2}\,dx=\int_0^{2a}\sqrt{a^2-(x-a)^2}\,dx=\left[\frac{1}{2}\left\{(x-a)\sqrt{a^2-(x-a)^2}+a^2\sin^{-1}\frac{x-a}{a}\right\}\right]_0^{2a}$$

$$=\frac{1}{2}\left\{a^2\frac{\pi}{2}-a^2\left(-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)\right\}=\frac{\pi a^2}{2}$$

### (3)

$$\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{dx}{(1+x^2)^3}=\left[\frac{1}{4}\left\{\frac{x}{(x^2+1)^2}+3\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{x}{x^2+1}+\tan^{-1}x\right)\right)\right\}\right]_{-1}^{1}$$

$$=\frac{1}{4}\left\{\frac{1}{4}+\frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+\frac{1}{4}-\frac{3}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right\}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{3}{16}\pi$$

### (4)

$$\int_0^a \frac{x^2}{(a^2+x^2)^2}\,dx=\left[\frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}\right)\right]_0^a=\frac{1}{2}\left\{-\frac{1}{2a}+\frac{1}{a}\cdot\frac{\pi}{4}\right\}=\frac{\pi-2}{8a}$$
