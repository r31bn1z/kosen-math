### (1)
$y = \dfrac{8}{x^2+4}$    $x = 2y$    $x = 0$

$$\int_0^2 \frac{8}{x^2+4}\,dx - \int_0^2 \frac{1}{2}x\,dx = \left[8\cdot\frac{1}{2}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{2}\right]_0^2 - \left[\frac{1}{4}x^2\right]_0^2 = 4\cdot\frac{\pi}{4}-1=\pi-1$$

### (2)
$y = \dfrac{8}{x^2+4}$    $y = \dfrac{x^2}{4}$

$$\int_{-2}^2 \frac{8}{x^2+4}\,dx - \int_{-2}^2 \frac{x^2}{4}\,dx = 2\left\{\int_0^2 \frac{8}{x^2+4}-\frac{x^2}{4}\,dx\right\} = 2\left[4\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{2}-\frac{x^3}{12}\right]_0^2 = 2\left(4\cdot\frac{\pi}{4}-\frac{2}{3}\right)=2\pi-\frac{4}{3}$$
