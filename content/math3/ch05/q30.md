### (1)

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{k}{n(n+k)} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{\dfrac{k}{n}}{1+\dfrac{k}{n}} = \int_0^1 \frac{x}{1+x}\,dx = \int_0^1 \left(1 - \frac{1}{1+x}\right)dx = \left[x - \log|x+1|\right]_0^1 = 1 - \log 2$$

### (2)

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n^2} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt{n^2 - k^2} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt{1 - \frac{k^2}{n^2}} = \int_0^1 \sqrt{1-x^2}\,dx = \left[\frac{1}{2}\left(x\sqrt{1-x^2} + \sin^{-1} x\right)\right]_0^1 = \frac{\pi}{4}$$
