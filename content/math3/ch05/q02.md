### (1)

$\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{\sin x+\cos x}$  $\tan\dfrac{x}{2}=t$ とおくと  $dx=\dfrac{2}{1+t^2}dt$  $\sin x=\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}$  $\cos x=\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}$

$x:0\to\pi/2$  $t:0\to1$  これから

$$=\int_0^1\frac{1}{\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}+\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}}\cdot\frac{2}{1+t^2}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{2}{2t+1-t^2}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{2}{2-(t-1)^2}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{-2}{(t-1)^2-2}\,dt$$

$$=-\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\left[\log\left|\frac{t-1-\sqrt2}{t-1+\sqrt2}\right|\right]_0^1=-\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\left(\log|-1|-\log\left|\frac{-1-\sqrt2}{-1+\sqrt2}\right|\right)=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\log\left|\frac{-1-\sqrt2}{-1+\sqrt2}\right|$$

$$=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\log\left|\frac{(-1-\sqrt2)^2}{(-1+\sqrt2)(-1-\sqrt2)}\right|=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\log(1+\sqrt2)^2=\frac{2}{\sqrt2}\log(1+\sqrt2)=\sqrt2\log(1+\sqrt2)$$

### (2)

$\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{2-\cos x}$  $\tan\dfrac{x}{2}=t$ とおくと  $dx=\dfrac{2}{1+t^2}dt$  $\cos x=\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}$

$x:0\to\pi/2$  $t:0\to1$  これから

$$=\int_0^1\frac{1}{2-\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}}\cdot\frac{2}{1+t^2}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{2}{2+2t^2-1+t^2}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{2}{3t^2+1}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{\dfrac{2}{3}}{t^2+\dfrac{1}{3}}\,dt=\frac{2}{3}\left[\frac{1}{1/\sqrt3}\tan^{-1}\frac{t}{1/\sqrt3}\right]_0^1$$

$$=\frac{2\sqrt3}{3}\left[\tan^{-1}\sqrt3\,x\right]_0^1=\frac{2\sqrt3}{9}\pi$$

### (3)

$\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{4+5\cos x}$  $\tan\dfrac{x}{2}=t$ とおくと  $dx=\dfrac{2}{1+t^2}dt$  $\cos x=\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}$

$x:0\to\pi/2$  $t:0\to1$  これから

$$=\int_0^1\frac{1}{4+5\cdot\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}}\cdot\frac{2}{1+t^2}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{2}{4+4t^2+5-5t^2}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{2}{9-t^2}\,dt=-\frac{1}{3}\left[\log\frac{t-3}{t+3}\right]_0^1=-\frac{1}{3}\log\frac{1}{2}$$

$$=\frac{1}{3}\log2$$

### (4)

$$\int_0^{\pi/4} \frac{dx}{2+\tan x}$$

$\tan x = t$ とおくと $x = \tan^{-1} t$ $dx = \dfrac{1}{t^2+1}dt$

$x : 0 \to \pi/4$ $t : 0 \to 1$ これから

$$=\int_0^1 \frac{1}{2+t}\cdot\frac{1}{t^2+1}dt = \int_0^1 \frac{1}{5}\left(\frac{1}{t+2}+\frac{-t+2}{t^2+1}\right)dt = \int_0^1 \frac{1}{5}\left(\frac{1}{t+2}-\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2t}{t^2+1}+\frac{2}{t^2+1}\right)dt$$

$$=\frac{1}{5}\left[\log(t+2)-\frac{1}{2}\log(t^2+1)+2\tan^{-1}t\right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{5}\left(\log 3-\log 2-\frac{1}{2}\log 2+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\frac{\pi}{10}+\frac{1}{10}\log\frac{9}{8}$$
