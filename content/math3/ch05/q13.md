### (1)
$$\int_0^\infty e^{-x}dx = \left[-e^{-x}\right]_0^\infty = 0-(-1) = 1$$

### (2)
$$\int_0^\infty xe^{-x}dx = \left[x(-e^{-x})\right]_0^\infty + \int_0^\infty e^{-x}dx = 0 + \left[-e^{-x}\right]_0^\infty = 1$$

### (3)
$$\int_1^e \frac{dx}{x\log x} \qquad \log x = t \ \text{とおくと} \quad \frac{1}{x}dx = dt \qquad x:1\to e \qquad t:0\to 1 \quad \text{これから}$$

$$= \int_0^1 \frac{1}{t}dt = \left[\log t\right]_0^1 = 0-(-\infty)$$

よって発散する

### (4)
$$\int_0^1 \log x\,dx = \left[x\log x\right]_0^1 - \int_0^1 x\cdot\frac{1}{x}dx = 0-\left[x\right]_0^1 = -1$$
