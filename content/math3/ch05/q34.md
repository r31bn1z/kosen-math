### (1)

$$x = a\cos t \qquad y = a\sin t \qquad z = \frac{ht}{2\pi} \qquad \pi a h = c$$

$$\left(\frac{dx}{dt}\right)^2 = (-a\sin t)^2 = a^2\sin^2 t \qquad \left(\frac{dy}{dt}\right)^2 = a^2\cos^2 t \qquad \left(\frac{dz}{dt}\right)^2 = \frac{h^2}{4\pi^2}$$

$$s = \int_0^{2\pi} \sqrt{a^2 + \frac{h^2}{4\pi^2}}\, dt = 2\pi\sqrt{a^2 + \frac{h^2}{4\pi^2}} \qquad \left(h = \frac{c}{a\pi}\ \text{から}\right)$$

$$= 2\pi\sqrt{a^2 + \frac{c^2}{4a^2\pi^4}}$$

$$s^2 = 4\pi^2\left(a^2 + \frac{c^2}{4a^2\pi^4}\right)$$

$$\frac{ds^2}{da} = 4\pi^2\left(2a - \frac{2c^2}{4a^3\pi^4}\right)$$

$\dfrac{ds^2}{da} = 0$ のとき $c = 2\pi^2 a^2$ これを $s$ に代入して

$$s = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{c}{2\pi^2} + \frac{c}{2\pi^2}} = 2\sqrt{c}$$

### (2)
$\pi a h = c$ から

$$\pi a h = 2\pi^2 a^2$$

$$\therefore h = 2\pi a$$
