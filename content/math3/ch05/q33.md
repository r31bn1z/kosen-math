$$\int_1^{\sqrt{3}} \frac{dx}{x^2(x^2+1)^2} = \int_1^{\sqrt{3}} \left(\frac{1}{x^2} - \frac{x^2+2}{x^4+2x^2+1}\right)dx = \int_1^{\sqrt{3}} \left(\frac{1}{x^2} - \frac{1}{x^2+1} - \frac{1}{(x^2+1)^2}\right)dx$$

$$= \left[-\frac{1}{x} - \tan^{-1}x - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{x}{x^2+1}+\tan^{-1}x\right)\right]_1^{\sqrt{3}} = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} - \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}+\frac{\pi}{3}\right) + 1 + \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$$

$$= \frac{5}{4} - \frac{11\sqrt{3}}{24} - \frac{\pi}{8}$$
