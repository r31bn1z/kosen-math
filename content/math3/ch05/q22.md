### (1)
$r = 2\sin\theta \qquad r = 6\sin\theta$

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 2\int_0^{\pi/2}(6\sin\theta)^2\,d\theta - \frac{1}{2}\cdot 2\int_0^{\pi/2}(2\sin\theta)^2\,d\theta = 36\left[\frac{1}{2}\theta-\frac{1}{4}\sin2\theta\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} - 4\left[\frac{1}{2}\theta-\frac{1}{4}\sin2\theta\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}$$

$$=9\pi-\pi=8\pi$$

### (2)
$r=a(1+\cos\theta) \qquad r=2a\cos\theta$

$$S=\frac{1}{2}\cdot 2\int_0^\pi (2a\cos\theta)^2\,d\theta-\frac{1}{2}\cdot 2\int_0^\pi \{a(1+\cos\theta)\}^2\,d\theta$$

$$=\int_0^\pi 4a^2\,\frac{1+\cos2\theta}{2}\,d\theta-\int_0^\pi a^2\left(1+2\cos\theta+\frac{1+\cos2\theta}{2}\right)d\theta$$

$$=\left[4a^2\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\sin2\theta\right)\right]_0^\pi-\left[a^2\left(\theta+2\sin\theta+\frac{1}{2}\theta+\frac{1}{4}\sin2\theta\right)\right]_0^\pi=2\pi a^2-\pi a^2-\frac{\pi}{2}a^2=\frac{\pi}{2}a^2$$
