$$r = a\theta \qquad (a > 0) \qquad (r, \theta)$$

$$s = \int_0^\theta \sqrt{a^2\theta^2 + a^2}\, d\theta = a\left[\frac{1}{2}\left\{\theta\sqrt{\theta^2+1} + \log\left|\theta + \sqrt{\theta^2+1}\right|\right\}\right]_0^\theta$$

$$= \frac{a}{2}\left\{\theta\sqrt{\theta^2+1} + \log\left|\theta + \sqrt{\theta^2+1}\right|\right\}$$
