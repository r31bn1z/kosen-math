### (1)

$\int_{k\pi}^{(k+1)\pi} e^{-x}\sin x\,dx$    5.36から

$$=\frac{1}{2}\left\{\left[-e^{-x}\sin x\right]_{k\pi}^{(k+1)\pi}+\left[-e^{-x}\cos x\right]_{k\pi}^{(k+1)\pi}\right\}=\frac{1}{2}\left(e^{-k\pi}+e^{-(k+1)\pi}\right)=\frac{1}{2}e^{-k\pi}(1+e^{-\pi})$$

### (2)

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n}S_k=\frac{1}{2}(1+e^{-\pi})\sum_{k=0}^{n}e^{-k\pi}=\frac{1}{2}(1+e^{-\pi})\{1+e^{-\pi}+e^{-2\pi}+\cdots+e^{-n\pi}\}=\frac{1}{2}(1+e^{-\pi})\frac{1-e^{-(n+1)\pi}}{1-e^{-\pi}}$$

### (3)

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n}S_k=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{2}(1+e^{-\pi})\frac{1-e^{-(n+1)\pi}}{1-e^{-\pi}}=\frac{1}{2}(1+e^{-\pi})\frac{1}{1-e^{-\pi}}=\frac{1+e^{-\pi}}{2(1-e^{-\pi})}$$
