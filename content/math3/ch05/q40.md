### (1)

$$F_{(x)} = \int_x^{\sqrt{3}x} \sqrt{1-t^2}\,dt = \left[ \frac{1}{2}\{t\sqrt{1-t^2} + \sin^{-1}t\} \right]_x^{\sqrt{3}x}$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\{\sqrt{3}x\sqrt{1-3x^2} + \sin^{-1}\sqrt{3}x - x\sqrt{1-x^2} - \sin^{-1}x\}$$

$$\frac{dF}{dx} = \frac{1}{2}\left\{ \sqrt{3}\sqrt{1-3x^2} + \sqrt{3}x\frac{-3x}{\sqrt{1-3x^2}} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{1-3x^2}} - \sqrt{1-x^2} - x\frac{-x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \right\}$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\left\{ \sqrt{3}\sqrt{1-3x^2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}(1-3x^2)\sqrt{1-3x^2}}{1-3x^2} - \sqrt{1-x^2} - \frac{(1-x^2)\sqrt{1-x^2}}{1-x^2} \right\}$$

$$= \sqrt{3}\sqrt{1-3x^2} - \sqrt{1-x^2}$$

### (1)

から　$\dfrac{dF}{dx} = \sqrt{3}\sqrt{1-3x^2} - \sqrt{1-x^2} = 0$　となるとき

$$\sqrt{3}\sqrt{1-3x^2} = \sqrt{1-x^2} \qquad 3(1-3x^2) = 1-x^2 \qquad 8x^2 = 2 \qquad x = \pm\frac{1}{2}$$

$0 \leq x \leq \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ から $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$

このとき

$$F_{(x)} = F_{(\frac{1}{2})} = \frac{1}{2}\left\{ \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sqrt{\frac{4-3}{4}} + \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{4-1}{4}} - \frac{\pi}{6} \right\} = \frac{\pi}{12}$$

### (3)

$$\lim_{x \to 0}\frac{F_{(x)}}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0}\frac{1}{2}\left\{ \sqrt{3}x\sqrt{\frac{1}{x^2}-3} + \frac{1}{x}\sin^{-1}\sqrt{3}x - x\sqrt{\frac{1}{x^2}-1} - \frac{1}{x}\sin^{-1}x \right\} \quad \text{ロピタルの定理から}$$

$$= \lim_{x \to 0}\frac{1}{2}\left\{ \sqrt{3}\sqrt{1-3x^2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{1-3x^2}} - \sqrt{1-x^2} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \right\} = \frac{1}{2}\{2\sqrt{3}-2\} = \sqrt{3}-1$$
