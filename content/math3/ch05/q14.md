### (1)
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}+\frac{1}{n+3}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n+n}\right)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{1}{n+k}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{1}{n}\cdot\frac{1}{1+\dfrac{k}{n}}$$

これから

$$=\int_0^1 \frac{1}{1+x}\,dx=\Big[\log|1+x|\Big]_0^1=\log 2$$

### (2)
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\frac{n}{n^2+1}+\frac{n}{n^2+4}+\frac{n}{n^2+9}+\cdots+\frac{n}{n^2+n^2}\right)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{n}{n^2+k^2}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{1}{n}\cdot\frac{1}{1+\left(\dfrac{k}{n}\right)^2}$$

これから

$$=\int_0^1 \frac{1}{1+x^2}\,dx=\Big[\tan^{-1}x\Big]_0^1=\frac{\pi}{4}$$
