### (1)

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 2 & -1 & 4 \\ 0 & -2 & -5 \end{pmatrix}

\begin{pmatrix} 1+9 & 4+18 & 7+27 \\ 2-2+12 & 8-5+24 & 14-8+36 \\ -4-15 & -10-30 & -16-45 \end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix} 10 & 22 & 34 \\ 12 & 27 & 42 \\ -19 & -40 & -61 \end{pmatrix}
$$

### (3)
