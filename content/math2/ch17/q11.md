### (1)

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 & 1\\0 & 1 & 1\\1 & 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$$
とおくと

$$|A-\lambda E|=\begin{vmatrix}1-\lambda & 0 & 1\\0 & 1-\lambda & 1\\1 & 1 & -\lambda\end{vmatrix}=-\lambda(1-\lambda)^2-(1-\lambda)-(1-\lambda)=\lambda^3+2\lambda^2+\lambda-2$$

$$=(\lambda-1)(\lambda+1)(\lambda-2)=0$$
$$\lambda=1,2,-1$$

$\lambda=1$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix}0 & 0 & 1\\0 & 0 & 1\\1 & 1 & -1\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 & -1\\0 & 0 & 1\\0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 & -1\\0 & 0 & 1\\0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 & 0\\0 & 0 & 1\\0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$$

$$p_1=C_1\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}\to\sqrt{2}C_1\begin{pmatrix}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\0\end{pmatrix}\to C_1'\begin{pmatrix}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\0\end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda=2$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix}-1 & 0 & 1\\0 & -1 & 1\\1 & 1 & -2\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 & -1\\0 & -1 & 1\\1 & 1 & -2\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 & -1\\0 & -1 & 1\\0 & 1 & -1\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 & -1\\0 & 1 & -1\\0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$$

$$p_2=C_2\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\to C_2\begin{pmatrix}\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\\\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\\\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda=-1$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix}2 & 0 & 1\\0 & 2 & 1\\1 & 1 & 1\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 & 1\\0 & 2 & 1\\2 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 & 1\\0 & 2 & 1\\0 & -2 & -1\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 & 1\\0 & 2 & 1\\0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 & 1\\0 & 1 & \frac{1}{2}\\0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}\to\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 & \frac{1}{2}\\0 & 1 & \frac{1}{2}\\0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$$

$p_3 = C_3 \begin{pmatrix} -1/2 \\ -1/2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \to C_3' \begin{pmatrix} -\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \\ -\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \\ \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} \end{pmatrix}$

これから直行行列Pは $\begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \\ -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \\ 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} \end{pmatrix}$ 対角化 $P^{-1}AP = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

### (2)

$A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ とすると

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} 3-\lambda & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2-\lambda & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 2-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = (3-\lambda)(2-\lambda)^2 - (2-\lambda) - (2-\lambda) = (2-\lambda)(6 - 5\lambda + \lambda^2 - 2)$$

$$= (2-\lambda)(\lambda^2 - 5\lambda + 4) = (2-\lambda)(\lambda-4)(\lambda-1)$$ $\lambda = 1,2,4$

λ = 1 のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$p_1 = C_1 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \to C_1' \begin{pmatrix} -\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \end{pmatrix}$

$\lambda = 2$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$p_2 = C_2 \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \to C_2' \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 4$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -2 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & -1 \\ 1 & -2 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -2 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$p_3 = C_3 \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \to C_3' \begin{pmatrix} \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \end{pmatrix}$$

これから直行行列 P は

$$P = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & 0 & \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \\ -\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \end{pmatrix}$$

対角化 $P^{-1}AP = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$

### (3)

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & 1 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \text{とするもと}$$

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} 1-\lambda & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & 1-\lambda & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 1-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = (1-\lambda)^3 - 8 - 8 - 12(1-\lambda) = -\lambda^3 + 3\lambda^2 - 3\lambda + 1 - 16 - 12 + 12\lambda$$

$$= -\lambda^3 + 3\lambda^2 + 9\lambda - 27 = -(\lambda + 3)(\lambda - 3)^2$$

$$\lambda = -3, 3 \text{ （３\text{は重解}）}$$

$\lambda = -3$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & 4 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 4 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & -1 \\ -2 & 4 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 4 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & 3 & -3 \\ 0 & -3 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & -2 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$p_1 = C\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \to C_1'\begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 3$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -2 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$p_2 = C_2\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} + C_3\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \to C_2'\begin{pmatrix} -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} + C_3'\begin{pmatrix} -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ 0 \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{pmatrix}$$

これから直行行列 P は

$$P = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & 0 \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{pmatrix}$$

対角化 $P^{-1}AP =$

$$\begin{pmatrix} -3 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$$
