### (1)

$$x:y:z = \begin{vmatrix} -2 & -1 \\ 3 & 2 \end{vmatrix} : \begin{vmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} : \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = -4+3:-2-2:3+4 = -1:-4:7 = 1:4:-7$$

### (2)

$$x:y:z = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ -2 & 0 \end{vmatrix} : \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 0 \end{vmatrix} : \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 3 & -2 \end{vmatrix} = 2:-3:-2-3 = 2:-3:-5$$
