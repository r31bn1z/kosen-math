### (1)

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & 4 & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$$ とする と

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} 2-\lambda & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & 4-\lambda & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & 2-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = (2-\lambda)^2(4-\lambda) + 1 + 1 - (4-\lambda) - 2(2-\lambda)$$

$$= (4-\lambda)(4-4\lambda + \lambda^2 - 1) + 2 - 4 + 2\lambda = (4-\lambda)(3 - 4\lambda + \lambda^2) - 2(1-\lambda) = (4-\lambda)(1-\lambda)(3-\lambda) - 2(1-\lambda)$$

$$= (1-\lambda)(12 - 7\lambda + \lambda^2 - 2) = (1-\lambda)(10 - 7\lambda + \lambda^2) = (1-\lambda)(2-\lambda)(5-\lambda) \quad \lambda = 1,2,5$$

$\lambda = 1$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & 3 & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_1 = C_1^1 \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 2$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ -1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_2 = C_2^1 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 5$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -3 & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & -1 & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & -3 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ -3 & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & 1 & -3 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & -4 \\ 0 & 2 & -4 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_3 = C_3^1 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

### (2)

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 2 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$$ とする と

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} 1-\lambda & 0 & -1 \\ 2 & 2-\lambda & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 2-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = (1-\lambda)(2-\lambda)^2 - 2 + 2(2-\lambda) - 2(1-\lambda)$$

$$= (1-\lambda)(4 - 4\lambda + \lambda^2 - 2) - 2 + 4 - 2\lambda = (1-\lambda)(2 - 4\lambda + \lambda^2) + 2(1-\lambda) = (1-\lambda)(4 - 4\lambda + \lambda^2)$$

$$= (1-\lambda)(2-\lambda)^2 \quad \lambda = 1, 2 \quad (2 \text{は重解})$$

$\lambda = 1$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & -1 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1/2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_1 = C_1 \begin{pmatrix} -1/2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 2$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 & -1 \\ 2 & 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_2 = C_2 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

### (3)

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \text{とするとき}$$

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} 2-\lambda & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 1-\lambda & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 1-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = (2-\lambda)(1-\lambda)^2 - 2(1-\lambda) = (1-\lambda)(2-3\lambda + \lambda^2 - 2)$$

$$= (1-\lambda)(\lambda^2 - 3\lambda) = \lambda(1-\lambda)(\lambda - 3) \quad \lambda = 0, 1, 3$$

$\lambda = 0$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 & -1 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_1 = C_1 \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 1$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_2 = C_3 \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 3$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & -2 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 1 & -2 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_3 = C_3 \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

### (4)

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 & -2 \\ 2 & 3 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \text{とすると}$$

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} 3-\lambda & 2 & -2 \\ 2 & 3-\lambda & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 3-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = (3-\lambda)^3 + 8 + 8 - 12(3-\lambda) = 27 - 27\lambda + 9\lambda^2 - \lambda^3 + 16 - 36 + 12\lambda$$

$$= -\lambda^3 + 9\lambda^2 - 15\lambda + 7 = -(\lambda - 1)(\lambda - 1)(\lambda - 7) \quad \lambda = 1, 7 \text{ （１\text{は重解}）}$$

$\lambda = 1$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 & -2 \\ 2 & 2 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_1 = C_1 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} + C_2 \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 7$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -4 & 2 & -2 \\ 2 & -4 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 & -4 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & -2 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & -2 & -1 \\ -2 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & -3 & -3 \\ 0 & 3 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$\to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_2 = C_3 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$
