### (1)

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} -1-\lambda & 8 \\ -2 & 7-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = -(1+\lambda)(7-\lambda) + 16 = -(7 + 6\lambda - \lambda^2) + 16 = \lambda^2 - 6\lambda + 9 = (\lambda-3)^2 = 0$$

$$\lambda = 3 \text{ （\text{重解}）}$$

$\lambda = 3$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -4 & 8 \\ -2 & 4 \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \quad p_1 = C\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

### (2)

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} -\lambda & -1 \\ 3 & 2-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = -\lambda(2-\lambda) + 3 = \lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 3 \quad \lambda = 1 \pm \sqrt{1-3} = 1 \pm \sqrt{2}i$$

$\lambda = 1+\sqrt{2}i$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -1-\sqrt{2}i & -1 \\ 3 & 1-\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} (1+\sqrt{2}i)(1-\sqrt{2}i) & 1-\sqrt{2}i \\ 3 & 1-\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 1+2 & 1-\sqrt{2}i \\ 3 & 1-\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1-\sqrt{2}i \\ 3 & 1-\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix}$$

$$\rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1-\sqrt{2}i \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$p_1 = C_1 \begin{pmatrix} 1-\sqrt{2}i \\ -3 \end{pmatrix} = C_1 \frac{1}{1+\sqrt{2}i} \begin{pmatrix} (1-\sqrt{2}i)(1+\sqrt{2}i) \\ -3(1+\sqrt{2}i) \end{pmatrix} = C_1 \frac{1}{1+\sqrt{2}i} \begin{pmatrix} 1+2 \\ -3(1+\sqrt{2}i) \end{pmatrix}$$

$$= C_1 \frac{3}{1+\sqrt{2}i} \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ (1+\sqrt{2}i) \end{pmatrix} = C_1 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1+\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 1-\sqrt{2}i$ のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix} -1+\sqrt{2}i & -1 \\ 3 & 1+\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} (1-\sqrt{2}i)(1+\sqrt{2}i) & 1+\sqrt{2}i \\ 3 & 1+\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 1+2 & 1+\sqrt{2}i \\ 3 & 1+\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1+\sqrt{2}i \\ 3 & 1+\sqrt{2}i \end{pmatrix}$$

$$\rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1+\sqrt{2}i \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$p_1 = C_2\left(\frac{1+\sqrt{2i}}{-3}\right) = C_2 \cdot \frac{1}{1-\sqrt{2i}}\left(\frac{(1+\sqrt{2i})(1-\sqrt{2i})}{-3(1-\sqrt{2i})}\right) = C_2 \cdot \frac{1}{1-\sqrt{2i}}\left(\frac{1 - 2i}{-3(1-\sqrt{2i})}\right)$$

$$= C_2 \frac{3}{1-\sqrt{2i}}\left(\frac{-1}{1-\sqrt{2i}}\right) = C_2\left(\frac{-1}{1-\sqrt{2i}}\right)$$
