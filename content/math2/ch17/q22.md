### (1)

$$|A - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} 2-\lambda & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 2-\lambda & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1-\lambda \end{vmatrix} = (2-\lambda)^2(1-\lambda) - 2(2-\lambda) = (2-\lambda)(2-3\lambda+\lambda^2-2)$$

$$= (2-\lambda)(\lambda^2-3\lambda) = \lambda(2-\lambda)(\lambda-3) = 0 \quad \lambda = 0,2,3$$
