### (2)

$$|A-\lambda E|=\begin{vmatrix}3-\lambda & 1 & 1\\1 & 3-\lambda & 1\\1 & 1 & 3-\lambda\end{vmatrix}=(3-\lambda)^3+1+1-3(3-\lambda)=27-27\lambda+9\lambda^2-\lambda^3-7+3\lambda$$

$$=-\lambda^3+9\lambda^2-24\lambda+20=-(\lambda-2)(\lambda-2)(\lambda-5)=0 \quad \lambda=5,2 \quad （２\text{は重解}）$$
