$A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$とすると、与式より$\begin{pmatrix}a_n\\a_{n+1}\end{pmatrix}=A^n\begin{pmatrix}a_1\\a_2\end{pmatrix}$が求められるので

$$|A-\lambda E|=\begin{vmatrix}-\lambda&1\\1&1-\lambda\end{vmatrix}=(-\lambda)(1-\lambda)-1=\lambda^2-\lambda-1$$

$\lambda=\frac{1\pm\sqrt{1+4}}{2}=\frac{1}{2}(1\pm\sqrt{5})$

ここで$\alpha=\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})$、$\beta=\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})$とおくと

$\lambda=\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})$のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix}-\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})&1\\1&\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow\begin{pmatrix}1&-\frac{1}{-\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})}\\1&\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow\begin{pmatrix}1&-\frac{1}{-\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})}\\0&m\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow\begin{pmatrix}1&-\alpha^{-1}\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$$

$m=\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})-\frac{1}{-\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})}=\frac{1-5+4}{2(1+\sqrt{5})}=0$ これから $P_1=C\begin{pmatrix}\alpha^{-1}\\1\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow C\begin{pmatrix}1\\\alpha\end{pmatrix}$

$\lambda=\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})$のとき

$$\begin{pmatrix}-\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})&1\\1&\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow\begin{pmatrix}1&-\frac{1}{-\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})}\\1&\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow\begin{pmatrix}1&-\frac{1}{-\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})}\\0&n\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow\begin{pmatrix}1&-\beta^{-1}\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$$

$n=\frac{1}{2}(1+\sqrt{5})-\frac{1}{-\frac{1}{2}(1-\sqrt{5})}=\frac{1-5+4}{2(1-\sqrt{5})}=0$ これから $P_1=C\begin{pmatrix}\beta^{-1}\\1\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow C\begin{pmatrix}1\\\alpha\end{pmatrix}$

よって$P=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\\alpha&\beta\end{pmatrix}$ $P^{-1}=\frac{1}{\beta-\alpha}\begin{pmatrix}\beta&-1\\-\alpha&1\end{pmatrix}$

$$P^{-1}AP = \begin{pmatrix} \alpha & 0 \\ 0 & \beta \end{pmatrix} \quad P^{-1}A^{n-1}P = \begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1} & 0 \\ 0 & \beta^{n-1} \end{pmatrix} \quad A^{n-1} = P\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1} & 0 \\ 0 & \beta^{n-1} \end{pmatrix}P^{-1}$$

$$A^{n-1} = \frac{1}{\beta - \alpha}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ \alpha & \beta \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1} & 0 \\ 0 & \beta^{n-1} \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \beta & -1 \\ -\alpha & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{\beta - \alpha}\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1} & \beta^{n-1} \\ \alpha^n & \beta^n \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \beta & -1 \\ -\alpha & 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$= \frac{1}{\beta - \alpha}\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1}\beta - \beta^{n-1}\alpha & -\alpha^{n-1} + \beta^{n-1} \\ \alpha^n\beta - \beta^n\alpha & -\alpha^n + \beta^n \end{pmatrix}$$

これから

$$\begin{pmatrix} a_n \\ a_{n+1} \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{\beta - \alpha}\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1}\beta - \beta^{n-1}\alpha & -\alpha^{n-1} + \beta^{n-1} \\ \alpha^n\beta - \beta^n\alpha & -\alpha^n + \beta^n \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} a_1 \\ a_2 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{\beta - \alpha}\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1}\beta - \beta^{n-1}\alpha & -\alpha^{n-1} + \beta^{n-1} \\ \alpha^n\beta - \beta^n\alpha & -\alpha^n + \beta^n \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$= \frac{1}{\beta - \alpha}\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1}\beta - \beta^{n-1}\alpha - \alpha^{n-1} + \beta^{n-1} \\ \alpha^n\beta - \beta^n\alpha - \alpha^n + \beta^n \end{pmatrix}$$

$$= \frac{1}{\beta - \alpha}\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^{n-1}(\beta - 1) + \beta^{n-1}(1 - \alpha) \\ \alpha^n(\beta - 1) + \beta^n(1 - \alpha) \end{pmatrix}$$

ここで $\beta - \alpha = -\sqrt{5}$、$\beta - 1 = -\alpha$、$1 - \alpha = \beta$ から

$$= \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\begin{pmatrix} \alpha^n - \beta^n \\ \alpha^{n+1} - \beta^{n+1} \end{pmatrix}$$

よって $a_n = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}(\alpha^n - \beta^n)$

$$\tau = \lim_{n \to \infty}\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \lim_{n \to \infty}\frac{\alpha^{n+1} - \beta^{n+1}}{\alpha^n - \beta^n} = \lim_{n \to \infty}\frac{\alpha - \frac{\beta^n}{\alpha^n}\beta}{1 - \frac{\beta^n}{\alpha^n}} = \frac{\alpha - 0 \cdot \beta}{1 - 0} = \alpha$$

$(\lim_{n \to \infty}\frac{\beta^n}{\alpha^n}は分母が大きいので n \to \infty では 0 となる)$
