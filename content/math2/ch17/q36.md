### (1)

$$|S_0 - \lambda E| = \begin{vmatrix} \cos\theta - \lambda & 0 & \sin\theta \\ 0 & -\lambda & 0 \\ \sin\theta & 0 & -\cos\theta - \lambda \end{vmatrix} = \lambda(\cos\theta - \lambda)(\cos\theta + \lambda) + \lambda\sin^2\theta$$

$$= \lambda\cos^2\theta - \lambda^3 + \lambda\sin^2\theta = -\lambda^3 + \lambda = -\lambda(\lambda^2 - 1) = 0$$

$$\lambda = 0, \pm 1$$

$\lambda = -1$のとき

$$\left(\begin{array}{ccc} \cos\theta + 1 & 0 & \sin\theta \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin\theta & 0 & -\cos\theta + 1 \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} \cos^2\theta - 1 & 0 & \sin\theta(\cos\theta - 1) \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin^2\theta & 0 & -\sin\theta(\cos\theta - 1) \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} -\sin^2\theta & 0 & \sin\theta(\cos\theta - 1) \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin^2\theta & 0 & -\sin\theta(\cos\theta - 1) \end{array}\right)$$

$$\to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} -\sin^2\theta & 0 & \sin\theta(\cos\theta - 1) \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} 1 & 0 & -\frac{\cos\theta - 1}{\sin\theta} \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right)$$

$$p_1 = C_1 \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\cos\theta - 1}{\sin\theta} \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \left(\begin{array}{c} \cos\theta - 1 \\ 0 \\ \sin\theta \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{c} -2\sin^2\frac{\theta}{2} \\ 0 \\ \sin\theta \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{c} -\sin\frac{\theta}{2} \\ 0 \\ 2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2} \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{c} -\sin\frac{\theta}{2} \\ 0 \\ 2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2} \end{array}\right)$$

$$\lambda = 0$$ のとき

$$\left(\begin{array}{ccc} \cos\theta & 0 & \sin\theta \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ \sin\theta & 0 & -\cos\theta \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} \cos\theta\sin\theta & 0 & \sin^2\theta \\ \cos\theta\sin\theta & 0 & -\cos^2\theta \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} \cos\theta\sin\theta & 0 & \sin^2\theta \\ 0 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} 1 & 0 & \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right)$$

$$p_2 = C_2 \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 1$ のとき

$$\left(\begin{array}{ccc} \cos\theta - 1 & 0 & \sin\theta \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin\theta & 0 & -\cos\theta - 1 \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} \cos^2\theta - 1 & 0 & \sin\theta(\cos\theta + 1) \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin^2\theta & 0 & -\sin\theta(\cos\theta + 1) \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} -\sin^2\theta & 0 & \sin\theta(\cos\theta + 1) \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin^2\theta & 0 & -\sin\theta(\cos\theta + 1) \end{array}\right)$$

$$\to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} -\sin^2\theta & 0 & \sin\theta(\cos\theta + 1) \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{ccc} 1 & 0 & -\frac{\cos\theta + 1}{\sin\theta} \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right)$$

$$p_1 = C_1 \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\cos\theta + 1}{\sin\theta} \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \to \left(\begin{array}{c} \cos\theta + 1 \\ 0 \\ \sin\theta \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{c} 2\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2} \\ 0 \\ \sin\theta \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{c} \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \\ 0 \\ 2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2} \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{c} \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \\ 0 \\ 2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2} \end{array}\right) \to \left(\begin{array}{c} \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \\ 0 \\ \sin\frac{\theta}{2} \end{array}\right)$$

### (2)

$$P = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\frac{\theta}{2} & -\sin\frac{\theta}{2} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ \sin\frac{\theta}{2} & \cos\frac{\theta}{2} & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

とするとこれは直交行列であるので ${}^t P = P^{-1}$

$v = Pv'$ となるような $v'$ を考えると ${}^t P v = v'$

$$v' = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\frac{\theta}{2} & 0 & \sin\frac{\theta}{2} \\ -\sin\frac{\theta}{2} & 0 & \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} \\ -x \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \\ y \end{pmatrix}$$

$${}^t S_\theta v = {}^t v' {}^t S_\theta P v' = {}^t v' \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} v' = \begin{pmatrix} x \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} \\ -x \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \\ y \end{pmatrix}^{t} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} \\ -x \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \\ y \end{pmatrix}$$

$$= \left( x \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} \quad x \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} - z \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \quad 0 \right) \begin{pmatrix} x \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} \\ -x \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \\ y \end{pmatrix}$$

$$= \left( x \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} + z \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} \right)^2 - \left( x \cdot \sin\frac{\theta}{2} - z \cdot \cos\frac{\theta}{2} \right)^2 = x^2 \left( \cos^2\frac{\theta}{2} - \sin^2\frac{\theta}{2} \right) + 4xz \sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2} + z^2 \left( \sin^2\frac{\theta}{2} - \cos^2\frac{\theta}{2} \right)$$

$$= x^2 \cdot \cos\theta + 2xz \cdot \sin\theta - z^2 \cdot \cos\theta = c$$

問題集 p.180 のように $v' = (x' \quad y' \quad z')$ と考えると上式は

$$x'^2 - y'^2 = c$$

これは双曲柱面を表す。

(2) と同様に考えると、問題集 p.181 より $v = Pv' = P \begin{pmatrix} x' \\ y' \\ z' \end{pmatrix}$ とおくと

$$v' = P^{-1}v = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ -\frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}(x + y + z) \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(y + z) \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}(-2x + y + z) \end{pmatrix}$$

$$S = \sqrt{v}PAP v' + 2 = \sqrt{v}\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}v' + 2 = -2x'^2 + y'^2 + z'^2 + 2 = 0$$

$v = p_1$ とすると、$v$ に直交する平面は問題集 p.66 の平面の方程式から考えて、法線ベクトルが $p_1$ となるので

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}x + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}y + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}z + d = 0$$

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}x + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}y + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}z = d$$

$x' = d$ となり、これを $S$ の式に代入すると

$$y'^2 + z'^2 = 2a^2 - 2$$ の円となる
