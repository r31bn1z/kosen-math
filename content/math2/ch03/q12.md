### (1)

$$S = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$$

$$S' = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 2r \cdot \theta = r\theta$$

### (2)

$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$$

$$V' = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2$$

### (3)

$$v = \frac{Q}{2\pi R^2}$$

$$v' = \frac{1}{2\pi R^2}$$

### (4)

$$u = U\left(1 - \frac{r^2}{R^2}\right) = U - U\frac{r^2}{R^2}$$

$$u' = -U\frac{2r}{R^2} = -\frac{2Ur}{R^2}$$
