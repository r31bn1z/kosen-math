### (1)

$$\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{5x^3-8x^2}{3x^4-16x^2}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{5x-8}{3x^2-16}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{-8}{-16}=\frac{1}{2}$$

### (2)

$$\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{3x^3-2x^2}{x^2-x}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{3x^2-2x}{x-1}=0$$

### (3)

$$\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{1}{x}\left\{1-\frac{1}{(1+x)^3}\right\}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{1}{x}\left\{\frac{1+3x+3x^2+x^3-1}{(1+x)^3}\right\}=\frac{1}{x}\left\{\frac{3x+3x^2+x^3}{(1+x)^3}\right\}=\frac{3+3x+x^2}{(1+x)^3}=3$$

### (4)

$$\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{1}{x}\left\{\frac{1}{(x-2)^2}-\frac{1}{4}\right\}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{1}{x}\left\{\frac{4-x^2+4x-4}{4(x-2)^2}\right\}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{-x+4}{4(x-2)^2}=\frac{1}{4}$$
