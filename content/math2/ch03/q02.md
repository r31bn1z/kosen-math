### (1)

$$\text{変化率} = \frac{(3 \cdot 1 + 2) - (3 \cdot (-2) + 2)}{1 - (-2)} = \frac{5+4}{3} = 3$$

### (2)

$$\text{変化率} = \frac{(9+1) - (1+1)}{3-1} = \frac{10-2}{2} = 4$$

### (3)

$$\text{変化率} = \frac{b^3 - a^3}{b-a} = \frac{(b-a)(b^2+ab+a^2)}{b-a} = b^2 + ab + a^2$$

### (4)
変化率

$$= \frac{\{(a+h)^2+2(a+h)-3\} - (a^2+2a-3)}{a+h-a} = \frac{a^2+2ah+h^2+2a+2h-3-a^2-2a+3}{h} = 2a+h+2$$
