### (1)
$$f'(x) = \frac{-3(x+\Delta x)+4+3x-4}{\Delta x} = -3$$

### (2)
$$f'(x) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{(x+\Delta x)^2 - 2(x+\Delta x) - 3 - x^2 + 2x + 3}{\Delta x} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{x^2 + 2x\Delta x + \Delta x^2 - 2x - 2\Delta x - 3 - x^2 + 2x + 3}{\Delta x}$$

$$= 2x - 2$$
