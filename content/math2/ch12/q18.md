### (1)
$$\vec{a} = \vec{e_1} + 3\vec{e_2}$$

$$\vec{b} = 2\vec{e_1} + t\vec{e_2}$$

$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 0$$

$$(\vec{e_1} + 3\vec{e_2})(2\vec{e_1} + t\vec{e_2}) = 0$$

$$2|\vec{e_1}|^2 + 3t|\vec{e_2}|^2 = 0$$

$$2 + 3t = 0$$

$$t = -\frac{2}{3}$$

### (2)
$$\vec{a} = (1,1) \qquad \vec{b} = (1,-\sqrt{3})$$

$$(t\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \cdot \vec{a} = 0$$

$$(1+t, -\sqrt{3}+t) \cdot (1,1) = 0$$

$$1 + t - \sqrt{3} + t = 0$$

$$t = \frac{\sqrt{3} - 1}{2}$$
