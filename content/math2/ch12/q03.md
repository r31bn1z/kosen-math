### (1)
$$c = a + tb = (1,2) + t(1,-1) = (1+t, 2-t)$$

### (2)
$$|c| = 3 = \sqrt{(1+t)^2 + (2-t)^2} = \sqrt{1+2t+t^2+4-4t+t^2} = \sqrt{5-2t+2t^2}$$

$$9 = 5-2t+2t^2 \qquad 2t^2-2t-4 = 2(t^2-t-2) = 2(t-2)(t+1) = 0 \qquad t = 2,-1$$

### (3)
$c \parallel d$ なので $c = kd$ となる

$$\begin{cases} 1+t = k4 & ① \\ 2-t = -k & ② \end{cases}$$

①+②から $k = 1$ これから $t = 3$
