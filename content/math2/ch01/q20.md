### (1)

$$\sum_{k=1}^{n}(2k-1)(2k+2) = \sum_{k=1}^{n}(4k^2+2k-2) = 4\sum_{k=1}^{n}k^2 + 2\sum_{k=1}^{n}k - 2\sum_{k=1}^{n}1$$

$$= 4\cdot\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6} + 2\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2} - 2n = \frac{1}{3}n\left\{2(n+1)\left(2n+1+\frac{3}{2}\right) - 6\right\}$$

$$= \frac{1}{3}n(4n^2+5n+4n+5-6) = \frac{n(4n^2+9n-1)}{3}$$

### (2)

$$\sum_{k=1}^{n}\sum_{i=1}^{k} i = \sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{k(k+1)}{2} = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{n}k^2 + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{n}k = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6} + \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

$$= \frac{1}{12}\cdot n(n+1)(2n+1+3) = \frac{1}{6}\cdot n(n+1)(n+2)$$

### (3)

$$\sum_{k=1}^{n}(2k-1)\cdot 2k \cdot (2k+1) = 8\sum_{k=1}^{n}k^3 - 2\sum_{k=1}^{n}k = 8\cdot\frac{n^2(n+1)^2}{4} - 2\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

$$= n(n+1)\{2n(n+1)-1\} = n(n+1)(2n^2+2n-1)$$
