### (1)
数列を計算すると

$$a_n \to 3, 2, \frac{5}{3}, \frac{14}{9}, \cdots$$

$$b_n \to -1, -\frac{1}{3}, -\frac{1}{9}, \cdots \qquad \therefore b_n = -\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n-1}$$

よって数列$\{a_n\}$の一般項は

$$a_n = a_1 + \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} b_k = 3 + \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} -\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{k-1} = 3 - \frac{1-\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n-1}}{1-\frac{1}{3}} = 3 - \frac{1-\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n-1}}{\frac{2}{3}} = \frac{3}{2}\left\{1+\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n-1}\right\}$$

### (2)

$$3a_{n+1} = a_n + 3 \qquad a_{n+1} = \frac{1}{3}a_n + 1 \qquad a_{n+1} - \frac{3}{2} = \frac{1}{3}\left(a_n - \frac{3}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{3}b_n \qquad b_{n+1} = \frac{1}{3}b_n$$

数列$b_n$は、初項$b_1 = 3 - \frac{3}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$、公比$\frac{1}{3}$の等比数列だから

$$b_n = \frac{3}{2}\cdot\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n-1} \qquad a_n - \frac{3}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\cdot\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n-1} \qquad \therefore a_n = \frac{3}{2}\cdot\left\{1+\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n-1}\right\}$$
