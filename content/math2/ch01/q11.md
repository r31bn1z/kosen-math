### (1)

$$\sum_{k=1}^n (2k)^2 = 4\sum_{k=1}^n k^2 = 4\cdot\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6} = \frac{2n(n+1)(2n+1)}{3}$$

### (2)

$$\sum_{k=1}^n k(k+2) = \sum_{k=1}^n k^2 + 2\sum_{k=1}^n k = \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6} + 2\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2} = \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)+6n(n+1)}{6}$$

$$= \frac{n(n+1)(2n+7)}{6}$$

### (3)

$$\sum_{k=1}^n k(k+1)(k+2) = \sum_{k=1}^n k^3+3k^2+2k = \sum_{k=1}^n k^3 + 3\sum_{k=1}^n k^2 + 2\sum_{k=1}^n k$$

$$= \frac{n^2(n+1)^2}{4} + 3\cdot\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6} + 2\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

$$= \frac{1}{4}n(n+1)\{n(n+1)+2(2n+1)+4\} = \frac{1}{4}n(n+1)(n^2+5n+6)$$

$$= \frac{n(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)}{4}$$

### (4)

$$\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{k(k+2)} = \sum_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{k}-\frac{1}{k+2}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{5}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+2}\right)$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{n+1}-\frac{1}{n+2}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2(n+1)(n+2)+(n+1)(n+2)-2(n+2)-2(n+1)}{2(n+1)(n+2)}\right)$$

$$= \frac{n(3n+5)}{4(n+1)(n+2)}$$
