### (1)
$$y = x^x$$

両辺の対数をとると

$$\log y = x \cdot \log x$$

$$\frac{y'}{y} = \log x + x \cdot \frac{1}{x} = \log x + 1 \qquad y' = y(\log x + 1) = x^x(\log x + 1)$$

### (2)
$$y = 3^{2x}$$

両辺の対数を取ると

$$\log y = 2x \cdot \log 3$$

$$\frac{y'}{y} = 2 \cdot \log 3 \qquad y' = 2y \cdot \log 3 \qquad y' = 2 \cdot 3^{2x} \cdot \log 3$$
