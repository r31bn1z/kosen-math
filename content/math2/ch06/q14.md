### (1)
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{2n}\right)^n$$

2n = t とおくと

$$=\lim_{n\to\pm\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}t} = e^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{e}$$

### (2)
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1-\frac{2}{n}\right)^n$$

$-\dfrac{n}{2} = t$ とおくと

$$=\lim_{n\to\pm\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{-2t} = e^{-2} = \frac{1}{e^2}$$

### (3)
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)^n$$

$-(n+1) = t$ とおくと

$$=\lim_{n\to\pm\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{-t-1} = \lim_{n\to\pm\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{-t} \times \lim_{n\to\pm\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{t}\right)^{-1} = e^{-1} \times (1+0)^{-1} = \frac{1}{e}$$
