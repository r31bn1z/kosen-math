### (1)
$$y'=\frac{(2x-3)'}{2x-3}=\frac{2}{2x-3}$$

### (2)
$$y'=\frac{(x^2+2x+2)'}{x^2+2x+2}=\frac{2x+2}{x^2+2x+2}$$

### (3)
$$y'=\frac{(\sqrt{1-x^2})'}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\frac{\dfrac{-2x}{2\sqrt{1-x^2}}}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\frac{-x}{1-x^2}=\frac{x}{x^2-1}$$

### (4)
$$y'=2x\cdot\log x+x^2\cdot\frac{1}{x}=2x\log x+x$$

### (5)
$$y'=\frac{0-\left(1+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)}{(x+\log x)^2}=-\frac{\dfrac{x+1}{x}}{(x+\log x)^2}=\frac{x+1}{x(x+\log x)^2}$$

### (6)
$$y'=\log(x^2+1)+x\cdot\frac{2x}{x^2+1}=\log(x^2+1)+\frac{2x^2}{x^2+1}$$
