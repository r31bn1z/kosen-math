### (1)

$y=0$ のとき $x^2+1=4$  $x^2=3$  $x=\pm\sqrt{3}$

$$V=2\pi\int_0^{\sqrt{3}}(\sqrt{4-x^2}-1)^2\,dx=2\pi\int_0^{\sqrt{3}}(4-x^2-2\sqrt{4-x^2}+1)dx=2\pi\int_0^{\sqrt{3}}(5-x^2)dx-4\pi\int_0^{\sqrt{3}}\sqrt{4-x^2}\,dx$$

第２項で $x=2\sin t$ とおくと $dx=2\cos t\,dt$  $x:0\to\sqrt{3}$  $t:0\to\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ これから

$$=2\pi\left[5x-\frac{1}{3}x^3\right]_0^{\sqrt{3}}-4\pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\sqrt{4-4\sin^2 t}\cdot 2\cos t\,dt=2\pi(5\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{3})-16\pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\cos^2 t\,dt$$

$$=8\pi\sqrt{3}-8\pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{3}}(1+\cos 2t)dt=8\pi\sqrt{3}-8\pi\left[t+\frac{1}{2}\sin 2t\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{3}}=8\pi\sqrt{3}-8\pi\left(\frac{\pi}{3}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)=6\sqrt{3}\pi-\frac{8}{3}\pi^2$$

### (2)

$$2\pi\int_0^2\left\{\left(\sqrt{1-\frac{x^2}{4}}+1\right)^2-\left(-\sqrt{1-\frac{x^2}{4}}+1\right)^2\right\}dx$$

$x=2\sin t$ とおくと $dx=2\cos t\,dt$  $x:0\to 2$  $t:0\to\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ これから

$$=2\pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\{(\cos t+1)^2-(-\cos t+1)^2\}\cdot 2\cos t\,dt=4\pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(\cos^2 t+2\cos t+1-\cos^2 t+2\cos t-1)\cos t\,dt$$

$$=16\pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\cos^2 t\,dt=16\pi\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=4\pi^2$$

### (3)

$$V=\pi\int_0^e(1)^2dx-\pi\int_1^e(\log x)^2dx=\pi[x]_0^e-\pi\left\{[\log x\cdot(x\log x-x)]_1^e-\int_1^e\frac{1}{x}(x\log x-x)dx\right\}$$

$$=e\pi-\pi\{(e-e)-\int_1^e(\log x-1)dx\}=e\pi+\pi[x\log x-x-x]_1^e=e\pi+\pi(e-e-e+1+1)=2\pi$$

### (4)

$$V=\pi\int_{a-r}^{a+r}(\sqrt{r^2-(x-a)^2}+b)^2-(-\sqrt{r^2-(x-a)^2}+b)^2\,dx$$

$x-a=r\sin t$ とおくと $dx=r\cos t$  $x:a-r\to a+r$  $t:-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\to\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ これから

$$= \pi\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \{(r\cos t+b)^2-(-r\cos t+b)^2\}r\cos t\,dt$$

$$= \pi\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} (r^2\cos^2 t+2rb\cos t+b^2-r^2\cos^2 t+2rb\cos t-b^2)r\cos t\,dt = \pi\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 4rb\cos t\cdot r\cos t\,dt$$

$$= 4\pi r^2 b\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\cos^2 t\,dt = 2\pi r^2 b\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(1+\cos 2t)dt = 2\pi r^2 b\left[t+\frac{1}{2}\cos 2t\right]_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = 2\pi r^2 b\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 2\pi^2 r^2 b$$
