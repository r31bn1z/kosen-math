$x = \sqrt{y}$ 高さを $h$ とすると

$$V = \pi\int_0^h (\sqrt{y})^2\,dy = \pi\left[\frac{1}{2}y^2\right]_0^h = \frac{\pi h^2}{2}$$

$$\frac{dV}{dt} = 8$$ より

$$V = \frac{\pi h^2}{2} = 8t \qquad h = \sqrt{\frac{16t}{\pi}}$$

$$h' = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{16}{\pi t}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi t}} \text{ cm/sec}$$
