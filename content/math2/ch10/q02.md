### (1)

$$\int_{-1}^{2}(x^2-2x+4)dx=\left[\frac{1}{3}x^3-x^2+4x\right]_{-1}^{2}=\frac{8}{3}-4+8+\frac{1}{3}+1+4=12$$

### (2)

$y=0$ のとき $x^2(x-1)=0$ $x=0,1$

$$\int_{0}^{1}(x^3-x^2)dx=\left[\frac{1}{4}x^4-\frac{1}{3}x^3\right]_{0}^{1}=\frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{3}=-\frac{1}{12}$$ 面積なので $\dfrac{1}{12}$

### (3)

$1-x=2x^2$  $2x^2+x-1=(2x-1)(x+1)=0$  $x=-1,\dfrac{1}{2}$

$$\int_{-1}^{\frac{1}{2}}(1-x)dx-\int_{-1}^{\frac{1}{2}}2x^2dx=\left[x-\frac{1}{2}x^2\right]_{-1}^{\frac{1}{2}}-\left[\frac{2}{3}x^3\right]_{-1}^{\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{8}+1+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{12}-\frac{2}{3}=\frac{27}{24}=\frac{9}{8}$$

### (4)

$y=0$ のとき $x(x^2-1)=x(x-1)(x+1)=0$  $x=0,-1,1$

$$\int_{-1}^{0}x(x^2-1)dx-\int_{0}^{1}x(x^2-1)dx=\int_{-1}^{0}(x^3-x)dx-\int_{0}^{1}(x^3-x)dx=\left[\frac{1}{4}x^4-\frac{1}{2}x^2\right]_{-1}^{0}-\left[\frac{1}{4}x^4-\frac{1}{2}x^2\right]_{0}^{1}$$

$$=-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{2}$$

### (5)

$y=0$ のとき $x=0$

$$\int_{0}^{2}\frac{x}{x^2+4}dx$$  $x^2=t$ とおくと $2xdx=dt$  $x:0\to2$  $t:0\to4$  これから

$$\int_{0}^{4}\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{t+4}dt=\frac{1}{2}\left[\log|t+4|\right]_{0}^{4}=\frac{1}{2}(\log8-\log4)=\frac{1}{2}\log2$$

### (6)

$$y = (1 - \sqrt{x})^2$$

定義域は $x, y > 0$  $y = 0$ のとき $x = 1$ これから

$$\int_0^1 (1 - \sqrt{x})^2\, dx = \int_0^1 (1 - 2\sqrt{x} + x)\, dx = \left[ x - \frac{4}{3}\sqrt{x^3} + \frac{1}{2}x^2 \right]_0^1 = 1 - \frac{4}{3} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{6}$$
