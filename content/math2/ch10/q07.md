x軸で回転

$y = 0$ のとき  $0 = 1 - \sqrt{x}$  $x = 1$  これから

$$V = \pi\int_0^1 (1-\sqrt{x})^2\,dx = \pi\int_0^1 (1-2\sqrt{x}+x)\,dx = \pi\left[x - \frac{4}{3}\sqrt{x^3} + \frac{1}{2}x^2\right]_0^1 = \pi\left(1 - \frac{4}{3} + \frac{1}{2}\right)$$

$$= \pi\left(\frac{6-8+3}{6}\right) = \frac{\pi}{6}$$

y軸で回転

$x = 0$ のとき  $y = 1$  これから

$$V = \pi\int_0^1 (1-y)^4\,dy = \pi\left[-\frac{1}{5}(1-y)^5\right]_0^1 = \pi \cdot \frac{1}{5} = \frac{\pi}{5}$$
