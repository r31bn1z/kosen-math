### (1)

$X = A^{-1}B$

$$\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 0 & 1 \\ -2 & 4 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 24 - 2 - 8 - 9 = 5$$

$$\begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 4 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = -4 \quad \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ -2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 9 + 2 = 11 \quad \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ -2 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 12$$

$$\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 4 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 3 - 8 = -5 \quad \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ -2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 6 + 4 = 10 \quad \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -2 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 8 + 2 = 10$$

$$\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1 \quad \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 2 - 6 = -4 \quad \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 3 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = -3$$

$$A^{-1} = \frac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix} -4 & 5 & 1 \\ -11 & 10 & 4 \\ 12 & -10 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$A^{-1}B = \frac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix} -4 & 5 & 1 \\ -11 & 10 & 4 \\ 12 & -10 & -3 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 5 & -3 \\ -1 & 10 & -7 \\ 2 & -10 & 9 \end{pmatrix}$$
