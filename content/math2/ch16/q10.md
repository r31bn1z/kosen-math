$$\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 4 - 1 = 3$$

$$\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 4 - 1 = 3 \quad - \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 0 \quad - \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 0$$

$$- \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 2 \quad \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 2 \quad - \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -1$$

$$\begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -4 \quad - \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -1 \quad \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 2$$

これから

$$\frac{1}{3}\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 & 0 \\ 2 & 2 & -1 \\ -4 & -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$$
