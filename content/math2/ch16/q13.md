### (1)

$$\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 2 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 2\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 & 0 \\ -1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} + (-1)\begin{vmatrix} -1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & 2 \end{vmatrix}$$

$$= 2(8 - 2 - 2) + (-4 + 1) = 8 - 3 = 5$$

### (2)

$$\begin{vmatrix} 9 & 5 & 4 & 3 \\ 4 & 18 & 2 & 8 \\ 6 & -4 & 3 & -2 \\ 9 & 13 & 4 & 7 \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 9 & 5 & 4 & 3 \\ 4 & 18 & 2 & 8 \\ 6 & -4 & 3 & -2 \\ 0 & 8 & 0 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 4\begin{vmatrix} 9 & 5 & 4 & 3 \\ 4 & 18 & 2 & 8 \\ 6 & -4 & 3 & -2 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix}$$

$$= 4\begin{vmatrix} 9 & -1 & 4 & 0 \\ 4 & 2 & 2 & 0 \\ 6 & 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix}$$

$$= 12\begin{vmatrix} 9 & -1 & 4 \\ 4 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 12\begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 12\begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 24$$
