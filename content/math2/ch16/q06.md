### (1)

$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & -2 & -3 & 7 \\ 3 & -4 & 1 & 4 \end{array}\right)
\to
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & -6 & -7 & 1 \\ 0 & -10 & -5 & -5 \end{array}\right)
\to
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & -6 & -7 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right)
\to
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -2 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 8 & 0 & 8 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right)
$$

$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -2 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right)
\to
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 \end{array}\right)
$$

これから $(x,y,z)=(3,1,-1)$

### (4)

$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 0 & 5 & 2 & 3 \\ 3 & 0 & 4 & 20 \\ 1 & -2 & 0 & 4 \end{array}\right)
\to
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -2 & 0 & 4 \\ 3 & 0 & 4 & 20 \\ 0 & 5 & 2 & 3 \end{array}\right)
\to
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -2 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & 6 & 4 & 8 \\ 0 & 5 & 2 & 3 \end{array}\right)
\to
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -2 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & -4 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 5 & 2 & 3 \end{array}\right)
$$

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 0 & \Big| & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & \Big| & -\dfrac{1}{2} \\ 0 & 5 & 2 & \Big| & 3 \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & \Big| & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & \Big| & -\dfrac{1}{2} \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & \Big| & \dfrac{11}{2} \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & \Big| & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & \Big| & -\dfrac{1}{2} \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & \Big| & \dfrac{11}{4} \end{pmatrix} \quad \text{これから} \quad (x,y,z) = \left(3, -\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{11}{4}\right)$$
