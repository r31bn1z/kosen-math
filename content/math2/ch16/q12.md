### (1)

$$\begin{vmatrix} 1+a & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1+b & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1+c \end{vmatrix}$$

$$= (1+a)(1+b)(1+c) + 1 + 1 - (1+a) - (1+b) - (1+c)$$

$$= 1 + ab + bc + ca + a + b + c + abc + 2 - 3 - a - b - c$$

$$= ab + bc + ca + abc$$

$$= abc\left(1 + \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}\right)$$

### (2)

$$\begin{vmatrix} 1+x^2 & xy & xz \\ yx & 1+y^2 & yz \\ zx & zy & 1+z^2 \end{vmatrix}$$

$$= (1+x^2)(1+y^2)(1+z^2) + x^2y^2z^2 + x^2y^2z^2 - y^2z^2(1+x^2) - x^2z^2(1+y^2) - x^2y^2(1+z^2)$$

$$= 1 + x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + x^2y^2 + y^2z^2 + z^2x^2 + x^2y^2z^2 + 2x^2y^2z^2 - y^2z^2 - x^2y^2z^2 - x^2z^2 - x^2y^2z^2 - x^2y^2 - x^2y^2z^2 - x^2y^2 - x^2y^2z^2$$

$$= 1 + x^2 + y^2 + z^2$$
