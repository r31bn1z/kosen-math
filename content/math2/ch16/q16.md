### (1)

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
a+b & b & b & \cdots & b \\
b & a+b & b & \cdots & b \\
b & b & a+b & \cdots & b \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
b & b & b & \cdots & a+b
\end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix}
a+nb & b & b & \cdots & b \\
a+nb & a+b & b & \cdots & b \\
a+nb & b & a+b & \cdots & b \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
a+nb & b & b & \cdots & a+b
\end{vmatrix}
$$

$$
= (a+nb) \begin{vmatrix}
1 & b & b & \cdots & b \\
1 & a+b & b & \cdots & b \\
1 & b & a+b & \cdots & b \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
1 & b & b & \cdots & a+b
\end{vmatrix} = (a+nb) \begin{vmatrix}
0 & b & b & \cdots & b \\
0 & a & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & 0 & a & \cdots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & a
\end{vmatrix} = a^{n-1}(a+nb)
$$

### (2)

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 & \cdots & n \\
-1 & 0 & 3 & \cdots & n \\
-1 & -2 & 0 & \cdots & n \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
-1 & -2 & -3 & \cdots & 0
\end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 & \cdots & n \\
0 & 2 & 6 & \cdots & 2n \\
0 & 0 & 3 & \cdots & 2n \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & n
\end{vmatrix} = n!
$$
