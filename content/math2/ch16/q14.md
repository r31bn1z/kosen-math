### (1)

$$\begin{vmatrix} 1 & \omega & \omega^2 \\ \omega & \omega^2 & 1 \\ \omega^2 & 1 & \omega \end{vmatrix} = \omega^3 + \omega^3 + \omega^3 - \omega^6 - 1 - \omega^3 = 2\omega^3 - \omega^6 - 1 = 2 - 1 - 1 = 0$$

### (2)

$$\begin{vmatrix} x & -1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & x & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & x & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & x \end{vmatrix} = x\begin{vmatrix} x & -1 & 0 \\ 1 & x & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & x \end{vmatrix} - \begin{vmatrix} -1 & 0 & 0 \\ x & x & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & x \end{vmatrix}$$

$$= x(x^3 + x + x) - (-x^2 - 1) = x^4 + 2x^2 + x^2 + 1 = x^4 + 3x^2 + 1$$
