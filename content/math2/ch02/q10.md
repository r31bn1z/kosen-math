### (1)

$$\frac{1+2+3+\cdots+n}{n^2} = \frac{\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}}{n^2} = \frac{n+1}{2n}$$

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{n+1}{2n} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1+\dfrac{1}{n}}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$$

### (2)

$$\frac{1^2+2^2+3^2+\cdots n^2}{n^3} = \frac{\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}}{n^3} = \frac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{6n^2}$$

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{6n^2} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\left(1+\dfrac{1}{n}\right)\left(2+\dfrac{1}{n}\right)}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$$

### (3)

$$\frac{1}{1\cdot3} + \frac{1}{3\cdot5} + \frac{1}{5\cdot7} + \cdots + \frac{1}{(2n-1)\cdot(2n+1)} =$$

$$\frac{1}{(2n-1)\cdot(2n+1)} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2n-1} - \frac{1}{2n+1}\right) \text{より}$$

$$\sum_{n=1}^{n} \frac{1}{(2n-1)\cdot(2n+1)} = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=1}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{2n-1} - \frac{1}{2n+1}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{1} - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{5} + \cdots + \frac{1}{2n-1} - \frac{1}{2n+1}\right)$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{2n+1}\right) = \frac{n}{2n+1}$$

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{n}{2n+1} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{2+\dfrac{1}{n}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

### (4)
分子は$a=1$ $r=1$の等比級数なので

$$\frac{S_n}{2^{n-1}} = \frac{\dfrac{2^n-1}{2-1}}{2^{n-1}} = \frac{2^n-1}{2^{n-1}} = 2 - \frac{1}{2^{n-1}} \qquad \lim_{n \to \infty}\left(2 - \frac{1}{2^{n-1}}\right) = 2$$
