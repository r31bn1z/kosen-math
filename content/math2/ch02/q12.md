### (1)
$$S_n = \sum_{n=1}^{2n-1} n^2 - \sum_{n=1}^{n-1} n^2 = \frac{(2n-1)2n(4n-1)}{6} - \frac{(n-1)n(2n-1)}{6} = \frac{n}{6}\{2(2n-1)(4n-1) - (n-1)(2n-1)\}$$

$$= \frac{n}{6}(16n^2 - 12n + 2 - 2n^2 + 3n - 1) = \frac{n(2n-1)(7n-1)}{6}$$

### (2)
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{S_n}{n^3} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\dfrac{n(2n-1)(7n-1)}{6}}{n^3} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{n(2n-1)(7n-1)}{6n^3} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\left(2 - \dfrac{1}{n}\right)\left(7 - \dfrac{1}{n}\right)}{6} = \frac{7}{3}$$
