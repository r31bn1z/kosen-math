### (1)

$$a_1 = 5 \qquad a_2 = 3\sqrt{5} \qquad a_3 = 3\cdot\sqrt{3}\cdot 5^{\frac{1}{4}} = 3^{\frac{3}{2}}\cdot 5^{\frac{1}{4}} \qquad a_4 = 3\cdot 3^{\frac{3}{4}}\cdot 5^{\frac{1}{8}} = 3^{\frac{7}{4}}\cdot 5^{\frac{1}{8}}$$

$$a_5 = 3\cdot 3^{\frac{7}{8}}\cdot 5^{\frac{1}{16}} = 3^{\frac{15}{8}}\cdot 5^{\frac{1}{16}}$$

$$a_n = 3^{\alpha}\cdot 5^{\beta} \qquad \alpha = 2 - \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n-2} \qquad \beta = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n-1}$$

### (2)

$\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty} 3^{\alpha}\cdot 5^{\beta}$ において

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\alpha = \lim_{n\to\infty}\left\{2 - \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n-2}\right\} = 2 - 0 = 2 \qquad \lim_{n\to\infty}\beta = \lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n-1} = 0 \quad \text{これから}$$

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} 3^{\alpha}\cdot 5^{\beta} = 3^2 \cdot 5^0 = 9$$
