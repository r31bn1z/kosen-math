### (1)
初項 $a = 1$ 公比 $r = 0.2$ から収束

$$S_n = \frac{a}{1-r} = \frac{1}{1-0.2} = \frac{5}{4}$$

### (2)
$a = 1$ $r = -\sqrt{3}$ $|r| > 1$ より発散

### (3)
$a = 6$ $r = -\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ から収束

$$S_n = \frac{6}{1-\left(-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)} = \frac{6\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}+1} = \frac{18-6\sqrt{3}}{3-1} = 9-3\sqrt{3}$$

### (4)
$a = \sqrt{2}+1$ $r = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}-1}{\sqrt{2}+1} = 3-2\sqrt{2}$

$$S_n = \frac{\sqrt{2}+1}{1-(3-2\sqrt{2})} = \frac{\sqrt{2}+1}{2(\sqrt{2}-1)} = \frac{(\sqrt{2}+1)^2}{2(\sqrt{2}-1)(\sqrt{2}+1)} = \frac{3+2\sqrt{2}}{2} = \sqrt{2}+\frac{3}{2}$$
