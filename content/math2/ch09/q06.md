### (1)

$$\int_{-1}^{1} \frac{dx}{2-x^2} = \int_{-1}^{1} \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}-x}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}+x}\right)dx = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\left[-\log|\sqrt{2}-x|+\log|\sqrt{2}+x|\right]_{-1}^{1} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\left[\log\frac{|\sqrt{2}+x|}{|\sqrt{2}-x|}\right]_{-1}^{1}$$

$$= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\left(\log\frac{|\sqrt{2}+1|}{|\sqrt{2}-1|}-\log\frac{|\sqrt{2}-x|}{|\sqrt{2}+x|}\right) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\left(\log\left(\frac{|\sqrt{2}+1|}{|\sqrt{2}-1|}\right)^{2}\right) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\log\frac{|\sqrt{2}+1|}{|\sqrt{2}-1|} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\log\frac{(\sqrt{2}+1)(\sqrt{2}+1)}{2-1}$$

$$= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\log(\sqrt{2}+1)^{2} = \sqrt{2}\log(\sqrt{2}+1)$$

### (2)

$$\int_{1}^{9}\frac{(1-\sqrt{x})^{2}}{x}dx = \int_{1}^{9}\frac{1-2\sqrt{x}+x}{x}dx = \int_{1}^{9}\left(\frac{1}{x}-\frac{2}{\sqrt{x}}+1\right)dx = \left[\log x - 4\sqrt{x}+x\right]_{1}^{9}$$

$$= \log 9 - 4\sqrt{9}+9-\log 1+4-1 = \log 9 = 2\log 3$$

### (3)

$$\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\,dx = \left[x\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\right]_{-1}^{1} - \int_{-1}^{1}\frac{x^{2}}{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}dx = 2\sqrt{2} - \int_{-1}^{1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}+\frac{1+x^{2}}{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}\right)dx$$

$$2\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\,dx = 2\sqrt{2} - \int_{-1}^{1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}dx = 2\sqrt{2} - \left[\log\left|x+\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\right|\right]_{-1}^{1} = 2\sqrt{2} - \left(\log\frac{|1+\sqrt{2}|}{|-1+\sqrt{2}|}\right)$$

$$\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\,dx = \sqrt{2} - \frac{1}{2}\log\left(\frac{(1+\sqrt{2})^{2}}{-1+2}\right) = \sqrt{2} - \log(1+\sqrt{2})$$

### (4)

$$\int_{1}^{2}(\log x)^{2}dx = \left[x(\log x)^{2}\right]_{1}^{2} - \int_{1}^{2}x\cdot 2\log x\cdot\frac{1}{x}dx = 2(\log 2)^{2}-2\int_{1}^{2}\log x\,dx$$

$$= 2(\log 2)^{2}-2\left\{\left[x\cdot\log x\right]_{1}^{2}-\int_{1}^{2}x\cdot\frac{1}{x}dx\right\} = 2(\log 2)^{2}-4\log 2+2\left[x\right]_{1}^{2} = 2(\log 2)^{2}-4\log 2+2 = 2(\log 2-1)^{2}$$

### (5)

$$\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\tan x\,dx = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}dx$$

$\cos x = t$ とおくと，$-\sin x\,dx = dt$，$x:0\to\dfrac{\pi}{3}$，$t:1\to\dfrac{1}{2}$ これから

$$= \int_{1}^{\frac{1}{2}}-\frac{1}{t}dt = \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}\frac{1}{t}dt = \left[\log t\right]_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1} = -\log\frac{1}{2} = \log 2$$

### (6)
$$\int_4^5 \frac{dx}{x^2-4x+3} = \int_4^5 \frac{dx}{(x-3)(x-1)} = \int_4^5 \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{x-3}-\frac{1}{x-1}\right)dx = \frac{1}{2}\left[\log\frac{|x-3|}{|x-1|}\right]_4^5$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\left(\log\frac{1}{2}-\log\frac{1}{3}\right) = \frac{1}{2}(\log 3-\log 2)$$

### (7)
$$\int_0^1 xe^{x^2}\,dx \qquad x^2=t \text{ とおくと} \qquad 2x\,dx=dt \qquad x:0\to1 \qquad t:0\to1 \qquad \text{これから}$$

$$= \int_0^1 \frac{1}{2}e^t dx = \frac{1}{2}[e^t]_0^1 = \frac{1}{2}(e-1)$$

### (8)
$$\int_0^1 e^{\sqrt{x}}\,dx \qquad \sqrt{x}=t \text{ とおくと} \qquad x=t^2 \qquad dx=2t\,dt \qquad x:0\to1 \qquad t:0\to1 \qquad \text{これから}$$

$$= \int_0^1 2te^t dt = 2\left\{[te^t]_0^1 - \int_0^1 e^t dt\right\} = 2\{e-[e^t]_0^1\} = 2(e-e+1) = 2$$
