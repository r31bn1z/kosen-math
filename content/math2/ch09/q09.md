### (1)

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^7 x \, dx = \frac{6}{7} \cdot \frac{4}{5} \cdot \frac{2}{3} = \frac{16}{35}$$

### (2)

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^{10} x \, dx = \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{7}{8} \cdot \frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{63}{512}\pi$$

### (3)

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^6 x \cdot \cos^2 x \, dx = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^6 x \cdot (1-\sin^2 x) \, dx = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^6 x - \sin^8 x \, dx$$

$$= \frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{7}{8} \cdot \frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{5}{256}\pi$$

### (4)

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^2 x \cdot \cos^7 x \, dx = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} (1-\cos^2 x) \cdot \cos^7 x \, dx = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^7 x - \cos^9 x \, dx$$

$$= \frac{6}{7} \cdot \frac{4}{5} \cdot \frac{2}{3} - \frac{8}{9} \cdot \frac{6}{7} \cdot \frac{4}{5} \cdot \frac{2}{3} = \frac{16}{315}$$

### (5)

$$\int_0^{\pi} \cos^6 x \, dx = 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^6 x \, dx = 2 \cdot \frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{5}{16}\pi$$

### (6)

$$\int_0^{\pi} \cos^4 x \cdot \sin^2 x \, dx = 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^4 x \cdot \sin^2 x \, dx = 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^4 x \cdot (1-\cos^2 x) \, dx = 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^4 x - \cos^6 x \, dx$$

$$= 2\left(\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{16}\pi$$

### (7)

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sin^3 2x\,dx \qquad 2x = t \text{ とおくと} \qquad 2dx = dt \qquad dx = \frac{1}{2}dt \qquad x : 0 \to \frac{\pi}{4} \qquad t : 0 \to \frac{\pi}{2} \qquad \text{これから}$$

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1}{2}\sin^3 t\,dt = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2}{3} = \frac{1}{3}$$

### (8)

$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{1}{\cos^4 x}\,dx \qquad \tan x = t \text{ とおくと} \qquad \frac{1}{\cos^2 x}dx = dt \qquad x : 0 \to \frac{\pi}{4} \qquad t : 0 \to 1 \qquad \text{これから}$$

$$\int_0^1 (1+t^2)\,dt = \left[ t + \frac{1}{3}t^3 \right]_0^1 = 1 + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3}$$
