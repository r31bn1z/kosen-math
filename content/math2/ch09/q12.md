### (1)

$$I_{m,n} = \frac{n}{m+n+1} I_{m,n-1}$$

$$I_{m,n} = \int_0^1 x^m(1-x)^n dx = \left[\frac{1}{m+1}x^{m+1}(1-x)^n\right]_0^1 - \int_0^1 \frac{1}{m+1}x^{m+1}\{-n(1-x)^{n-1}\}dx$$

$$= \frac{n}{m+1}\int_0^1 x^m(x-1+1)(1-x)^{n-1}dx = \frac{n}{m+1}\int_0^1 \{x^m(1-x)^{n-1} - x^m(1-x)^n\}dx$$

$$= \frac{n}{m+1}\left\{\int_0^1 x^m(1-x)^{n-1}dx - \int_0^1 x^m(1-x)^n dx\right\} = \frac{n}{m+1}\left(I_{m,n-1} - I_{m,n}\right)$$

$$\left(1 + \frac{n}{m+1}\right)I_{m,n} = \frac{n}{m+1}I_{m,n-1}$$

$$\frac{m+1+n}{m+1}I_{m,n} = \frac{n}{m+1}I_{m,n-1} \qquad I_{m,n} = \frac{n}{m+n+1}I_{m,n-1}$$

### (2)

$$I_{m,n} = \int_0^1 x^m(1-x)^n dx = \left[-\frac{1}{n+1}(1-x)^{n+1}x^m\right]_0^1 + \int_0^1 \frac{1}{n+1}(1-x)^{n+1} m x^{m-1} dx$$

$$= \frac{m}{n+1}\int_0^1 (1-x)^n(1-x)x^{m-1}dx = \frac{m}{n+1}\int_0^1 \{x^{m-1}(1-x)^n - x^m(1-x)^n\}dx = \frac{m}{n+1}\left(I_{m-1,n} - I_{m,n}\right)$$

$$\left(1 + \frac{m}{n+1}\right)I_{m,n} = \frac{m}{n+1}I_{m-1,n} \qquad I_{m,n} = \frac{m}{m+n+1}I_{m-1,n}$$

$m=0$ $n=0$ のとき

$$I_{0,0} = \int_0^1 x^0(1-x)^0 dx = \int_0^1 dx = 1$$

このとき $I_{m,n} = \dfrac{m!\,n!}{(m+n+1)!}$ が成り立っている

よって(1)、(2)で求めた式から

$$I_{m,n} = 1 \times \cdots \times \frac{mn}{m+n+1} = \frac{m!\,n!}{(m+n+1)!}$$ が全ての自然数 $m,n$ で成り立つことが分かる
