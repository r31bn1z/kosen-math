$$y = \sqrt{100^2 - (4t)^2}$$

$$y' = \frac{-32t}{2\sqrt{100^2-(4t)^2}} = -\frac{16t}{\sqrt{100^2-(4t)^2}}$$

OA = 60cm なので t = 15 秒のとき

$$y' = \frac{-480}{2\sqrt{10000-3600}} = \frac{-240}{\sqrt{6400}} = -\frac{240}{80} = -3$$ －3 cm/s

加速度は

$$y'' = -\frac{16\sqrt{100^2-(4t)^2} - 16t\cdot\left(-\dfrac{16t}{\sqrt{100^2-(4t)^2}}\right)}{100^2-(4t)^2} = -\frac{\dfrac{16\cdot(100^2-(4t)^2)+256t^2}{\sqrt{100^2-(4t)^2}}}{100^2-(4t)^2}$$

t = 15 のとき

$$y'' = -\frac{\dfrac{16\cdot 6400 + 256\cdot 225}{80}}{6400} = -\frac{160000}{512000} = -0.3125$$ －0.3125 cm/s²
