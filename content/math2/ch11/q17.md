$\overrightarrow{AB} = \vec{a}$ 、 $\overrightarrow{AC} = \vec{b}$ 、 $\overrightarrow{AD} = \vec{c}$ とおく

$$
\begin{cases}
\overrightarrow{CD} = -\vec{b} + \vec{c} \\
\overrightarrow{BC} = -\vec{a} + \vec{b} \\
\overrightarrow{BD} = -\vec{a} + \vec{c}
\end{cases}
$$

よって $AB^2 + CD^2 = AD^2 + BC^2$

$(\vec{a})^2 + (-\vec{b}+\vec{c})^2 = (\vec{c})^2 + (-\vec{a}+\vec{b})^2$  $a^2 + b^2 - 2\vec{b}\vec{c} + c^2 = c^2 + a^2 - 2\vec{a}\vec{b} + b^2$

$\vec{b}\vec{c} - \vec{a}\vec{b} = 0$  $\vec{b}(-\vec{a}+\vec{c}) = 0$  $\overrightarrow{AC} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = 0$  $\therefore AC \perp BD$
