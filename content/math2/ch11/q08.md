### (1)
$$(2\vec{a}+\vec{b})\cdot(\vec{a}-\vec{b}) = 2|a|^2 - \vec{a}\cdot\vec{b} - |b|^2 = 8+3-9=2$$

### (2)
$$|\vec{a}-2\vec{b}|^2 = |a|^2 - 4\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}+4|b|^2 = 4+12+36=52$$

### (3)
$$\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{a}|\cdot|\vec{b}|} = \frac{-3}{2\cdot 3} = -\frac{1}{2} \qquad \theta = 120°$$

### (4)
$$|\vec{a}+\vec{b}| = \sqrt{(\vec{a}+\vec{b})^2} = \sqrt{|a|^2+2\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}+|b|^2} = \sqrt{4-6+9} = \sqrt{7}$$
