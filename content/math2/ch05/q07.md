### (1)
$$y' = \frac{2(3x+1)-(2x-3)\cdot 3}{(3x+1)^2} = \frac{6x+2-6x+9}{(3x+1)^2} = \frac{11}{(3x+1)^2}$$

### (2)
$$y' = \frac{2x(3x^2+1)-(x^2-1)\cdot 6x}{(3x^2+1)^2} = \frac{6x^3+2x-6x^3+6x}{(3x^2+1)^2} = \frac{8x}{(3x^2+1)^2}$$

### (3)
$$y' = \frac{-2}{(2x-3)^2}$$

### (4)
$$t' = \frac{(s^2+2s+2)-(s+1)(2s+2)}{(s^2+2s+2)^2} = \frac{s^2+2s+2-2s^2-4s-2}{(s^2+2s+2)^2} = \frac{-s(s+2)}{(s^2+2s+2)^2}$$
