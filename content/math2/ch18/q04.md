### (1)

$$\begin{vmatrix} \vec{a} & \vec{b} & \vec{c} \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & -1 & -4 \\ 4 & 5 & -6 \end{vmatrix} = 6 + 30 + (-32) + 12 + 20 + 24 = 60 \neq 0$$

より１次独立

$\vec{v} = p\vec{a} + q\vec{b} + r\vec{c}$ とおくと

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & -1 & -4 \\ 4 & 5 & -6 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} p \\ q \\ r \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 13 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 2 & -1 & -4 & 13 \\ 4 & 5 & -6 & 7 \end{array}\right] \to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 0 & -5 & -10 & 5 \\ 0 & -3 & -18 & -9 \end{array}\right] \to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 6 & 3 \end{array}\right] \to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & -1 & 6 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 & 4 \end{array}\right]$$

$$\to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & -1 & 6 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right] \to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 0 & 7 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right]$$

これから $p = 7, q = -3, r = 1$ となり $\vec{v} = 7\vec{a} - 3\vec{b} + \vec{c}$ となる

### (2)

$$\begin{vmatrix} \vec{a} & \vec{b} & \vec{c} \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 4 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 1 \\ 3 & 2 & 9 \end{vmatrix} = 27 + 12 + 12 - 27 - 2 - 72 = -50 \neq 0$$

より１次独立

$\vec{v} = p\vec{a} + q\vec{b} + r\vec{c}$ とおくと

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 1 \\ 3 & 2 & 9 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} p \\ q \\ r \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 11 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 4 & 3 & 8 \\ 2 & 3 & 1 & 11 \\ 3 & 2 & 9 & 4 \end{array}\right] \to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 4 & 3 & 8 \\ 0 & -5 & -5 & -5 \\ 0 & -10 & 0 & -20 \end{array}\right] \to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 4 & 3 & 8 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right] \to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 \end{array}\right]$$

$$\to \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 \end{array}\right]$$

これから $p = 3, q = 2, r = -1$ となり $\vec{v} = 3\vec{a} + 2\vec{b} - \vec{c}$ となる
