$ax^2 + 2bxy + cy^2 = 1$

$$\begin{pmatrix} x' \\ y' \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$$

から与式は

$a(x'\cos\theta - y'\sin\theta)^2 + 2b(x'\cos\theta - y'\sin\theta)(x'\sin\theta + y'\cos\theta) + c(x'\sin\theta + y'\cos\theta)^2 = 1$

$x'^2(a\cos^2\theta + 2b\cos\theta \cdot \sin\theta + c\sin\theta) + y'^2(a^2\sin^2\theta - 2b\cos\theta\sin\theta + c\cos^2\theta)$
$+ xy(-2a\cos\theta\sin\theta + 2b(\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta) + 2c\cos\theta\sin\theta) = 1$

x軸とy軸に一致させるには

$-2a\cos\theta\sin\theta + 2b(\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta) + 2c\cos\theta\sin\theta = 0$

$2(c-a)\cos\theta\sin\theta + 2b(\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta) = 0$

$(c-a)\sin2\theta + 2b\cos2\theta = 0$

$$\frac{\sin2\theta}{\cos2\theta} = \frac{2b}{a-c}$$

$$\tan2\theta = \frac{2b}{a-c}$$
