### (1)

$$
\begin{pmatrix} x' \\ y' \\ z' \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 5 & -4 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix}
$$

より

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 5 & -4 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}
\qquad
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 5 & -4 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2-6-3 \\ 4-2+6 \\ 10+8+9 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -7 \\ 8 \\ 27 \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 5 & -4 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 3 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -2+9 \\ -4+3 \\ -10-12 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ -1 \\ -22 \end{pmatrix}
$$

逆変換は

$$
\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 5 & -4 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 3+8+30+5+8-18 = 36
$$

$$
\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ -4 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 3+8 = 11
\qquad
-\begin{vmatrix} 3 & -1 \\ -4 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = -9+4 = -5
\qquad
\begin{vmatrix} 3 & -1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 6+1 = 7
$$

$$
-\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 5 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = -6+10 = 4
\qquad
\begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 5 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 3+5 = 8
\qquad
-\begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = -2-2 = -4
$$

$$
\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 5 & -4 \end{vmatrix} = -8-5 = -13
\qquad
-\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 5 & -4 \end{vmatrix} = 4+15 = 19
\qquad
\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1-6 = -5
$$

これから

$$
\frac{1}{36}\begin{pmatrix} 11 & -5 & 7 \\ 4 & 8 & -4 \\ -13 & 19 & -5 \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\frac{1}{36}\begin{pmatrix} 11 & -5 & 7 \\ 4 & 8 & -4 \\ -13 & 19 & -5 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 5 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{36}\begin{pmatrix} 33-25+28 \\ 12+40-16 \\ -39+95-20 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
