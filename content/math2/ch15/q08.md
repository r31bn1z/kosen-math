$$L = \begin{pmatrix} \cos 60^\circ & -\sin 60^\circ \\ \sin 60^\circ & \cos 60^\circ \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \dfrac{1}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} & \dfrac{1}{2} \end{pmatrix} \qquad M = \begin{pmatrix} \cos 135^\circ & -\sin 135^\circ \\ \sin 135^\circ & \cos 135^\circ \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\ \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \end{pmatrix}$$

### (1)

Ⓛ

$$\begin{pmatrix} \dfrac{1}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} & \dfrac{1}{2} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1+2\sqrt{3} \\ \sqrt{3}-2 \end{pmatrix}$$

Ⓜ

$$\begin{pmatrix} -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\ \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -\sqrt{2}+2\sqrt{2} \\ \sqrt{2}+2\sqrt{2} \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \sqrt{2} \\ 3\sqrt{2} \end{pmatrix}$$

### (2)

Ⓛ

$$\begin{pmatrix} \dfrac{1}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} & \dfrac{1}{2} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} \sqrt{3} \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}-\sqrt{3} \\ \dfrac{3}{2}+1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \\ \dfrac{5}{2} \end{pmatrix}$$

Ⓜ

$$\begin{pmatrix} -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\ \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} \sqrt{3} \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}-\sqrt{2} \\ \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}-\sqrt{2} \end{pmatrix}$$
