### (1)

$$f:\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix} \qquad g:\begin{pmatrix}2&-3\\3&-7\end{pmatrix}$$

### (2)

$$f\circ g=\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}2&-3\\3&-7\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}6+3&-9-7\\2+6&-3-14\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}9&-16\\8&-17\end{pmatrix}$$

$$g\circ f=\begin{pmatrix}2&-3\\3&-7\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}6-3&2-6\\9-7&3-14\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}3&-4\\2&-11\end{pmatrix}$$

### (3)

$$P'=\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}2\\-1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}6-1\\2-2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}5\\0\end{pmatrix} \qquad Q'=\begin{pmatrix}2&-3\\3&-7\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}3\\0\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}6\\9\end{pmatrix}$$

### (4)

逆変換 $f^{-1}=\dfrac{1}{6-1}\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&3\end{pmatrix}=\dfrac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&3\end{pmatrix} \qquad g^{-1}=\dfrac{1}{-14+9}\begin{pmatrix}-7&3\\-3&2\end{pmatrix}=\dfrac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}7&-3\\3&-2\end{pmatrix}$

これから

$$\dfrac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&3\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}7\\4\end{pmatrix}=\dfrac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}14-4\\-7+12\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix} \qquad \dfrac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}7&-3\\3&-2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}5\\4\end{pmatrix}=\dfrac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}35-12\\15-8\end{pmatrix}=\dfrac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}23\\7\end{pmatrix}$$
