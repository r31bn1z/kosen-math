$$\cos\theta_{ab} = \frac{(4,2,2)\cdot(4,\sqrt{3}-1,-\sqrt{3}-1)}{\sqrt{4^2+2^2+2^2}\sqrt{4^2+(\sqrt{3}-1)^2+(-\sqrt{3}-1)^2}} = \frac{16+2\sqrt{3}-2-2\sqrt{3}-2}{\sqrt{24}\sqrt{24}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\cos\theta_{bc} = \frac{(4,\sqrt{3}-1,-\sqrt{3}-1)\cdot(4,-\sqrt{3}-1,\sqrt{3}-1)}{\sqrt{4^2+(\sqrt{3}-1)^2+(-\sqrt{3}-1)^2}\sqrt{4^2+(-\sqrt{3}-1)^2+(\sqrt{3}-1)^2}} = \frac{12}{\sqrt{24}\sqrt{24}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\cos\theta_{ca} = \frac{(4,2,2)\cdot(4,\sqrt{3}-1,-\sqrt{3}-1)}{\sqrt{4^2+2^2+2^2}\sqrt{4^2+(\sqrt{3}-1)^2+(-\sqrt{3}-1)^2}} = \frac{16+2\sqrt{3}-2-2\sqrt{3}-2}{\sqrt{24}\sqrt{24}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

これから $\theta_{ab} = \theta_{bc} = \theta_{ca} = 60°$
