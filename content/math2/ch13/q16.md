### (1)
$(2,3,4)$ $(1,-2,-3)$ $(4,3,-2)$

法線ベクトル $n=(a,b,c)$ とおき、平面の方程式を $ax+by+cz+d=0$ とおくと

$$\begin{pmatrix}2&3&4\\1&-2&-3\\4&3&-2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\\c\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-d\\-d\\-d\end{pmatrix}$$

$$\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}2&3&4&-d\\1&-2&-3&-d\\4&3&-2&-d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&7&10&d\\1&-2&-3&-d\\0&11&10&3d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&7&10&d\\1&-2&-3&-d\\0&4&0&2d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&7&10&d\\1&-2&-3&-d\\0&1&0&\dfrac{d}{2}\end{array}\right)$$

$$\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&0&10&-\dfrac{5}{2}d\\1&0&-3&0\\0&1&0&\dfrac{d}{2}\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&0&1&-\dfrac{d}{4}\\1&0&-3&0\\0&1&0&d/2\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&0&1&-\dfrac{d}{4}\\1&0&0&-\dfrac{3}{4}d\\0&1&0&\dfrac{d}{2}\end{array}\right)$$

これから $-\dfrac{3}{4}dx+\dfrac{d}{2}y-\dfrac{d}{4}z+d=0$ $-3x+2y-z+4=0$

### (2)
$(3,2,1)$ $(1,9,2)$ $(-4,4,-3)$

$$\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}3&2&1&-d\\1&9&2&-d\\-4&4&-3&-d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&-25&-5&2d\\1&9&2&-d\\0&40&5&-5d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&-25&-5&2d\\1&9&2&-d\\0&15&0&-3d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&-25&-5&2d\\1&9&2&-d\\0&1&0&-\dfrac{d}{5}\end{array}\right)$$

$$\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&0&-5&-3d\\1&0&2&\dfrac{4}{5}d\\0&1&0&-\dfrac{d}{5}\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&0&1&\dfrac{3}{5}d\\1&0&2&\dfrac{4}{5}d\\0&1&0&-\dfrac{d}{5}\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}0&0&1&\dfrac{3}{5}d\\1&0&0&-\dfrac{2}{5}d\\0&1&0&-\dfrac{d}{5}\end{array}\right)$$

これから $-\dfrac{2}{5}dx-\dfrac{d}{5}y+\dfrac{3}{5}dz+d=0$  $2x+y-3z-5=0$

### (3)
$(1,4,2)$ $(2,1,3)$ $(3,-2,0)$

$$\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}1&4&2&-d\\2&1&3&-d\\3&-2&0&-d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}1&4&2&-d\\0&-7&-1&d\\0&-14&-6&2d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}1&4&2&-d\\0&7&1&-d\\0&7&3&-d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}1&-10&0&d\\0&7&1&-d\\0&-14&0&2d\end{array}\right)\to\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}1&-10&0&d\\0&7&1&-d\\0&7&0&-d\end{array}\right)$$

$$\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -10 & 0 & d \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 7 & 0 & -d \end{array}\right) \rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -10 & 0 & d \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & -\dfrac{d}{7} \end{array}\right) \rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 0 & -\dfrac{3}{7}d \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & -\dfrac{d}{7} \end{array}\right)$$

これから $-\dfrac{3}{7}dx - \dfrac{d}{7}y + d = 0$    $3x + y - 7 = 0$
