### (1)
$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 3 \cdot 2 + (-1) \cdot 4 + 2 \cdot (-1) = 6 - 4 - 2 = 0$$

### (2)
$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{c} = 3 \cdot 1 + (-1) \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot 3 = 3 - 2 + 6 = 7$$

### (3)
$$\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}}{|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|} = \frac{0}{\sqrt{3^2 + (-1)^2 + 2^2} \cdot \sqrt{2^2 + 4^2 + (-1)^2}} = 0 \qquad \theta = 90°$$

### (4)
$$\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{a} \cdot \vec{c}}{|\vec{a}||\vec{c}|} = \frac{7}{\sqrt{3^2 + (-1)^2 + 2^2} \cdot \sqrt{1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2}} = \frac{7}{\sqrt{14}\sqrt{14}} = \frac{1}{2} \qquad \theta = 60°$$

### (5)
$\sqrt{6}$のベクトルを$\vec{d} = (x, y, z)$とおくと

$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{d} = 3x + (-1) \cdot y + 2z = 0 \qquad 3x - y + 2z = 0 \qquad ①$$

$$\vec{b} \cdot \vec{d} = 2x + 4y + (-1) \cdot z = 0 \qquad 2x + 4y - z = 0 \qquad ②$$

$$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 6 \qquad ③$$

①＋②×２より  $$7x + 7y = 0 \qquad x = -y \qquad ④$$

①×２－②×３  $$-14y + 7z = 0 \qquad z = 2y \qquad ⑤$$

④、⑤を③に代入して  $$y^2 + y^2 + 4y^2 = 6 \qquad y^2 = 1 \qquad y = \pm 1 \qquad ⑥$$

④、⑥を③に代入して  $$y^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 6 \qquad 1 + 1 + z^2 = 6 \qquad z = \pm 2 \qquad ⑦$$

⑥、⑦を①に代入して  $$3x \mp 1 \pm 4 = 0 \qquad 3x \pm 3 = 0 \qquad x = \mp 1$$

これから

$$(\mp 1, \pm 1, \pm 2)$$
