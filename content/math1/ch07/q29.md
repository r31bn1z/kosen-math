$$\frac{4x^2}{x^4-1} = \frac{4x^2}{(x^2-1)(x^2+1)} = \frac{4x^2}{(x-1)(x+1)(x^2+1)} = \frac{a}{x-1}+\frac{b}{x+1}+\frac{cx+d}{x^2+1}$$

$$a(x+1)(x^2+1)+b(x-1)(x^2+1)+(cx+d)(x-1)(x+1)=4x^2$$

$$a(x^3+x^2+x+1)+b(x^3-x^2+x-1)+(cx^3+dx^2-cx-d)=4x^2$$

$$a+b+c=0 \qquad a-b+d=4 \qquad a+b-c=0 \qquad a-b-d=0$$

これから $a=1$ $b=-1$ $c=0$ $d=2$ が求まる

よって

$$\frac{1}{x-1}-\frac{1}{x+1}+\frac{2}{x^2+1}$$
