$$x^2+x-2=(x-1)(x+2)$$

$$
\begin{array}{r}
x^5+x^4+x^3+(a+1)x^2+(a+1)x+(a+1) \\[4pt]
x-1\big)\ \overline{x^6 \qquad\qquad\qquad +ax^3 \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\ \ b} \\
\underline{x^6-x^5} \\
x^5 \\
\underline{x^5-x^4} \\
x^4+ax^3 \\
\underline{x^4-x^3} \\
(a+1)x^3 \\
\underline{(a+1)x^3-(a+1)x^2} \\
(a+1)x^2 \\
\underline{(a+1)x^2-(a+1)x} \\
(a+1)x+b \\
\underline{(a+1)x-(a+1)} \\
b+(a+1)
\end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r}
x^5-2x^4+4x^3+(a-8)x^2-2(a-8)x+4(a-8) \\[4pt]
x+2\big)\ \overline{x^6 \qquad\qquad\qquad +ax^3 \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\ \ b} \\
\underline{x^6+2x^5} \\
-2x^5 \\
\underline{-2x^5-4x^4} \\
4x^4+ax^3 \\
\underline{4x^4+8x^3} \\
(a-8)x^3 \\
\underline{(a-8)x^3+2(a-8)x^2} \\
-2(a-8)x^2 \\
\underline{-2(a-8)x^2-4(a-8)x} \\
4(a-8)x+b \\
\underline{4(a-8)x+8(a-8)} \\
b-8(a-8)
\end{array}
$$

$$b+a+1=0$$

$$b-8a+64=0$$

これから $a=7$ $b=-8$
