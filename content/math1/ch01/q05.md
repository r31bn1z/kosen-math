### (1)
$$(2a+3)(4a-2) = 8a^2 + 12a - 4a - 6 = 8a^2 + 8a - 6$$

### (2)
$$(x^2-2x+3)(x+4) = x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x^2 - 8x + 3x + 12 = x^3 + 2x^2 - 5x + 12$$

### (3)
$$(x-3)(2x^2+x+7) = 2x^3 + x^2 + 7x - 6x^2 - 3x - 21 = 2x^3 - 5x^2 + 4x - 21$$

### (4)
$$(x^2+2x-1)(3x^2-x+5) = 3x^4 - x^3 + 5x^2 + 6x^3 - 2x^2 + 10x - 3x^2 + x - 5 = 3x^4 + 5x^3 + 11x - 5$$
