### (1)
$$(a + 4)^3 = a^3 + 12a^2 + 48a + 64$$

### (2)
$$(3x - y)^3 = 27x^3 - 27x^2y + 9xy^2 - y^3$$

### (3)
$$(2s - 3t)^3 = 8s^3 - 36s^2t + 54st^2 - 27t^3$$

### (4)
$$\left(3a - \frac{1}{3}\right)^3 = 27a^3 - 9a^2 + a - \frac{1}{27}$$

### (5)
$$(a + 2b)(a^2 - 2ab + 4b^2) = a^3 + 8b^3$$

### (6)
$$\left(\frac{1}{2}x - 2y\right)\left(\frac{x^2}{4} + xy + 4y^2\right) = \frac{1}{8}x^3 - 8y^3$$
