### (3)

$$a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4}-2a^{2}b^{2}-2b^{2}c^{2}-2c^{2}a^{2} = a^{4}-2a^{2}(b^{2}+c^{2})+(b^{4}-2b^{2}c^{2}+c^{4})$$

$$= a^{4}-2a^{2}(b^{2}+c^{2})+(b^{2}-c^{2})^{2} = a^{4}-2a^{2}(b^{2}+c^{2})+(b+c)^{2}(b-c)^{2}$$

$$= \{a+(b+c)\}\{a-(b+c)\}\{a+(b-c)\}\{a-(b-c)\}$$

$$= (a+b+c)(a-b-c)(a+b-c)(a-b+c)$$
