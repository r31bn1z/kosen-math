### (1)

$$\frac{4x+3}{2x+1} = \frac{2(2x+1)+1}{2x+1} = 2 + \frac{1}{2x+1}$$

### (2)

$$\frac{x^2-2x+3}{x-2} = \frac{x(x-2)+3}{x-2} = x + \frac{3}{x-2}$$

### (3)

$$
\begin{array}{r}
3x-13\phantom{00}\\[-2pt]
x+3\ \overline{\smash{\big)}\ 3x^2-4x\ +5\phantom{0}}\\[-2pt]
\underline{3x^2+9x\phantom{000}}\\[-2pt]
-13x+5\phantom{0}\\[-2pt]
\underline{-13x-39}\\[-2pt]

\end{array}
$$

$$\frac{3x^2-4x+5}{x+3} = 3x-13+\frac{44}{x+3}$$

### (4)

$$
\begin{array}{r}
2x-1\phantom{00}\\[-2pt]
3x-1\ \overline{\smash{\big)}\ 6x^2-5x\ +6\phantom{0}}\\[-2pt]
\underline{6x^2-2x\phantom{000}}\\[-2pt]
-3x+6\phantom{0}\\[-2pt]
\underline{-3x+1}\\[-2pt]

\end{array}
$$

$$\frac{6x^2-5x+6}{3x-1} = 2x-1+\frac{5}{3x-1}$$

### (5)

$$
\begin{array}{r}
3x-4\phantom{000000}\\[-2pt]
x^2+2x-3\ \overline{\smash{\big)}\ 3x^3+2x^2-5x+3\phantom{0}}\\[-2pt]
\underline{3x^3+6x^2-9x\phantom{00000}}\\[-2pt]
-4x^2+4x+3\phantom{0}\\[-2pt]
\underline{-4x^2-8x+12}\\[-2pt]

\end{array}
$$

$$\frac{3x^3+2x^2-5x+3}{x^2+2x-3} = 3x-4+\frac{12x-9}{x^2+2x-3}$$
