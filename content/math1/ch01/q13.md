### (1)
$$x^3 + 6x^2y + 12xy^2 + 8y^3 = (x + 2y)^3$$

### (2)
$$8x^3 - 12x^2 + 6x - 1 = (2x - 1)^3$$

### (3)
$$x^3 - 9x^2y + 27xy^2 - 27y^3 = (x - 3y)^3$$

### (4)
$$a^3 + 27 = (a + 3)(a^2 - 3a + 9)$$

### (5)
$$27p^3 - 8q^3 = (3p - 2q)(9p^2 + 6pq + 4q^2)$$

### (6)
$$x^6 - 64y^6 = (x^3 - 8y^3)(x^3 + 8y^3) = (x - 2y)(x^2 + 2xy + 4y^2)(x + 2y)(x^2 - 2xy + 4y^2)$$
