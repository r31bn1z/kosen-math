### (1)

$$\frac{x^2-(y-z)^2}{(x-y)^2-z^2}=\frac{\{x-(y-z)\}(x+(y-z))}{\{(x-y)-z\}\{(x-y)+z\}}=\frac{x+y-z}{x-y-z}$$

### (2)

$$\frac{a^3-a^2-8a+12}{a^2-4a+4}=\frac{(a-2)^2(a+3)}{(a-2)^2}=a+3$$

### (3)

$$\frac{2x^3+x^2+x-1}{x^3-1}=\frac{(2x-1)(x^2+x+1)}{(x-1)(x^2+x+1)}=\frac{2x-1}{x-1}$$

### (4)

$$\frac{x^3+3x^2-x-3}{x^4+x^3+x^2-x-2}=\frac{(x-1)(x+3)(x+1)}{(x-1)(x^3+2x^2+3x+2)}=\frac{(x-1)(x+3)(x+1)}{(x-1)(x+1)(x^2+x+2)}=\frac{x+3}{x^2+x+2}$$
