### (1)
$3x^2 - 5x + 2 = (3x-2)(x-1)$ より

$$(3x^2 - 5x + 2) \div (x-1) = \frac{(3x-2)(x-1)}{x-1} = 3x-2$$

### (2)
$2x^3 - 7x^2 + 12x - 9 = (2x-3)(x^2-2x+3)$ より

$$(2x^3 - 7x^2 + 12x - 9) \div (2x-3) = \frac{(2x-3)(x^2-2x+3)}{2x-3} = x^2-2x+3$$

### (3)
$4x^3 - 5x^2 + x - 66 = (x-3)(4x^2+7x+22)$ より

$$(4x^3 - 5x^2 + x - 66) \div (x-3) = \frac{(x-3)(4x^2+7x+22)}{x-3} = 4x^2+7x+22$$

### (4)

$$
\begin{array}{r}
2x^{2}+x+2 \\
3x^{2}-x-1{\big)}\ \overline{6x^{4}+x^{3}+3x^{2}-x+3} \\
\underline{6x^{4}-2x^{3}-2x^{2}} \\
3x^{3}+5x^{2}-x \\
\underline{3x^{3}-x^{2}-x} \\
6x^{2}+3 \\
\underline{6x^{2}-2x-2} \\
2x+5
\end{array}
$$

これから

$$(6x^4+x^3+3x^2-x+3) \div (3x^2-x-1) = 2x^2+x+2 \quad \text{余り} \quad 2x+5$$
