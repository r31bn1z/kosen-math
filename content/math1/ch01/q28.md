### (1)
$$(x^2-3x+2)(2x-1)=2x^3-x^2-6x^2+3x+4x-2=2x^3-7x^2+7x-2$$

### (2)
$$(x+2)(x^2-2x+4)=x^3+2x^2-2x^2-4x+4x+8=x^3+8$$

### (3)
$$(1-a-a^2)(3-2a)=3-2a-3a+2a^2-3a^2+2a^3=3-5a-a^2+2a^3$$

### (4)
$$(x^2-y^2)(x^4-x^2y^2+y^4)=x^6-x^4y^2+x^2y^4-x^4y^2+x^2y^4-y^6=x^6-2x^4y^2+2x^2y^4-y^6$$

### (5)
$$(x^2-1)(x^3-2x^2+1)=x^5-2x^4+x^2-x^3+2x^2-1=x^5-2x^4-x^3+3x^2-1$$

### (6)
$$(x+y+z)(x^2+y^2+z^2-xy-yz-zx)$$

$$=x^3+xy^2+xz^2-x^2y-xyz-x^2z+x^2y+y^3+yz^2-xy^2-y^2z-xyz+x^2z+y^2z+z^3-xyz-yz^2-xz^2$$

$$=x^3+y^3+z^3-3xyz$$
