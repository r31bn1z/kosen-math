### (1)
$$x^2 - y^2 + 2y - 1 = x^2 - (y-1)^2 = (x-y+1)(x+y-1)$$

### (2)
$$x^2 - y^2 - 4x - 2y + 3 = (x-2)^2 - (y+1)^2 = (x+y-1)(x-y-3)$$

### (3)
$$x^4 + x^2 - 2 = (x^2+2)(x^2-1) = (x^2+2)(x-1)(x+1)$$

### (4)
$$a^4 - 10a^2 + 9 = (a^2-9)(a^2-1) = (a-3)(a+3)(a-1)(a+1)$$

### (5)
$$(a+b)^2 - (a-b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 - a^2 + 2ab - b^2 = 4ab$$

### (6)
$$(a^2+b^2)^2 - 4a^2b^2 = a^4 + 2a^2b^2 + b^4 - 4a^2b^2 = a^4 - 2a^2b^2 + b^4 = (a^2-b^2)^2 = (a+b)^2(a-b)^2$$
