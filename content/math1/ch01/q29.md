### (1)

$$(x+y+3)(x+y-2)=(x+y)^2+(x+y)-6=x^2+2xy+y^2+x+y-6$$

### (2)

$$(2x+y+1)(2x+y-3)=(2x+y)^2-2(2x+y)-3=4x^2+4xy+y^2-4x-2y-3$$

### (3)

$$(x-2y-3)(x-2y+2)=(x-2y)^2-(x-2y)-6=x^2-4xy-4y^2-x+2y-6$$

### (4)

$$(x^2+x+1)(x^2-x+1)=(x^2+1)^2-x^2=x^4+2x^2+1-x^2=x^4+x^2+1$$

### (5)

$$(x+1)(x+2)(x+3)(x+4)=(x^2+3x+2)(x^2+7x+12)$$

$$=x^4+7x^3+12x^2+3x^3+21x^2+36x+2x^2+14x+24=x^4+10x^3+35x^2+50x+24$$

### (6)

$$(x-1)(x+1)(x^4+x^2+1)=(x^2-1)(x^4+x^2+1)$$

$$=x^6+x^4+x^2-x^4-x^2-1=x^6-1$$

### (7)

$$(a+b-c)^2=(a+b)^2-2c(a+b)-c^2=a^2+2ab+b^2-2ac-2bc+c^2$$

### (8)

$$(x-2y+3)^2=(x-2y)^2+6(x-2y)+9=x^2-4xy-4y^2+6x-12y+9$$

### (9)

$$(a+b+c)(a+b-c)(a-b+c)(a-b-c)=\{(a+b)^2-c^2\}\{(a-b)^2-c^2\}$$

$$=(a^2+b^2-c^2+2ab)(a^2+b^2-c^2-2ab)$$

$$=(a^2+b^2-c^2)^2-4a^2b^2$$

$$=a^4+b^4+c^4-2a^2b^2-2b^2c^2-2a^2c^2$$
