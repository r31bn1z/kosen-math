### (1)

$$\dfrac{1}{c-\dfrac{1}{c+\dfrac{1}{c}}} = \dfrac{1}{c-\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{c^2+1}{c}}} = \dfrac{1}{c-\dfrac{c}{c^2+1}} = \dfrac{c^2+1}{c^3+c-c} = \dfrac{c^2+1}{c^3}$$

### (2)

$$1-\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{x}}}} = 1-\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{x}{x-1}}} = 1-\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{x-1}{x-1-x}} = 1-\dfrac{1}{1+x-1} = 1-\dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{x-1}{x}$$

### (3)

$$\dfrac{x}{x-\dfrac{x+1}{x+3-\dfrac{x-1}{x}}} = \dfrac{x}{x-\dfrac{x(x+1)}{x^2+3x-x+1}} = \dfrac{x}{x-\dfrac{x(x+1)}{x^2+2x+1}} = \dfrac{x}{x-\dfrac{x(x+1)}{(x+1)^2}} = \dfrac{x}{x-\dfrac{x}{(x+1)}} = \dfrac{x(x+1)}{x^2+x-x} = \dfrac{x+1}{x}$$
