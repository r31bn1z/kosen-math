### (1)
$$\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{x}}{1+\dfrac{1}{x}} = \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{x}}{\dfrac{x+1}{x}} = \dfrac{1}{x+1}$$

### (2)
$$\dfrac{x}{1+\dfrac{1}{x-1}} = \dfrac{x}{\dfrac{x-1+1}{x-1}} = x-1$$

### (3)
$$\dfrac{x-\dfrac{2}{x+1}}{\dfrac{x}{x+1}-2} = \dfrac{\dfrac{x(x+1)-2}{x+1}}{\dfrac{x-2(x+1)}{x+1}} = \dfrac{x^2+x-2}{x-2x-2} = \dfrac{(x-1)(x+2)}{-x-2} = 1-x$$

### (4)
$$\dfrac{x+y}{\dfrac{y}{x}-\dfrac{x}{y}} = \dfrac{x+y}{\dfrac{y^2-x^2}{xy}} = \dfrac{xy(x+y)}{(y-x)(y+x)} = \dfrac{xy}{y-x}$$

### (5)
$$\dfrac{1+\dfrac{a-b}{a+b}}{1-\dfrac{a-b}{a+b}} = \dfrac{\dfrac{a+b+a-b}{a+b}}{\dfrac{a+b-a+b}{a+b}} = \dfrac{2a}{2b} = \dfrac{a}{b}$$
