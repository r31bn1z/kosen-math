### (1)
$$x^4 + 4 = (x^2 + 2)^2 - 4x^2 = (x^2 + 2x + 2)(x^2 - 2x + 2)$$

### (2)
$$a^4 - 7a^2 + 9 = (a^2 - 3)^2 - a^2 = (a^2 - a - 3)(a^2 + a - 3)$$

### (3)
$$p^4 + p^2 + 25 = (p^2 + 5)^2 - 9p^2 = (p^2 + 3p + 5)(p^2 - 3p + 5)$$

### (4)
$$p^4 - 13p^2q^2 + 4q^4 = (p^2 - 2q^2)^2 - 9p^2q^2 = (p^2 + 3pq - 2q^2)(p^2 - 3pq - 2q^2)$$
