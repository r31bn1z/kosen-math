### (1)
$$\frac{(x+1)(x^2-4x+4)}{(x-2)(x^2+2x+1)}=\frac{(x+1)(x-2)^2}{(x-2)(x+1)^2}=\frac{x-2}{x+1}$$

### (2)
$$\frac{x^2-xy-6y^2}{x^2-5xy+6y^2}=\frac{(x-3y)(x+2y)}{(x-2y)(x-3y)}=\frac{x+2y}{x-2y}$$

### (3)
$$\frac{6x^3-6x^2-12x}{3x^3-12x}=\frac{6x(x-2)(x+1)}{3x(x-2)(x+2)}=\frac{2(x+1)}{x+2}$$

### (4)
$$\frac{x^3-x^2-20x}{x^3-x^2-2x}=\frac{x(x-5)(x+4)}{x(x-2)(x+1)}=\frac{(x-5)(x+4)}{(x-2)(x+1)}=\frac{x^2-x-20}{x^2-x-2}$$
