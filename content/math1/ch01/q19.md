### (1)
$$\dfrac{12ab^4}{3a^2b} = \dfrac{4b^3}{a}$$

### (2)
$$\dfrac{12x^4}{18xy^2} = \dfrac{2x^3}{3y^2}$$

### (3)
$$\dfrac{2xy-3x}{5xy} = \dfrac{2y-3}{5y}$$

### (4)
$$\dfrac{3u-2v}{9u^2-4v^2} = \dfrac{3u-2v}{(3u-2v)(3u+2v)} = \dfrac{1}{3u+2v}$$

### (5)
$$\dfrac{t^2+2t-15}{t^2-6t+9} = \dfrac{(t+5)(t-3)}{(t-3)^2} = \dfrac{t+5}{t-3}$$
