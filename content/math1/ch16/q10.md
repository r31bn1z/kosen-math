### (1)

$$4x^2 - 9y^2 = 36 \qquad \dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$$

$a=3$、$b=2$ から漸近線は $y=\pm\dfrac{2}{3}x$

### (2)

$$-4x^2 + 9y^2 = 36 \qquad \dfrac{x^2}{-9} + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$$

$a=-3$、$b=2$ から漸近線は $y=\pm\dfrac{2}{3}x$

### (3)

$$x^2 - 2y^2 = 1 \qquad \dfrac{x^2}{1} - \dfrac{y^2}{\frac{1}{2}} = 1$$

$a=1$、$b=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ から漸近線は $y=\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}x$

### (4)

$$-x^2 + 2y^2 = 1 \qquad \dfrac{x^2}{-1} + \dfrac{y^2}{\frac{1}{2}} = 1$$

$a=-1$、$b=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ から漸近線は $y=\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}x$
