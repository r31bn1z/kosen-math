### (1)
$$x^2 = 4y$$

$$x^2 = 4py$$

$$p = 1$$

焦点$(0,1)$  準線$y = -1$

### (2)
$$y = x^2$$

$$x^2 = 4py$$

$$p = \dfrac{1}{4}$$

焦点$\left(0, \dfrac{1}{4}\right)$  準線 $y = -\dfrac{1}{4}$

### (3)
$$y = ax^2$$

$$x^2 = 4py$$

$$p = \dfrac{1}{4a}$$

焦点$\left(0, \dfrac{1}{4a}\right)$  準線 $y = -\dfrac{1}{4a}$

### (4)
$$y = a(x-p)^2 + q$$    $$y - q = a(x-p)^2$$  これは$y = ax^2$を$(p,q)$平行移動した者なので

(3)から

焦点$\left(p, \dfrac{1}{4a} + q\right)$  準線 $y = -\dfrac{1}{4a} + q$
