### (1)
$$(x-1)^2+(y-2)^2=9$$

### (2)
$$r^2=5^2+(-1)^2=26$$

$$(x-5)^2+(y+1)^2=26$$

### (3)
$$r^2=\left(1-(-3)\right)^2+(-1-1)^2=20$$

$$(x-1)^2+(y+1)^2=20$$

### (4)
中心は$\left(\dfrac{1+(-3)}{2},\dfrac{-5+7}{2}\right)=(-1,1)$

$$r^2=\dfrac{\left(1-(-3)\right)^2+(-5-7)^2}{4}=40$$

$$(x+1)^2+(y-1)^2=40$$

### (5)
中心を$(x,y)$とおくと

$$x^2+y^2=k \qquad ①$$

$$x^2+(y-1)^2=k \qquad ②$$

$$(x-2)^2+(y-2)^2=k \qquad ③$$

①＝②から $y=\dfrac{1}{2}$  ④

①＝③から $x+y=2$  ④を代入して

$$x=\dfrac{3}{2}$$

$$r^2 = \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{10}{4}$$

$$\left(x - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 + \left(y - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{10}{4}$$
