### (1)

$$x^2 + 2(2m-1)x + 5m^2 - 4 = 0$$

$$\text{判別式}D = 4(2m-1)^2 - 4(5m^2-4) = 4(4m^2-4m+1) - 20m^2 + 16 = -4m^2 - 16m + 20$$

$$= -4(m^2+4m-5) = -4(m+5)(m-1) \geq 0 \text{ となればよいので}$$

$$-5 \leq m \leq 1$$

### (2)

$$x = 2m - 1 \pm \sqrt{(2m-1)^2 - (5m^2-4)} = 2m - 1 \pm \sqrt{-(m+5)(m-1)}$$

解が正と負なので２つの解の積は負になるはずである

$$\left(2m-1+\sqrt{-(m+5)(m-1)}\right)\left(2m-1-\sqrt{-(m+5)(m-1)}\right) = (2m-1)^2 - \{-(m+5)(m-1)\}$$

$$= 4m^2 - 4m + 1 + m^2 + 4m - 5 = 5m^2 - 4 < 0$$

$$m^2 < \frac{4}{5} \qquad -\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5} < m < \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$$
