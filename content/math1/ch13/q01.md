$\sin\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$から $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$  $\cos\beta = \dfrac{5}{13}$から $\sin\beta = \dfrac{12}{13}$

### (1)
$$\sin(\alpha+\beta) = \sin\alpha \cdot \cos\beta + \cos\alpha \cdot \sin\beta = \dfrac{4}{5}\cdot\dfrac{5}{13} + \dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{12}{13} = \dfrac{4}{13} + \dfrac{36}{65} = \dfrac{56}{65}$$

### (2)
$$\cos(\alpha+\beta) = \cos\alpha \cdot \cos\beta - \sin\alpha \cdot \sin\beta = \dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{5}{13} - \dfrac{4}{5}\cdot\dfrac{12}{13} = \dfrac{15}{65} - \dfrac{48}{65} = -\dfrac{33}{65}$$

### (3)
$$\sin(\beta-\alpha) = \sin\beta \cdot \cos\alpha - \cos\beta \cdot \sin\alpha = \dfrac{12}{13}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5} - \dfrac{5}{13}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5} = \dfrac{36}{65} - \dfrac{20}{65} = \dfrac{16}{65}$$

### (4)
$$\cos(\beta-\alpha) = \cos\beta \cdot \cos\alpha + \sin\beta \cdot \sin\alpha = \dfrac{5}{13}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5} + \dfrac{12}{13}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5} = \dfrac{15}{65} + \dfrac{48}{65} = \dfrac{63}{65}$$
