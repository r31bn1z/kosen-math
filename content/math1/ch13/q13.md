$$\sin x = \frac{4}{5} \qquad \cos x = -\frac{3}{5} \qquad (\frac{\pi}{2} < x < \pi)$$

### (1)
$$\sin^2\frac{x}{2} = \frac{1}{2}(1-\cos x) = \frac{1}{2}\left(1+\frac{3}{5}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{8}{5} = \frac{4}{5}$$

$$\sin\frac{x}{2} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$$

### (2)
$$\cos^2\frac{x}{2} = \frac{1}{2}(1+\cos x) = \frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{3}{5}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2}{5} = \frac{1}{5}$$

$$\cos\frac{x}{2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$$

### (3)
$$\tan\frac{x}{2} = \frac{\sin\frac{x}{2}}{\cos\frac{x}{2}} = 2$$
