$$\cos\left(x+\frac{2}{3}\pi\right)+\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\left(\cos x\cdot\cos\frac{2}{3}\pi-\sin x\cdot\sin\frac{2}{3}\pi\right)+\left(\sin x\cdot\cos\frac{\pi}{4}+\cos x\cdot\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$$

$$=-\frac{1}{2}\cos x-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin x+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\sin x+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\cos x$$

$$=\frac{\sqrt{2}-1}{2}\cos x+\frac{\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin x$$
