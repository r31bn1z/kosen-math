### (1)
$$\sin\frac{\pi}{9}+\sin\frac{2}{9}\pi=2\sin\frac{\dfrac{\pi}{9}+\dfrac{2}{9}\pi}{2}\cos\frac{\left(\dfrac{\pi}{9}-\dfrac{2}{9}\pi\right)}{2}=2\sin\frac{\pi}{6}\cos\frac{\pi}{18}$$

### (2)
$$\cos\frac{2}{9}\pi+\cos\frac{7}{9}\pi=2\cos\frac{\dfrac{2}{9}\pi+\dfrac{7}{9}\pi}{2}\cos\frac{\dfrac{2}{9}\pi-\dfrac{7}{9}\pi}{2}=2\cos\frac{\pi}{2}\cos\frac{5}{18}\pi$$

### (3)
$$\cos\frac{3}{4}\pi-\cos\frac{2}{3}\pi=-2\sin\frac{\dfrac{3}{4}\pi+\dfrac{2}{3}\pi}{2}\sin\frac{\dfrac{3}{4}\pi-\dfrac{2}{3}\pi}{2}=-2\sin\frac{17}{24}\pi\sin\frac{\pi}{24}$$

### (4)
$$\sin8x-\sin4x=2\cos\frac{8x+4x}{2}\sin\frac{8x-4x}{2}=2\cos6x\cdot\sin2x$$

### (5)
$$\cos2x+\cos4x=2\cos\frac{2x+4x}{2}\cos\frac{2x-4x}{2}=2\cos3x\cdot\cos x$$

### (6)
$$\cos6x-\cos2x=-2\sin\frac{6x+2x}{2}\cdot\sin\frac{6x-2x}{2}=-2\sin4x\cdot\sin2x$$
