$\sin x = \dfrac{1}{3}$ なので $\cos x = \dfrac{\sqrt{8}}{3}$ $\tan x = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{8}}$ となる

### (1)
$$\sin\left(x-\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \sin x \cdot \cos\frac{\pi}{3} - \cos x \cdot \sin\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{8}}{3}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{1}{6} - \frac{2\sqrt{6}}{6} = \frac{1-2\sqrt{6}}{6}$$

### (2)
$$\cos\left(x-\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \cos x \cdot \cos\frac{\pi}{3} + \sin x \cdot \sin\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{8}}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}}{6}$$

### (3)
$$\tan\left(x-\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{\tan x - \tan\dfrac{\pi}{3}}{1+\tan x \cdot \tan\dfrac{\pi}{3}} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{8}}-\sqrt{3}}{1+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{8}}\cdot\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1-2\sqrt{6}}{2\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}} = \frac{(1-2\sqrt{6})(2\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{3})}{8-3} = \frac{8\sqrt{2}-9\sqrt{3}}{5}$$
