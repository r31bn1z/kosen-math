### (1)

$$\cos\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \cos\theta\cdot\cos\frac{\pi}{4} - \sin\theta\cdot\sin\frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\cos\theta-\sin\theta)$$

### (2)

$$\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\theta\right) = \sin\frac{\pi}{3}\cdot\cos\theta - \cos\frac{\pi}{3}\cdot\sin\theta = \frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{3}\cos\theta-\sin\theta)$$

### (3)

$$\tan\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{\tan\theta+\tan\dfrac{\pi}{6}}{1-\tan\theta\cdot\tan\dfrac{\pi}{6}} = \frac{\tan\theta+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}}{1-\tan\theta\cdot\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}\tan\theta+1}{\sqrt{3}-\tan\theta}$$

### (4)

$$\cos\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \cos\theta\cdot\cos\frac{\pi}{4} + \sin\theta\cdot\sin\frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\cos\theta+\sin\theta)$$

### (5)

$$\sin\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \cos\theta\cdot\cos\frac{\pi}{6} - \sin\theta\cdot\sin\frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{3}\sin\theta-\cos\theta)$$

### (6)

$$\tan\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{\tan\theta+\tan\dfrac{\pi}{4}}{1-\tan\theta\cdot\tan\dfrac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{1+\tan\theta}{1-\tan\theta}$$
