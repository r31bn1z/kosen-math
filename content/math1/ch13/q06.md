$\sin x = \dfrac{4}{5}$  から  $\cos x = \dfrac{3}{5}$

### (1)
$$\sin 2x = 2\sin x \cdot \cos x = 2 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{24}{25}$$

### (2)
$$\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x = 1 - 2 \cdot \left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^2 = 1 - \dfrac{32}{25} = -\dfrac{7}{25}$$

### (3)
$$\tan 2x = \dfrac{\sin 2x}{\cos 2x} = -\dfrac{24}{7}$$
