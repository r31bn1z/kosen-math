### (1)
$$(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2 = \sin^2\theta + 2\sin\theta\cos\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1 + 2\sin\theta\cos\theta = 1 + \sin2\theta$$

### (2)
$$\cos^4\theta - \sin^4\theta = (\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta)(\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta) = \cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta = \frac{1}{2}(1+\cos2\theta) - \frac{1}{2}(1-\cos2\theta) = \cos2\theta$$

### (3)
$$\frac{1-\cos2\theta}{\sin2\theta} = \frac{1-\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta}{2\sin\theta\cos\theta} = \frac{\sin^2\theta+\sin^2\theta}{2\sin\theta\cos\theta} = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} = \tan\theta$$

### (4)
$$\cos2\theta = \cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta = \frac{\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta}{1} = \frac{\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta} = \frac{1-\dfrac{\sin^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta}}{1+\dfrac{\sin^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta}} = \frac{1-\tan^2\theta}{1+\tan^2\theta}$$
