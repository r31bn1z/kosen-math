### (1)
$$4x^2-4x+1=0$$

$$(2x-1)^2=0 \quad x=\dfrac{1}{2}$$

### (2)
$$9x^2+6x+1=0$$

$$(3x+1)^2=0 \quad x=-\dfrac{1}{3}$$

### (3)
$$50x^2-20x+2=0$$

$$(5x-1)(10x-2)=0 \quad x=\dfrac{1}{5}$$

### (4)
$$12x^2+27-36x=0$$

$$3(4x^2-12x+9)=3(2x-3)^2=0 \quad x=\dfrac{3}{2}$$
