### (1)

$$x^2 - 5 = (x+\sqrt{5})(x-\sqrt{5})$$

### (2)

$$x^2+2x-5$$

$$x = -1\pm\sqrt{1+5} = -1\pm\sqrt{6}\quad\text{これから}$$

$$(x+1+\sqrt{6})(x+1-\sqrt{6})$$

### (3)

$$a^2+a-1$$

$$a = \dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{1+4}}{2} = \dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{5}}{2}\quad\text{これから}$$

$$\left(x+\dfrac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)\left(x+\dfrac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)$$

### (4)

$$x^2+2x-4$$

$$x = -1\pm\sqrt{1+4} = -1\pm\sqrt{5}\quad\text{これから}$$

$$(x+1+\sqrt{5})(x+1-\sqrt{5})$$

### (5)

$$4x^2+4x-1$$

$$x = \dfrac{-2\pm\sqrt{4+4}}{4} = \dfrac{-2\pm2\sqrt{2}}{4} = \dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{2}}{2}\quad\text{これから}$$

$$\left(x+\dfrac{1+\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)\left(x+\dfrac{1-\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$$

### (6)
$$a^2 + 2ab - 5b^2$$

$$a = -b \pm \sqrt{b^2+5b^2} = -b \pm \sqrt{6}b$$ これから

$$(a+b+\sqrt{6}b)(a+b-\sqrt{6}b)$$
