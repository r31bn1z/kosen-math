### (1)
$$4i - (3 - 2i) = -3 + 6i$$

### (2)
$$1 + 5i - 2(-4 - i) = 9 + 7i$$

### (3)
$$2(5 - 3i) - 3(3 - 2i) = 10 - 6i - 9 + 6i = 1$$

### (4)
$$(4 - 3i)(2 + i) = 8 - 2i + 3 = 11 - 2i$$

### (5)
$$(1 + i)^2 = 1 + 2i - 1 = 2i$$

### (6)
$$(\sqrt{2} - i)(3 + \sqrt{2}i) = 3\sqrt{2} - i + \sqrt{2} = 4\sqrt{2} - i$$

### (7)
$$\frac{2}{i} = -2i$$

### (8)
$$\frac{6 + 4i}{5 - i} = \frac{(6 + 4i)(5 + i)}{(5 - i)(5 + i)} = \frac{30 + 26i - 4}{25 + 1} = \frac{26 + 26i}{26} = 1 + i$$
