### (1)
$$x^4 - 9x^2 + 20 = (x^2-5)(x^2-4) = (x-\sqrt{5})(x+\sqrt{5})(x-2)(x+2)$$

### (2)
$$x^4 - x^2 - 6 = (x^2-3)(x^2+2) = (x-\sqrt{3})(x+\sqrt{3})(x^2+2)$$

### (3)
$$x^4 + 3x^2 + 9 = (x^2+3)^2 - 3x^2 = (x^2-\sqrt{3}x+3)(x^2+\sqrt{3}x+3)$$

### (4)
$$x^4 + 4y^4 + 2x^2y^2 = (x^2+2y^2)^2 - 2x^2y^2 = (x^2-\sqrt{2}xy+2y^2)(x^2+\sqrt{2}xy+2y^2)$$
