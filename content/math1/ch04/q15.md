### (1)
$$(x-3)(x-3) = x^2 - 6x + 9$$

### (2)
$$\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(x+\frac{1}{3}\right) = x^2 - \frac{1}{6}x - \frac{1}{6}$$

### (3)
$$(x-3+\sqrt{2})(x-3-\sqrt{2}) = x^2 - (3-\sqrt{2}+3+\sqrt{2})x + (3+\sqrt{2})(3-\sqrt{2}) = x^2 - 6x + (9-2) = x^2 - 6x + 7$$

### (4)
$$(x-4+3\sqrt{3}i)(x-4-3\sqrt{3}i) = x^2 - (4-3\sqrt{3}i+4+3\sqrt{3}i)x + (4-3\sqrt{3}i)(4+3\sqrt{3}i) = x^2 - 8x + (16+27)$$
$$= x^2 - 8x + 43$$

### (5)
$$\left(x-\frac{-5-2\sqrt{5}i}{3}\right)\left(x-\frac{-5+2\sqrt{5}i}{3}\right) = x^2 - \left(\frac{-5-2\sqrt{5}i}{3}+\frac{-5+2\sqrt{5}i}{3}\right)x + \left(\frac{-5-2\sqrt{5}i}{3}\right)\left(\frac{-5+2\sqrt{5}i}{3}\right)$$
$$= x^2 + \frac{10}{3}x + 5$$
