### (1)
$$(1-2i)^2 = 1-4i-4 = -3-4i$$

### (2)
$$(2-\sqrt5 i)^2 - 4(2-\sqrt5 i) = (2-\sqrt5 i)(2-\sqrt5 i-4) = -(2-\sqrt5 i)(2+\sqrt5 i) = -(4+5) = -9$$

### (3)
$$(-\sqrt{-12})^3 = -(-1)\cdot 12\sqrt{12}i = 12\sqrt{12}i = 24\sqrt3 i$$

### (4)
$$\left(\frac{1+i}{1-i}\right)^3 = \left(\frac{(1+i)^2}{(1-i)(1+i)}\right)^3 = \left(\frac{1+2i-1}{1+1}\right)^3 = i^3 = -i$$

### (5)
$$\frac{1+2i}{4+3i} = \frac{(1+2i)(4-3i)}{(4+3i)(4-3i)} = \frac{4+5i+6}{16+9} = \frac{10+5i}{25} = \frac{2+i}{5}$$
