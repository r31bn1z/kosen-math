### (1)
$$x^2 + x + 3 = 0$$

$$x = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1-12}}{2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{11}i}{2}$$

### (2)
$$x^2 - 2x + 2 = 0$$

$$x = 1 \pm \sqrt{1-2} = 1 \pm i$$

### (3)
$$x^2 - 4x + 6 = 0$$

$$x = 2 \pm \sqrt{4-6} = 2 \pm \sqrt{2}i$$

### (4)
$$x - 2 = 2x^2 + 2 \qquad 2x^2 - x + 4 = 0$$

$$x = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{1-32}}{4} = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{31}i}{4}$$

### (5)
$$\frac{x^2+1}{2} = x - 1 \qquad x^2 + 1 = 2x - 2 \qquad x^2 - 2x + 3 = 0$$

$$x = 1 \pm \sqrt{1-3} = 1 \pm \sqrt{2}i$$

### (6)
$$\frac{x^2}{3} - \frac{x}{5} + \frac{13}{30} = 0 \qquad 10x^2 - 6x + 13 = 0$$

$$x = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{9-130}}{10} = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{121}i}{10} = \frac{3 \pm 11i}{10}$$
