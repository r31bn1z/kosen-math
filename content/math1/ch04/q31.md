$$3x^2+5x+m=0$$

$$x=\frac{-5\pm\sqrt{25-12m}}{6}$$

### (1)
$$\frac{-5-\sqrt{25-12m}}{6} : \frac{-5+\sqrt{25-12m}}{6} = 3:2$$

$$18\left(-5+\sqrt{25-12m}\right)=12(-5-\sqrt{25-12m})$$

$$-15+3\sqrt{25-12m}=-10-2\sqrt{25-12m}$$

$$\sqrt{25-12m}=1 \qquad 12m=24 \qquad m=2$$

このとき $x=-1,-\dfrac{2}{3}$ となる

### (2)
$$\frac{-5+\sqrt{25-12m}}{6}-\frac{-5-\sqrt{25-12m}}{6}=3$$

$$-5+\sqrt{25-12m}+5+\sqrt{25-12m}=18$$

$$\sqrt{25-12m}=9$$

$$25-12m=81 \qquad 12m=-56 \qquad m=-\frac{14}{3}$$

このとき $x=-\dfrac{7}{3},\dfrac{2}{3}$

### (3)
$$\frac{-5-\sqrt{25-12m}}{6}=2\cdot\frac{-5+\sqrt{25-12m}}{6}$$

$$-5-\sqrt{25-12m}=2(-5+\sqrt{25-12m})$$

$$3\sqrt{25-12m}=5 \qquad 9(25-12m)=25 \qquad 108m=200 \qquad m=\frac{50}{27}$$

このとき $x=-\dfrac{5}{9},-\dfrac{10}{9}$
