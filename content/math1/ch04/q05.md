### (1)
$$\sqrt{-16} + \sqrt{-4} = 4i + 2i = 6i$$

### (2)
$$\sqrt{-49} - \sqrt{-25} = 7i - 5i = 2i$$

### (3)
$$\sqrt{-9} \times \sqrt{-3} = 3i \times \sqrt{3}i = -3\sqrt{3}$$

### (4)
$$\sqrt{-3} \times \sqrt{-12} = \sqrt{3}i \times 2\sqrt{3}i = -6$$

### (5)
$$\frac{\sqrt{-32}}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{16}i = 4i$$

### (6)
$$\frac{\sqrt{12}}{\sqrt{-4}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{i} = -\sqrt{3}i$$
