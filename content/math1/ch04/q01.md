### (1)
$x^2 + 3x - 4 = 0$  $(x+4)(x-1) = 0$  $x = -4, 1$

$$x = \dfrac{-3 \pm \sqrt{9+16}}{2} = \dfrac{-3 \pm 5}{2} = -4, 1$$

### (2)
$x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0$  $(x-2)^2 = 0$  $x = 2$

$$x = 2 \pm \sqrt{4-4} = 2$$

### (3)
$x^2 + x - 30 = 0$  $(x+6)(x-5) = 0$  $x = -6, 5$

$$x = \dfrac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1+120}}{2} = \dfrac{-1 \pm 11}{2} = -6, 5$$

### (4)
$2x^2 - 4x = 0$  $2x(x-2) = 0$  $x = 0, 2$

$$x = \dfrac{2 \pm \sqrt{4}}{2} = \dfrac{2 \pm 2}{2} = 0, 2$$

### (5)
$3x^2 + 4x + 1 = 0$  $(3x+1)(x+1) = 0$  $x = -\dfrac{1}{3}, -1$

$$x = \dfrac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4-3}}{3} = \dfrac{-2 \pm 1}{3} = -\dfrac{1}{3}, -1$$

### (6)
$2(x-1)^2 = 5(x-1) + 3$    $2(x-1)^2 - 5(x-1) - 3 = 0$

$\{2(x-1)+1\}\{(x-1)-3\} = 0$    $(2x-1)(x-4) = 0$  $x = \dfrac{1}{2}, 4$

$$x - 1 = \dfrac{5 \pm \sqrt{25+24}}{4} = \dfrac{5 \pm 7}{4} = -\dfrac{1}{2}, 3$$    $x = \dfrac{1}{2}, 4$
