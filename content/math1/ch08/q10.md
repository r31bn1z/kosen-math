### (1)
$$y = \frac{2}{x}$$

漸近線は$x = 0, y = 0$

### (2)
$$y = \frac{2x}{x-1} = 2 + \frac{2}{x-1} \qquad (y-2) = \frac{2}{x-1}$$

漸近線は$x = 1$、$y = 2$

### (3)
$$y = -\frac{3x+4}{x+2} = -\left(3 - \frac{2}{x+2}\right) \qquad (y+3) = \frac{2}{x+2}$$

漸近線は$x = -2$、$y = -3$
