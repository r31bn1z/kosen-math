### (1)
$$16^{\frac{1}{2}} = (4^2)^{\frac{1}{2}} = 4$$

### (2)
$$\left(\frac{9}{25}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = \left(\frac{3^2}{5^2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{3}{5}$$

### (3)
$$6^{-2} = \frac{1}{36}$$

### (4)
$$\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^{2} = \frac{16}{25}$$

### (5)
$$8^0 = 1$$

### (6)
$$2.4 \times 10^3 = 2.4 \times 1000 = 2400$$

### (7)
$$25^{-\frac{1}{2}} = (5^2)^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{1}{5}$$

### (8)
$$625^{\frac{3}{4}} = (5^4)^{\frac{3}{4}} = 5^3 = 125$$

### (9)
$$16^{-\frac{3}{4}} = (2^4)^{-\frac{3}{4}} = 2^{-3} = \frac{1}{8}$$

### (10)
$$12^{\frac{1}{2}} \cdot 3^{\frac{3}{2}} = (3^4 \cdot 2^2)^{\frac{1}{2}} = 3^2 \cdot 2 = 18$$

### (11)
$$\frac{\sqrt[3]{24}}{\sqrt[3]{3}} = \sqrt[3]{8} = \sqrt[3]{2^3} = 2$$

### (12)
$$\sqrt[5]{-32} = \sqrt[5]{(-2)^5} = -2$$
