### (1)
$$(-4)^0 = 1$$

### (2)
$$(-2)^{-3} = \frac{1}{(-2)^3} = -\frac{1}{8}$$

### (3)
$$\frac{3^2 \cdot 3^{-2}}{5^2 - 5^0} = \frac{1}{25 - 1} = \frac{1}{24}$$

### (4)
$$(2 + 2^{-1})^{-1} = \left(2 + \frac{1}{2}\right)^{-1} = \left(\frac{5}{2}\right)^{-1} = \frac{2}{5}$$

### (5)
$$(3 + 3^{-1})^{-2} = \left(3 + \frac{1}{3}\right)^{-2} = \left(\frac{10}{3}\right)^{-2} = \frac{9}{100}$$
