### (1)
$$2^{\frac{1}{3}} = \sqrt[3]{2}$$

### (2)
$$15^{\frac{2}{5}} = \sqrt[5]{15^2}$$

### (3)
$$(4x)^{\frac{4}{5}} = \sqrt[5]{(4x)^4}$$

### (4)
$$10^{0.5} = 10^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{10}$$

### (5)
$$10^{0.3} = 10^{\frac{3}{10}} = \sqrt[10]{10^3}$$

### (6)
$$5^{1.2} = 5^{\frac{6}{5}} = \sqrt[5]{5^6}$$

### (7)
$$(2x^2)^{2.3} = (2x^2)^{\frac{23}{10}} = \sqrt[10]{(2x^2)^{23}}$$

### (8)
$$(x^{-1}y)^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \left(\frac{x}{y}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{x}{y}}$$

### (9)
$$(2x)^{-0.2} = (2x)^{-\frac{1}{5}} = \sqrt[5]{\frac{1}{2x}}$$

### (10)
$$(16x)^{0.75} = (16x)^{\frac{3}{4}} = \sqrt[4]{(16x)^3} = \sqrt[4]{(2^3)^4 x^3} = 2^3\sqrt[4]{x^3} = 8\sqrt[4]{x^3}$$
