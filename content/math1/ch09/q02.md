### (1)
$$\sqrt{2} = 2^{\frac{1}{2}}$$

### (2)
$$\sqrt[4]{3} = 3^{\frac{1}{4}}$$

### (3)
$$\sqrt[4]{x^3} = x^{\frac{3}{4}}$$

### (4)
$$\sqrt{2x} = (2x)^{\frac{1}{2}}$$

### (5)
$$\sqrt[3]{(3x)^2} = (3x)^{\frac{2}{3}}$$

### (6)
$$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt[5]{x^2}} = 2^{\frac{1}{2}}x^{-\frac{2}{5}}$$

### (7)
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{5^3}} = 5^{-\frac{3}{4}}$$

### (8)
$$\frac{\sqrt{x^3}}{\sqrt[3]{4x^2}} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}(4x^2)^{-\frac{1}{3}} = 4^{-\frac{1}{3}}x^{\frac{5}{6}}$$
