### (1)
$$(a^{-1}+b^{-1})^{-1} = \frac{1}{a^{-1}+b^{-1}} = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}} = \frac{ab}{a+b}$$

### (2)
$$(2^{-2}-4^{-1})^2 = 2^{-4} - 2\cdot 2^{-2}\cdot 4^{-1} + 4^{-2} = \frac{1}{16} - \frac{2}{16} + \frac{1}{16} = 0$$

### (3)
$$(5t^{-6})^3 \cdot (4t^{-7}) = 125t^{-18}\cdot 4t^{-7} = \frac{500}{t^{25}}$$

### (4)
$$\frac{2x^{-1}}{x^{-1}+y^{-1}} = 2x^{-1}\cdot\frac{xy}{x+y} = \frac{2y}{x+y}$$

### (5)
$$\frac{x^{-2}-y^{-2}}{x^{-1}-y^{-1}} = \frac{\dfrac{y^2-x^2}{x^2y^2}}{\dfrac{y-x}{xy}} = \frac{(y-x)(y+x)}{(y-x)xy} = \frac{y+x}{xy}$$

### (6)
$$\left\{\left(a^{\frac{1}{2}}-a^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)^2+4\right\}^{\frac{1}{2}} = (a-2+a^{-1}+4)^{\frac{1}{2}} = \left\{\left(a^{\frac{1}{2}}+a^{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)^2\right\}^{\frac{1}{2}} = a^{\frac{1}{2}}+a^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{a}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}}$$
