### (1)
$$x+y=\dfrac{2}{3+\sqrt{5}}+\dfrac{2}{3-\sqrt{5}}=\dfrac{2\left(3-\sqrt{5}\right)+2(3+\sqrt{5})}{\left(3+\sqrt{5}\right)(3-\sqrt{5})}=\dfrac{6-2\sqrt{5}+6+2\sqrt{5}}{9-5}=3$$

### (2)
$$xy=\dfrac{2}{3+\sqrt{5}}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3-\sqrt{5}}=\dfrac{4}{9-5}=1$$

### (3)
$$x^2+y^2=(x+y)^2-2xy=3^2-2=7$$

### (4)
$$x^3+y^3=(x+y)(x^2-xy+y^2)=3(7-1)=18$$
