### (1)
$$\sqrt{54} = \sqrt{6 \cdot 3^2} = 3\sqrt{6}$$

### (2)
$$\sqrt{63} = \sqrt{7 \cdot 3^2} = 3\sqrt{7}$$

### (3)
$$\sqrt{\frac{12}{25}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 \cdot 2^2}{5^2}} = \frac{2}{5}\sqrt{3}$$

### (4)
$$\sqrt{8} + \sqrt{18} = \sqrt{2^3} + \sqrt{2 \cdot 3^2} = 2\sqrt{2} + 3\sqrt{2}$$

### (5)
$$\sqrt{50} - \sqrt{32} - \sqrt{18} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 5^2} - \sqrt{2 \cdot 4^2} - \sqrt{2 \cdot 3^2} = 5\sqrt{2} - 4\sqrt{2} - 3\sqrt{2} = -2\sqrt{2}$$
