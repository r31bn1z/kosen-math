$$x = \frac{2a}{1+a^2} \quad (-1 \le a \le 1)$$

$$\frac{\sqrt{1+x}-\sqrt{1-x}}{\sqrt{1+x}+\sqrt{1-x}} = \frac{\sqrt{1+\dfrac{2a}{1+a^2}}-\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2a}{1+a^2}}}{\sqrt{1+\dfrac{2a}{1+a^2}}+\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2a}{1+a^2}}} = \frac{\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a^2+2a}{1+a^2}}-\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a^2-2a}{1+a^2}}}{\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a^2+2a}{1+a^2}}+\sqrt{\dfrac{1+a^2-2a}{1+a^2}}} = \frac{\sqrt{(a+1)^2}-\sqrt{(a-1)^2}}{\sqrt{(a+1)^2}+\sqrt{(a-1)^2}}$$

$$= \frac{a+1-1+a}{a+1+1-a} = a$$
