$$x = a + \frac{1}{a} \qquad x^2 = \left(a+\frac{1}{a}\right)^2 = a^2+2+\frac{1}{a^2}$$

$$x+\sqrt{x^2-4} = a+\frac{1}{a}+\sqrt{a^2+2+\frac{1}{a^2}-4} = a+\frac{1}{a}+\sqrt{a^2-2+\frac{1}{a^2}} = a+\frac{1}{a}+\sqrt{\left(a-\frac{1}{a}\right)^2} = \left(a+\frac{1}{a}\right)+\left|a-\frac{1}{a}\right|$$

$a \geq 1$ より  $a+\frac{1}{a}+a-\frac{1}{a} = 2a$
