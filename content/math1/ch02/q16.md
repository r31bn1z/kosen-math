### (1)
$$\frac{1}{1+\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1+\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{3}}{\left(1+\sqrt{2}\right)^2-3} = \frac{1+\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{3}}{1+2\sqrt{2}+2-3} = \frac{1+\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{3}}{2\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}+2-\sqrt{6}}{4} = \frac{2+\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{6}}{4}$$

### (2)
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{5}}{\left(\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}\right)^2-5} = \frac{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{5}}{2+2\sqrt{6}+3-5} = \frac{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{5}}{2\sqrt{6}} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}+3\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{30}}{12}$$
