### (1)
$|x-3|=2$

$$(x-3)^2=4 \qquad x^2-6x+9=4 \qquad x^2-6x+5=0 \qquad (x-5)(x-1)=0 \qquad x=1,5$$

### (2)
$|1-x|=5$

$$(1-x)^2=25 \qquad 1-2x+x^2=25 \qquad x^2-2x-24=0 \qquad (x-6)(x+4)=0 \qquad x=6,-4$$

### (3)
$|2x-3|=7$

$$(2x-3)^2=49 \qquad 4x^2-12x+9=49 \qquad 4x^2-12x-40=0 \qquad 4(x^2-3x-10)=4(x-5)(x+2)=0$$

$$x=5,-2$$
