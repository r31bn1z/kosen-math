### (1)

$$(\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2})^2 = 3 - 2\sqrt{6} + 2 = 5 - 2\sqrt{6}$$

### (2)

$$(2\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{5})^2 = 8 + 4\sqrt{10} + 5 = 13 + 4\sqrt{10}$$

### (3)

$$(\sqrt{7}+\sqrt{2})(\sqrt{7}-\sqrt{2}) = 7 - 2 = 5$$

### (4)

$$(\sqrt{15}+\sqrt{3})(\sqrt{60}-\sqrt{12}) = (\sqrt{15}+\sqrt{3})(2\sqrt{15}-2\sqrt{3}) = 30 - 6 = 24$$
