$$S = \frac{1}{2}ab \cdot \sin C$$

$$b = \frac{\sin B}{\sin A}a$$

$$\sin A = \sin\bigl(180 - (B+C)\bigr) = \sin 180 \cdot \cos(B+C) - \cos 180 \cdot \sin(B+C) = \sin(B+C)$$

これから

$$b = \frac{\sin B}{\sin(B+C)}a$$

$$S = \frac{1}{2}a \cdot \frac{\sin B}{\sin(B+C)}a \cdot \sin C = \frac{a^2 \sin B \cdot \sin C}{2\sin(B+C)}$$
