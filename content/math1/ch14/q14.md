### (1)
$\angle DAP = x$ とおくと

四角形AEPDの角は$360°$であるので$\angle ADP = \angle AEP = 90°$から

$$\angle DPE = 180° - A$$

$$PD = AP\sin x \qquad PE = AP\sin(A - x)$$

また$DE$に関する余弦定理より

$$DE^2 = PD^2 + PE^2 - 2PD \cdot PE \cdot \cos(180° - A)$$

$$= (AP\sin x)^2 + (AP\sin(A - x))^2 - 2(AP\sin x \cdot AP\sin(A - x)) \cdot \cos(180° - A)$$

$$= AP^2\sin^2 x + AP^2\sin^2(A - x) + 2AP^2\sin x \cdot \sin(A - x) \cdot \cos A$$

$$= AP^2\sin^2 x + AP^2\sin^2(A - x) + 2AP^2\sin x \cdot \cos A(\sin A \cdot \cos x - \cos A \cdot \sin x)$$

$$= AP^2\sin^2 x + AP^2\sin^2(A - x) + 2AP^2\sin x \cdot \sin A \cdot \cos A \cdot \cos x - 2AP^2\sin^2 x \cdot \cos^2 A$$

$$= AP^2\sin^2 x + AP^2(\sin A \cdot \cos x - \cos A \cdot \sin x)^2 + 2AP^2\sin x \cdot \sin A \cdot \cos A \cdot \cos x - 2AP^2\sin^2 x \cdot \cos^2 A$$

$$= AP^2\sin^2 x + AP^2\sin^2 A \cdot \cos^2 x - AP^2\cos^2 A \cdot \sin^2 x$$

$$= AP^2(\sin^2 x + \sin^2 A \cdot (1 - \sin^2 x) - (1 - \sin^2 A)\sin^2 x)$$

$$= AP^2\sin^2 A$$

これから

$$DE = AP\sin A$$

### (2)
$AP\sin A$が最小となるには

$AP$が最小となればよい

これは$AP$が$BC$の垂線であれば最小となるので

$AP \perp BC$
