### (1)

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{bc}\sin A = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 3 \cdot 4 \cdot \sin 60° = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 12 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 3\sqrt{3}$$

### (2)

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{ac}\sin 60° = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 4 \cdot 5 \cdot \sin 60° = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 20 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 5\sqrt{3}$$

### (3)

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{ab}\sin C = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 3\sqrt{2} \cdot 4 \cdot \sin 45° = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 12\sqrt{2} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = 6$$

### (4)

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{ab}\sin C = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot \sin(180-30)° = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 1$$
