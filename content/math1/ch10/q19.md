$8^x = 9^y = 6^z$ 全辺対数を取ると $x\log 8 = y\log 9 = z\log 6$

$$x = \dfrac{y\log 9}{\log 8} \qquad z = \dfrac{y\log 9}{\log 6}$$

$$\dfrac{2}{x} + \dfrac{3}{y} = \dfrac{6}{z}$$

$$\dfrac{2\log 8}{y\log 9} + \dfrac{3}{y} = \dfrac{6\log 6}{y\log 9}$$

$$\dfrac{6\log 2}{\log 9} + 3 = \dfrac{6\log 6}{\log 9}$$

$$6\log 2 + 3\log 9 = 6\log 6$$

$$6\log 2 + 6\log 3 = 6\log 2 + 6\log 3$$

よって成り立つ
