### (1)
$$3^3 = 27 \qquad \log_3 3^3 = \log_3 27 \qquad 3 = \log_3 27$$

### (2)
$$5^2 = 25 \qquad \log_5 5^2 = \log_5 25 \qquad 2 = \log_5 25$$

### (3)
$$3^0 = 1 \qquad \log_3 3^0 = \log_3 1 \qquad 0 = \log_3 1$$

### (4)
$$4^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{16} \qquad \log_4 4^{-2} = \log_4 \dfrac{1}{16} \qquad -2 = \log_4 \dfrac{1}{16}$$

### (5)
$$2^{-6} = \dfrac{1}{64} \qquad \log_2 2^{-6} = \log_2 \dfrac{1}{64} \qquad -6 = \log_2 \dfrac{1}{64}$$

### (6)
$$8^{\frac{1}{3}} = 2 \qquad \log_8 8^{\frac{1}{3}} = \log_8 2 \qquad \dfrac{1}{3} = \log_8 2$$

### (7)
$$\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{16} \qquad \log_{\frac{1}{4}} \left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \log_{\frac{1}{4}} \dfrac{1}{16} \qquad 2 = \log_{\frac{1}{4}} \dfrac{1}{16}$$

### (8)
$$\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-2} = 4 \qquad \log_{\frac{1}{2}} \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-2} = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 4 \qquad -2 = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 4$$

### (9)
$$81^{\frac{3}{4}} = 27 \qquad \log_{81} 81^{\frac{3}{4}} = \log_{81} 27 \qquad \dfrac{3}{4} = \log_{81} 27$$

### (10)
$$7^1 = 7 \qquad \log_7 7^1 = \log_7 7 \qquad 1 = \log_7 7$$
