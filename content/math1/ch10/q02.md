### (2)
$$\log_4 16 = \log_4 4^2 = 2$$

### (3)
$$\log_{16} 4 = \log_{16} 16^{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

### (4)

### (5)

### (6)

### (7)

### (8)
