グラフは問題集１ p.131

### (1)
$$y = \log_2(x+2) \qquad x \text{軸に} -2 \text{平行移動}$$

### (2)
$$y = \log_2(-x) \qquad y \text{軸対称}$$

### (3)
$$y = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} x = \dfrac{\log_2 x}{\log_2 \frac{1}{2}} = -\log_2 x \qquad x \text{軸対称}$$

### (4)
$$y = \log_2 4x = \log_2 4 + \log_2 x = 2 + \log_2 x \qquad y \text{軸に} 2 \text{平行移動}$$

### (5)
$$y = \log_2 \dfrac{1}{x} = -\log_2 x \qquad x \text{軸対称}$$
