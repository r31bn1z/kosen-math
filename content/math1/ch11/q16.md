### (1)

$$\tan\theta = \dfrac{94}{188 \div 2} = \dfrac{94}{94} = 1 \qquad \theta = 45°$$

### (2)

底辺中心から底辺の角までの距離$94\sqrt{2}$はなので

$$\tan\theta = \dfrac{94}{94\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \qquad \theta \cong 35.26°$$

### (3)

側面の二等辺三角形の高さは$94\sqrt{2}$なので

$$\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2} = \dfrac{94}{94\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \qquad \dfrac{\theta}{2} \cong 35.26 \qquad \theta = 70.5°$$
