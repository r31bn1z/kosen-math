動径の位置は問題集１p.133を参照

### (1)
$$\frac{3}{4}\pi + 2n\pi = \frac{3}{4}\pi$$

### (2)
$$2n\pi \pm \frac{\pi}{3} = \pm \frac{\pi}{3}$$

### (3)
$$(2n+1)\pi = \pi + 2n\pi = \pi$$

### (4)
$$\frac{2}{3}\pi + n\pi = \frac{2}{3}\pi, \frac{5}{3}\pi$$

### (5)
$$n\pi + (-1)^n \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{5}{6}\pi, \frac{\pi}{6}$$
