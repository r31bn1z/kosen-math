### (1)

$$\sin\alpha = \frac{5}{13}$$

$$\cos\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{13^2-5^2}}{13} = \frac{12}{13}$$

$$\tan\alpha = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{5}{12}$$

### (2)

$$\tan\alpha = \frac{3}{4}$$

$$\sin\alpha = \frac{3}{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}} = \frac{3}{5}$$

$$\cos\alpha = \frac{4}{5}$$
