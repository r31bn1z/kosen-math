### (1)
$$60° = 60° \cdot \dfrac{2\pi}{360°} = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$$

### (2)
$$135° = 135° \cdot \dfrac{2\pi}{360°} = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$$

### (3)
$$270° = 270° \cdot \dfrac{2\pi}{360°} = \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$$

### (4)
$$480° = 480° \cdot \dfrac{2\pi}{360°} = \dfrac{8\pi}{3}$$
