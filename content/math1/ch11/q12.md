$$\tan\theta = 2 \qquad \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} = \frac{2}{1} = \frac{\frac{2}{r}}{\frac{1}{r}}$$

$$r = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}$$

これから

$$\cos\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} \qquad \sin\theta = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$$

$$\cos^2\theta = \frac{1}{5} \qquad \sin\theta\cos\theta = \frac{2}{5}$$
