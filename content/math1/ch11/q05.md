### (1)
$$\frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi}{4} \cdot \frac{360°}{2\pi} = 45°$$

### (2)
$$\frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{6} \cdot \frac{360°}{2\pi} = 30°$$

### (3)
$$\frac{5\pi}{3} = \frac{5\pi}{3} \cdot \frac{360°}{2\pi} = 300°$$

### (4)
$$1.2\pi = 1.2\pi \cdot \frac{360°}{2\pi} = 216°$$
