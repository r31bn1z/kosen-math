### (1)
$$\sin\frac{1}{6}\pi = \frac{1}{2}$$

### (2)
$$\sin\frac{7}{4}\pi = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$

### (3)
$$\sin\left(-\frac{2}{3}\pi\right) = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

### (4)
$$\cos\frac{1}{2}\pi = 0$$

### (5)
$$\cos\frac{7}{6}\pi = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

### (6)
$$\cos\left(-\frac{1}{4}\pi\right) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$

### (7)
$$\tan\frac{1}{4}\pi = 1$$

### (8)
$$\tan\frac{2}{3}\pi = -\sqrt{3}$$

### (9)
$$\tan\left(-\frac{1}{6}\pi\right) = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$$
