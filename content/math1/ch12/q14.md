### (1)

$$\frac{1+\tan\theta}{1-\tan\theta} = 2$$

$$\frac{1+\tan\theta}{1-\tan\theta} = \frac{1+\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}{1-\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}} = \frac{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}{\cos\theta-\sin\theta} = 2$$

$$\cos\theta = -3\sin\theta = -3\sqrt{1-\cos^2\theta}$$

$$\cos^2\theta = 9(1-\cos^2\theta)$$

$$10\cos^2\theta = 9 \qquad \cos^2\theta = \frac{9}{10} \qquad \cos\theta = \pm\frac{3}{\sqrt{10}}$$

### (2)

$$\sin\theta+\cos\theta = \frac{1}{2} \qquad (\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2 = \frac{1}{4} \qquad \sin^2\theta+2\sin\theta\cos\theta+\cos^2\theta = \frac{1}{4} \qquad \sin\theta\cos\theta = -\frac{3}{8}$$

これから

$$\tan\theta+\frac{1}{\tan\theta} = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}+\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta} = \frac{\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta}{\sin\theta\cos\theta} = \frac{1}{-\frac{3}{8}} = -\frac{8}{3}$$
