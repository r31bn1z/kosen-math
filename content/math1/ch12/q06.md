### (1)
$$(\sin\theta + \cos\theta)^2 = \sin^2\theta + 2\sin\theta\cos\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1 + 2\sin\theta\cos\theta = (\sqrt{2})^2$$

$$\sin\theta\cos\theta = \frac{1}{2}$$

### (2)
$$\sin^4\theta + \cos^4\theta = (\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta)^2 - 2\sin^2\theta\cos^2\theta = 1 - 2(\sin\theta\cos\theta)^2 = 1 - 2\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{2}$$
