### (1)
$$\left(1+\frac{1}{\cos\theta}\right)(1-\cos\theta)=\left(\frac{\cos\theta+1}{\cos\theta}\right)(1-\cos\theta)=\frac{1-\cos^2\theta}{\cos\theta}=\frac{\sin^2\theta}{\cos\theta}=\sin\theta\tan\theta$$

### (2)
$$\frac{\cos\theta-\sin\theta}{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}=\frac{1-\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}{1+\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}=\frac{1-\tan\theta}{1+\tan\theta}$$

### (3)
$$\frac{\tan\theta}{\sin\theta}-\frac{\sin\theta}{\tan\theta}=\frac{1}{\cos\theta}-\cos\theta=\frac{1-\cos^2\theta}{\cos\theta}=\frac{\sin^2\theta}{\cos\theta}=\sin\theta\tan\theta$$
