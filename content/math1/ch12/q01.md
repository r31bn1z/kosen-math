### (1)

$$\frac{\sin\theta+\tan\theta}{1+\cos\theta}=\frac{\sin\theta+\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}{1+\cos\theta}=\frac{\sin\theta\cos\theta+\sin\theta}{\cos\theta(1+\cos\theta)}=\frac{\sin\theta(1+\cos\theta)}{\cos\theta(1+\cos\theta)}=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$$

### (2)

$$\frac{\tan^2\theta+1}{\tan\theta}=\frac{\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}=\frac{1}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}$$
